# Summary

[DEME Offshore](/companies/deme-offshore.md) has been awarded a contract by the
**Zeevonk joint venture** — comprising [Vattenfall](/companies/vattenfall.md)
and [Copenhagen Infrastructure Partners (CIP)](/companies/copenhagen-infrastructure-partners.md)
— for the **foundation installation of the first phase** of the
[Zeevonk offshore wind farm](/projects/zeevonk.md) in the Dutch North Sea
(IJmuiden Ver zone).

The contract award is a durable milestone, so a Zeevonk project page was created
and the DEME Offshore key-projects list was updated. The available excerpt is
brief and does not disclose the project's capacity, turbine or foundation count,
contract value, or installation schedule.
