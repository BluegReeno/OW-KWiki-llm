# Summary

Belgium-headquartered survey and geo-data specialist GEOxyz is bringing its
offshore survey capabilities to South Korea through the establishment of a
strategic local partnership. The move extends the company's footprint into the
fast-growing South Korean offshore wind market, where a wave of projects — from
[Shinan Ui](/projects/shinan-ui.md) to the [Jindo cluster](/projects/jindo.md) —
is driving demand for site-investigation and survey services.

The offshoreWIND.biz excerpt is truncated and names neither the local partner nor
the partnership's structure, scope, timeline or target projects. A market-entry
via an unnamed partnership is not on its own a durable fact warranting a company
page, so none was created for GEOxyz or its partner.
