# Summary

The Romanian Ministry of Energy published, for public consultation, a list of
**six offshore wind development areas** in the Romanian sector of the Black Sea,
together with the government decision approving it. The documentation was published
on 22 July 2026 and reported on 28 July.

The six perimeters are estimated to accommodate **11.5 GW** of installed capacity
and are grouped into three phases:

- **Phase 1 — 3.1 GW**: three areas (one ~1.9 GW site and two 600 MW sites), the
  first to be put out to tender; delivering them meets the target of Romania's
  Energy Strategy 2025-2035.
- **Phase 2 — 2.6 GW**: two areas, of 1.8 GW and 800 MW.
- **Phase 3 — 6 GW**: a single area described as suitable for a mix of fixed-bottom
  and floating turbines.

Approving the areas operationalises **Law No. 121/2024** on offshore wind energy and
conditions the launch of competitive concession procedures. The delimitation study,
initiated in 2025, weighed wind potential, geotechnics, environmental impact and
biodiversity, grid connection options, and compatibility with navigation, fishing,
extraction and military uses of the maritime space. The Ministry noted the sites
finally tendered remain subject to concession, authorisation and environmental
assessment procedures.

# Why It Matters

This is the gating step for Romania's entry into offshore wind: a two-year-old
enabling law had no perimeters to apply to. The phase-1 areas effectively define
the scope of the country's first offshore wind tender round — hence the `tenders`
tag — while the 6 GW phase-3 perimeter is the first official signal that
[floating wind](/technology/floating-wind.md) is expected in deeper Romanian
waters. Romania has so far appeared in this bundle only on the supply side, as a
[cable manufacturing](/companies/nkt.md) location, not as a generation market.

No tender timetable, concession duration or support mechanism accompanied the list,
and no project or company is named, so no project or tender page was created —
a `tenders/` page will follow once a concession procedure is actually launched.
