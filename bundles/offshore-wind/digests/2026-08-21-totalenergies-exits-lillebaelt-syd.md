# Danish Utility Seeks New Partner as TotalEnergies Exits Lillebælt Syd

[TotalEnergies](/companies/totalenergies.md) has decided to withdraw from
[Lillebælt Syd](/projects/lillebaelt-syd.md), the 165 MW Danish offshore wind
farm planned in the Little Belt off Sønderborg, where it had been the majority
partner since 2024. Danish utility [SONFOR](/companies/sonfor.md) is taking
over the majority stake in project company **Lillebælt Vind A/S** and will use
the coming months to assess both potential new partners and the project's
business case, a process expected to be completed by the **end of June 2027**.
European Energy, which joined in 2022, remains a minority shareholder.

The project — eleven 15 MW turbines, ~650 GWh a year, operation targeted for
2029/2030 — has completed its environmental investigations, holds an
establishment permit (November 2024) and has a grid connection agreement, so
what is in question is ownership and economics rather than consent. SONFOR
called the project still relevant for supply security and local development
around Sønderborg while acknowledging that offshore wind market conditions have
changed significantly. Per Danish media cited by offshoreWIND.biz,
TotalEnergies is also exiting **Jammerland Bugt**, another project with
European Energy as minority shareholder.

What stands out is that this is a permitted, grid-connected Danish project
losing its majority sponsor back to a municipal utility — the mirror image of
[Vattenfall winning Nordsøen Midt and Hesselø](/digests/2026-08-04-vattenfall-wins-danish-offshore-wind-tender.md)
in the state CfD round earlier this month, and part of the same
withdrawal pattern as
[Sumitomo exiting three Belgian projects](/digests/2026-07-06-sumitomo-exits-belgian-offshore-wind.md)
and
[RWE's US exit settlement](/digests/2026-08-07-rwe-us-offshore-wind-exit-settlement.md).

No `tenders` tag: a partner withdrawal and a search for a replacement investor
is not a call for bids, auction round or contract award. No European Energy or
Jammerland Bugt page: both are named without any capacity, stake size or scope
beyond the mention. Nothing beyond the article was asserted — no stake
