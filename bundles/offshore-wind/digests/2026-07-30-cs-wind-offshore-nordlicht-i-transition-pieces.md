# Summary

[CS Wind Offshore](/companies/cs-wind-offshore.md) has sent off the first
batch of transition pieces (TPs) for
[Vattenfall](/companies/vattenfall.md)'s 980 MW
[Nordlicht I](/projects/nordlicht.md) offshore wind farm in the German North
Sea. Nordlicht I requires 68 TPs, one for each monopile, and CS Wind Offshore
fabricates them in Aalborg, Denmark;
[DEME Offshore](/companies/deme-offshore.md) holds the transport and
installation contract for both monopiles and TPs at Nordlicht I and
Nordlicht II. The delivery follows DEME's installation of the first monopile
at the site on 1 July 2026, marking the point where the
[fixed-bottom foundation](/technology/fixed-bottom-foundations.md) supply
chain begins feeding the offshore campaign.

The excerpt available is truncated: it gives no batch size, no shipping
vessel or receiving marshalling port, no TP dimensions or weights, and no
date for the start of offshore TP installation, so none of that is asserted
here.
