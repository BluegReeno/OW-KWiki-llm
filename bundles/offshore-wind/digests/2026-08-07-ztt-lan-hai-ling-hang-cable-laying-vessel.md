# Summary

China's [ZTT Submarine Cable & System](/companies/ztt-submarine-cable-system.md)
has launched *Lan Hai Ling Hang*, a new cable-laying vessel (CLV) for offshore
wind. The excerpt is truncated after the opening clause, so nothing further is
asserted here: no building yard, vessel dimensions, cable carousel capacity,
laying or burial equipment, delivery or commissioning date, and no named
projects or charters for the vessel.

The launch continues a run of new cable-installation tonnage entering the market
from Asian cable makers and yards — [Taihan's second CLV](/digests/2026-07-17-taihan-second-cable-laying-vessel.md),
[LS Cable's newbuild at Tersan](/digests/2026-07-06-ls-cable-clv-tersan-shipyard.md)
and [ZPMC's CLV for Far East Cable](/digests/2026-07-23-zpmc-clv-far-east-cable.md) —
against European cable-laying capacity such as [NKT Eleonora](/digests/2026-07-21-nkt-eleonora-romania-to-norway.md).
Vessel supply is one of the binding constraints on
[grid connection and array cable installation](/technology/hvdc-grid-connection.md).
