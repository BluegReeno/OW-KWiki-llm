# Glamox Wins Marine Lighting Contract for Taiwan's Formosa 4

Norwegian company [Glamox](/companies/glamox.md) has secured a contract to supply marine
lighting for **35 wind turbine foundations** at the [Formosa 4](/projects/formosa-4.md)
offshore wind farm in Taiwan.

- **Scope**: more than **900 marine luminaires** for the transition pieces, covering both
  interior and exterior — boat landing areas, guardrails, ladders and access doors — plus
  **FL60 LED floodlights** on the platforms. The transition pieces get a variant of the
  **MIR G2** system that Glamox says is designed for offshore wind applications in Taiwan.
- **Counterparties**: an agreement with **ProCon Taiwan** and
  [SK oceanplant](/companies/sk-oceanplant.md).
- **Fabrication**: split between **Century Wind Power (CWP)** in Taiwan and SK oceanplant in
  South Korea, with deliveries scheduled for **August 2026**.

Glamox has a Taiwanese track record: [Hai Long](/projects/hai-long.md) 2 and 3 and Greater
Changhua 2b & 4 ([Changhua](/projects/changhua.md)) in 2024, and the 495 MW
[Fengmiao 1](/projects/fengmiao-1.md) in 2025 — all but the Changhua award contracted
through SK oceanplant.

Formosa 4, developed by **Synera Renewable Energy (SRE)**, will sit about **20 km off
Miaoli County** and comprise 35 [Siemens Gamesa](/companies/siemens-gamesa.md) 14 MW
turbines. SRE said in late December 2025 that the 495 MW project's entire output was sold
under three corporate PPAs.

No contract value, and no Formosa 4 installation or commissioning schedule, is stated.
