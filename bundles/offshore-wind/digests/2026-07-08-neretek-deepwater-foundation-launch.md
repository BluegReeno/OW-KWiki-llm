Offshore wind engineering firm Wood Thilsted and COP Frontier (a sister company to developer Copenhagen Offshore Partners) launched [Neretek](/companies/neretek.md), a new company built to unlock cost-effective deepwater offshore wind. Former GWEC Chair Jonathan Cole was named Executive Chair.

Neretek's proprietary Neretek BC90 foundation targets the 60-90 m "transitional" water-depth band, aiming to deliver monopile-like simplicity at lower CAPEX than deepwater monopiles or jackets and to bridge the gap between [fixed-bottom foundations](/technology/fixed-bottom-foundations.md) and [floating wind](/technology/floating-wind.md). It is designed to use proven technologies, existing local supply chains and ports, and to avoid reliance on large specialist vessels.

The launch was timed to the UK Government's 6 July announcement of AR8 (Allocation Round 8), which introduced a new "Other Deepwater Offshore Wind" (ODOW) category for 60-90 m depths.
