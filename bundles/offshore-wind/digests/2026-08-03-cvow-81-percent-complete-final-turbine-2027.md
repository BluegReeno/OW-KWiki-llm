# Summary

[Dominion Energy](/companies/dominion-energy.md) has revised the completion
timeline for [Coastal Virginia Offshore Wind](/projects/coastal-virginia-offshore-wind.md)
(CVOW), its 2.6 GW project off Virginia Beach and the largest offshore wind
farm under construction in the United States. The project is reported **81%
complete**, with the **final turbine expected by the end of 2027**.

Two new pages were created: CVOW had been referenced in the bundle — on the
[Siemens Gamesa](/companies/siemens-gamesa.md) page as an SG 14-222 DD
deployment, and on the [US BOEM leasing process](/policy/us-boem-leasing-process.md)
page as one of five projects hit by the December 2025 stop-work orders — but
had no page of its own, and neither did its sole owner.

The excerpt stops at the headline figures, so nothing further was asserted: no
revised full-commercial-operation date, no updated capital cost, no cause given
for the schedule revision, and no installed-turbine count. No tender or award
element is involved, so nothing is tagged `tenders`.
