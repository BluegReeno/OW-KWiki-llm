# Hai Long Secures Financing with 11 New Banks Joining

The [Hai Long offshore wind farm](/projects/hai-long.md) in Taiwan has secured
TWD 55 billion (nearly EUR 1.5 billion) in incremental financing within its
existing project finance framework, in a transaction involving 17 financial
institutions. Eleven lenders joined the structure for the first time —
Taiwanese state-owned and commercial banks plus Société Générale — while six
existing lenders, among them HSBC, Crédit Agricole CIB and DBS, increased their
commitments. Taiwan's National Credit Guarantee Administration participated
alongside the project's seven export credit agencies, bringing the number of
institutions supporting Hai Long to 35.

Hai Long is developed by Mitsui & Co., Northland Power and Gentari, and will
total more than 1 GW from 73 [Siemens Gamesa](/companies/siemens-gamesa.md)
14 MW turbines across three phases 45-70 km off Changhua County. Phase 2A
(21 turbines) was grid-connected at the end of 2025 and 2B's 16 turbines were
installed the same year; the 36-turbine Hai Long 3 is under construction toward
grid connection by the end of 2026.
