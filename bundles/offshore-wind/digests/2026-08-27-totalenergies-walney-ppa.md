# TotalEnergies Buys Walney Offshore Wind Power

**TotalEnergies Gas & Power Limited** has entered into a **long-term power
purchase agreement** for electricity produced by the
[Walney 1 & 2](/projects/walney.md) offshore wind farms in the UK. The PPA was
signed with **Offshore Partners Walney (OPW)**, which holds a **24.8% stake**
in the wind farms; [Ørsted](/companies/orsted.md) majority-owns and operates
them. TotalEnergies will buy **REGO-backed electricity** annually from OPW's
share of generation **through to 2030**. No volume, price or start date was
disclosed.

Walney 1 & 2 sit in the Irish Sea **15 km west of Barrow-in-Furness, Cumbria**,
with a combined **367 MW** from **102 Siemens 3.6 MW turbines**. Walney 1 was
fully operational in May 2011 and Walney 2 was completed at the end of 2011,
the whole project entering commercial operation in 2012.

This is the second Walney offtake deal reported in two months: in **July 2026**
UK supplier **Good Energy** signed a two-year PPA with Ørsted for **200 GWh a
year**, about one-sixth of the wind farms' output.

For [TotalEnergies](/companies/totalenergies.md) the deal lands a week after it
withdrew from [Lillebælt Syd](/projects/lillebaelt-syd.md) in Denmark — buying
power from an operating UK wind farm rather than holding equity in a project
still to be built.
