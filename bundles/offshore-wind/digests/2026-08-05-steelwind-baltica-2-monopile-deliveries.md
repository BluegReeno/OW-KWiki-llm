# Summary

[Steelwind Nordenham](/companies/steelwind-nordenham.md) has completed the delivery of all
monopiles under its contract for [Baltica 2](/projects/baltica-2.md), the ~1.5 GW Polish Baltic
Sea wind farm developed by PGE and [Ørsted](/companies/orsted.md). Completing a full foundation
supply scope is a durable milestone rather than routine news: it moves the German fabricator's
part of the [fixed-bottom foundation](/technology/fixed-bottom-foundations.md) chain from
production to done, and it marks the Polish market's second large project — after
[Baltic Power](/projects/baltic-power.md), which is already delivering electricity to the grid —
clearing its foundation-fabrication stage.

The excerpt is truncated after the opening clause, so nothing further is asserted here: no number
or weight of monopiles delivered, no contract value or award date, no share of the project's total
foundations (other suppliers may hold the remainder), no shipping or marshalling port, no
installation contractor or vessel, and no offshore installation or commissioning schedule.
