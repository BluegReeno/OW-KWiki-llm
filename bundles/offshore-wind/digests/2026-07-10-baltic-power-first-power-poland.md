# Poland Receives Its First Electricity from Offshore Wind as Baltic Power Starts Delivering

Electricity generated by the [Baltic Power offshore wind farm](/projects/baltic-power.md) has
entered Poland's national power grid, marking the first electricity ever produced by an offshore
wind farm for Poland and the start of the country's offshore wind era.

Baltic Power is a ~1,140 MW project in the Polish Baltic Sea, a joint venture between ORLEN and
Northland Power, being fitted with 76 [Vestas](/companies/vestas.md) V236-15.0 MW turbines.
