# Summary

[Arenko](/companies/arenko.md) has been selected by [Ørsted](/companies/orsted.md)
to deploy its **Nimbus** optimisation, asset management and trading platform at
**Iceni**, the 300 MW/600 MWh battery energy storage system (BESS) forming part
of the 2.9 GW [Hornsea 3](/projects/hornsea-3.md) offshore wind farm in the UK.
The battery is scheduled to enter operations in **Q1 2027**.

Iceni is being developed as one of the UK's first large-scale co-location
projects under the **Offshore Transmission Network Review (OTNR)**: rather than
securing a separate grid connection, it shares Hornsea 3's transmission
infrastructure and onshore grid connection — which Arenko calls the world's
first utility-scale battery directly integrated into offshore transmission
infrastructure.

Nimbus will forecast market and asset conditions, decide how battery capacity
is allocated in real time, automate trading across power markets, and support
the technical, data and controls integration required to run battery and wind
farm through a shared connection point. Ørsted's Katinka Hussman Palbo (VP,
Global Trading & Revenue) framed the challenge as optimising two very different
assets, wind and storage, through one connection; Arenko CEO Rupert Newland
called Iceni a coupling of a grid-scale battery with one of the UK's biggest
offshore wind farms.

Ørsted took FID on the BESS in June 2024, specifying a **Tesla** system of
600 MWh / 300 MW, and broke ground near **Norwich** in March 2025 at the same
site as the Hornsea 3 onshore converter station. Hornsea 3 itself, ~120 km off
Norfolk with 197 [Siemens Gamesa](/companies/siemens-gamesa.md) SG 14-236 DD
turbines, remains under construction and is scheduled for commissioning in 2027.
