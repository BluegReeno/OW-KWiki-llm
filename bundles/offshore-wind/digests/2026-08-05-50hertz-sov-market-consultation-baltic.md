# Summary

German transmission system operator [50Hertz](/companies/50hertz.md) has
launched a **preliminary market consultation** to assess the market for a
service operation vessel (SOV) to serve its offshore grid platforms in the
Baltic Sea.

A company page was created: 50Hertz is the TSO for the German Baltic Sea and
the counterpart to TenneT's North Sea role, and the bundle had no entry for it
despite already tracking German Baltic projects such as
[Gennaker](/projects/gennaker.md) and the
[HVDC/grid-connection](/technology/hvdc-grid-connection.md) thread. The page is
tagged `tenders`: a preliminary market consultation is the pre-procurement
stage of a possible SOV charter — a sounding of available tonnage, capability
and pricing, not yet a call for bids — so no `tenders/` page was created for a
procurement that does not formally exist yet.

What stands out is the buyer: vessel demand here comes from a **grid operator**
rather than a wind farm developer, the offshore transmission platforms
themselves now needing dedicated service logistics, alongside the developer-side
CSOV/SOV fleet build-out of
[Windward Munich](/digests/2026-07-31-windward-offshore-third-csov-windward-munich.md),
[North Star's fleet financing](/digests/2026-08-03-north-star-eur-321-million-fleet-financing.md)
and [Siemens Gamesa's Taiwan CSOV charters](/digests/2026-07-22-siemens-gamesa-marco-polo-marine-csovs-taiwan.md).

The item is a short excerpt truncated after the opening clause, so nothing
further was asserted: no number of vessels, charter length or start date, no
vessel specification, no named platforms or wind farms to be served, no
consultation deadline, and no timetable for an actual tender.
