# PTSC M&C Loads Out Fengmiao Offshore Substation

[PTSC Mechanical & Construction](/companies/ptsc-mc.md) has completed the
construction and loadout of the offshore substation for the
[Fengmiao 1](/projects/fengmiao-1.md) offshore wind farm, developed by
[Copenhagen Infrastructure Partners](/companies/copenhagen-infrastructure-partners.md)
in Taiwan.

Loadout is a durable construction milestone: it ends onshore fabrication and
hands the structure over to the offshore transport and installation campaign,
so the project's [grid connection](/technology/hvdc-grid-connection.md) hardware
is now ready to sail. It also records a Vietnamese yard delivering a full
offshore substation for a Taiwanese project, an Asia-Pacific supply-chain fact
comparable to the localisation moves tracked at
[EEW's Gwangyang expansion](/digests/2026-07-07-eew-group-south-korea-plant-expansion.md)
and the [Jindo supply chain MOU](/digests/2026-07-09-pacifico-jindo-supply-chain-mou.md).

The excerpt is truncated: no substation weight or dimensions, no fabrication
yard named, no sail-away or installation date, no installation contractor, and
no project capacity or commissioning schedule. Semco Maritime is listed among
the article's associated parties but the excerpt does not state its role, so no
page was created for it — the same treatment given to the
[Semco Maritime rebrand item](/digests/2026-07-23-wind-multiplikator-semco-maritime-rebrand.md).
New pages were created for both PTSC M&C and Fengmiao 1, neither of which
existed in the bundle, and the CIP company page was updated with its Taiwanese
project.
