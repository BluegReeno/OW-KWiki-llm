# Summary

Türkiye's Tersan Shipyard has begun construction of an ultra-large
cable-laying vessel (CLV) for South Korean cable manufacturer
[LS Cable & System](/companies/ls-cable-system.md). The excerpt available does
not disclose further vessel specifications (dimensions, capacity, delivery
date). The new CLV would add cable-installation capability alongside LS
Cable & System's manufacturing footprint, which includes its LS GreenLink
subsea cable factory under construction in the US.
