# Türkiye to Hold First Offshore Wind Tender in Early 2027

Energy and Natural Resources Minister **Alparslan Bayraktar** said Türkiye
will hold its first offshore wind **Renewable Energy Resource Area (YEKA)**
tender in **Q1 2027**, with the tender to be **announced in September 2026**,
ahead of COP31 in Antalya in November. He set out a national target of
**5,000 MW of offshore wind by 2035** and invited domestic and foreign
investors to participate.

Four candidate areas — **Saros Bay, Gökçeada, Bozcaada and Edremit** in
northwestern Türkiye — were announced in May 2026. Draft specifications
published in June envisage **1,000 MW of grid connection capacity**, a
**49-year YEKA usage and concession period**, a **reverse auction** with an
electricity price corridor of **USD 0.07-0.11/kWh**, three years for
pre-licence site work and permitting plus three years for construction, and
developer withdrawal rights if wind assessments show a capacity factor below
40% or seabed surveys reveal significant technical difficulties. The
consultation on the draft closed on **17 August 2026**.

Recorded as a new [Türkiye's First Offshore Wind Tender (YEKA)](/tenders/turkiye-first-yeka-offshore-wind-tender.md)
page: a dated auction timeline plus published draft rules is a durable
tender fact, and Türkiye had no entry in this bundle. It adds another
emerging market to the bundle's first-time-auction thread beside the
[Philippines' GEA-5](/tenders/philippines-gea-5-offshore-wind-auction.md),
[Romania's six Black Sea areas](/policy/romanian-offshore-wind-framework.md)
and [Azerbaijan's Caspian zone](/policy/azerbaijan-offshore-wind-framework.md).
No project or company pages: no sites are yet allocated and no bidders are
named.
