# Summary

Archer, EPC contractor for the [Culzean Floating Wind pilot](/projects/culzean-floating-wind.md), has completed integration of a [Vestas](/companies/vestas.md) turbine — tower, nacelle and blades — with the Ocergy-designed semi-submersible floater, and the unit is ready to be towed to its offshore location. Integration was carried out with project partners Global Maritime, Navitas Renewables, Wergeland Group, Sarens and Archer Vertikal.

The pilot is a single 3 MW [floating](/technology/floating-wind.md) turbine to be installed about 2 km west of the Culzean gas platform, some 220 km off Scotland's east coast. It is expected to cover roughly 20% of the platform's electricity demand, supplementing power now produced by gas turbines, and to serve as a demonstrator generating operational data for future commercial floating projects. For Ocergy it is the first commercial deployment of its OCG-Wind foundation.

Culzean was one of 13 projects awarded seabed rights in Crown Estate Scotland's 2023 Innovation and Targeted Oil & Gas (INTOG) leasing round, secured by TotalEnergies as operator of the Culzean gas field. TotalEnergies launched the project in August 2024, fabrication started that November, and the project is now led by NEO NEXT+ Energy, formed on 30 March 2026 by the merger of NEO Energy, Repsol UK and TotalEnergies' UK upstream business.

No tow date, installation window or commissioning schedule was given.
