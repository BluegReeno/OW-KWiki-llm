# MacGregor Secures DNV Certification for Floating-to-Floating Personnel Transfers

[MacGregor](/companies/macgregor.md) has received DNV certification for
floating-to-floating (F2F) personnel transfers using its Horizon gangway,
following a sea acceptance test.

For F2F operations the gangway carries a purpose-designed flap at its tip in
place of the docking head used for conventional landings on fixed structures,
while LiDAR-based relative-motion tracking feeds the motion-compensation
system so it can follow the movements of both vessels and maintain the
connection.

MacGregor says the capability opens up personnel transfers in offshore
operations including [floating wind](/technology/floating-wind.md), as well as
FPSOs, floating rigs and offshore service fleets, allowing crew changes and
vessel-to-vessel access offshore without fixed landing points or coordinating
around port calls. Prateek Sadhana, the company's Product Owner for
Walk-to-Work Gangways, framed it as supporting "the service logistics that
floating wind operations demand — where there is no fixed structure to land
on whatsoever". The F2F function is an extension of the existing Horizon
range rather than a separate product, and can be retrofitted or added to
gangways already in service.
