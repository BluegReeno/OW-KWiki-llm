# Summary

[Dajin Heavy Industry](/companies/dajin-heavy-industry.md) and
[Haventus](/companies/haventus.md), the owner and developer of **Ardersier
Port** in Scotland, have signed a Memorandum of Understanding to explore
combining Dajin's manufacturing and transport capability with Ardersier's port
infrastructure, in support of both
[fixed-bottom](/technology/fixed-bottom-foundations.md) and
[floating wind](/technology/floating-wind.md) projects in the UK and across
Europe. The two will also identify new commercial opportunities and assess how
the port could give Dajin a site for storage, marshalling, assembly and
transportation of offshore wind components.

Haventus markets Ardersier on extensive quayside land, deep-water access and a
high-voltage power connection, capable of supporting manufacturing,
fabrication, assembly and storage of large-scale structures. Chief commercial
officer Mark Gillespie framed the deal as reinforcing an ambition to make
Ardersier the leading offshore wind facility in the UK and Northern Europe;
Dajin business development director Artur Pielech described the port's scale
and location as a natural fit for bringing the company's logistics capability
closer to European projects. Dajin has recently put two King-class heavy
transport vessels into service.

Context reported by the article: Ardersier was the intended site of a GBP 1.5
billion turbine factory planned by **Mingyang Smart Energy**, which the UK
government rejected on suitability and national security grounds, with Mingyang
since reported to be eyeing Spain. Dajin, by contrast, has already supplied
foundations to several European projects, including
[Hornsea 3](/projects/hornsea-3.md), Moray West and
[Inch Cape](/projects/inch-cape.md).

# Why it matters

It is the second Chinese-supplier-into-Ardersier story at the same berth, but
of a different kind: components and logistics rather than a turbine factory —
a distinction that carries weight given why the previous plan was refused. It
also adds a second Moray Firth energy port to this bundle beside
[Maraen (Port of Nigg)](/companies/maraen.md), and moves Dajin from shipping
Chinese-fabricated foundations to European sites toward holding European
marshalling capacity.
