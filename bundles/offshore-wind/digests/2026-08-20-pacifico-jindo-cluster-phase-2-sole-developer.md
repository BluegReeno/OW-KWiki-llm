# Pacifico Energy Korea Named Sole Developer of 2.13 GW Jindo Cluster Phase 2

[Pacifico Energy Korea](/companies/pacifico-energy-korea.md) said on
20 August 2026 that it has been selected as the sole developer of the
2.13 GW Jindo Offshore Wind Integrated Cluster Phase 2 in South Korea. The
phase comprises the developer's Manho (990 MW) and Jindo Baram (1.8 GW)
projects, the second and third phases of its 3.2 GW
[Jindo Offshore Wind Project Cluster](/projects/jindo.md) off Jindo County.

The selection follows an Implementation Management Agreement signed between
the two projects, Jeonnam-Gwangju Special Metropolitan City (formerly South
Jeolla Province) and Jindo County. The regional government, which acts as
implementing institution, finalised the cluster development plans in 2025
and set up an evaluation committee in June 2026 to assess prospective
developers' capabilities and implementation plans. South Korea's Ministry of
Climate, Energy and Environment designated the area as an Integrated Cluster
in March 2026.

Phase 1, the 420 MW Myeong Ryang project, secured its Electricity Business
Licence in September 2025; the Manho and Jindo Baram licence applications
remain under review. Pacifico and the regional government point to the
planned Honam Semiconductor Cluster as a potential offtaker of the cluster's
renewable power.
