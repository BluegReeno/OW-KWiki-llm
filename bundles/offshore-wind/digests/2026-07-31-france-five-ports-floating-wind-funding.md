# Summary

The French government announced on 30 July 2026 that five ports — **Cherbourg,
Brest, Nantes-Saint Nazaire, Port-la-Nouvelle and Marseille-Fos** — will share
nearly **EUR 260 million** in France 2030 funding, administered by ADEME, to
upgrade infrastructure for the manufacture, assembly and deployment of
[floating offshore wind](/technology/floating-wind.md) projects. The award is
expected to leverage almost EUR 1 billion in total investment and to create a
network of complementary industrial hubs on the Atlantic, Channel and
Mediterranean coasts, in support of France's stated target of 6 GW of floating
wind by 2040.

Ports de Normandie put its Cherbourg upgrade at EUR 30 million: EUR 10.5 million
from ADEME, EUR 18.1 million from revenue generated by offshore renewable energy
activities at the port, and EUR 1.4 million from the Cotentin Urban Community.
No breakdown was published for the four other ports.

The funding lands between two other French milestones already tracked here: the
European Commission's [14 July clearance of a EUR 63 billion CfD scheme](/digests/2026-07-14-ec-approves-france-63bn-offshore-wind-aid.md)
for eleven wind farms, and the AO10 tender launched in June 2026, which is to
award a little over 10 GW across seven floating and four fixed-bottom farms.
