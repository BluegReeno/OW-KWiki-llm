# Cadeler Adds Wind Ace to Fleet, First Stop East Anglia Two

[Cadeler](/companies/cadeler.md) has taken delivery of **Wind Ace**, the
Danish installation contractor's eleventh wind installation vessel. The
vessel's first assignment is the **East Anglia Two** offshore wind farm in
the UK.

The delivery adds to Cadeler's growing fleet alongside newbuilds such as
Wind Apex, still under construction at COSCO Shipping Offshore. The excerpt
does not detail Wind Ace's specifications, builder, or charter terms.
