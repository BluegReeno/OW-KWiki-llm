# Dutch Cable Services Firm WIND Opens Baltic Hub in Port of Oskarshamn

[WIND Group](/companies/wind-group.md), a Netherlands-based subsea cable
logistics, cable recovery, and offshore services company, has opened a cable
storage and offshore service hub in Sweden to move closer to its clients in the
Baltic region.

WIND signed a long-term agreement with port operator Smålandshamnar to establish
a dedicated yard in the **Port of Oskarshamn**, from where it will offer cable
transportation, storage, cable equipment supply, carousel rental, logistics and
marshalling, plus maintenance and vessel mobilisation through its Draftec
business. The company said the port's 435-metre quay and 10.3-metre minimum water
depth suit the relevant vessels and enable efficient ship-to-ship cable
transfers. CEO Tom Nooij called the hub "an important step in the expansion of
WIND's activities in the Baltic region."
