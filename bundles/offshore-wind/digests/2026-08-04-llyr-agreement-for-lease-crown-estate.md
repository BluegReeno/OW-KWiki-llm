# Summary

[Cierco Energy](/companies/cierco-energy.md) has signed an **Agreement for Lease** with
The Crown Estate for its two test and demonstration projects off Wales,
[Llŷr 1 and Llŷr 2](/projects/llyr.md), which are expected to use two different types of
[floating wind](/technology/floating-wind.md) technology.

The sites were selected for development in 2021 in The Crown Estate's leasing opportunity
for early commercial-scale floating wind in the Celtic Sea, and are held through
**Floventis Energy**, Cierco's joint venture with SBM Offshore; public consultation on
Llŷr 1 opened in early 2024. They sit off the Pembrokeshire coast, about 40 km offshore
at depths averaging 60–70 metres, each rated 100 MW with six to eight turbines. In July
2025 Cierco signed an MoU with Marine Power Systems on possibly deploying MPS's PelaFlex
technology at one of the sites.

Cierco Energy's managing director Scott Harper called the projects "vital stepping stones,
giving us the chance to test world-leading offshore foundations, mooring systems and
dynamic cabling before scaling up to commercial deployments", and said delivery would
catalyse an industrial ecosystem across offshore fabrication, engineering, advanced
manufacturing and technology exports.

Tagged `tenders`: the AfL is the seabed-rights outcome of a Crown Estate leasing process.
No consent, turbine supplier, per-site floater choice, construction schedule, grid
connection or route to market was reported, and none is asserted here.
