# Summary

[Ocean Winds](/companies/ocean-winds.md) — the 50-50 joint venture of EDP Renewables and ENGIE — has secured offshore consent from the Scottish government for its 2 GW [Caledonia offshore wind farm](/projects/caledonia.md), proposed in the Moray Firth around 40 km off Wick and Banff. Announced on 17 July 2026, the offshore consent clears what Ocean Winds called the final major regulatory hurdle, following full onshore consent from Aberdeenshire Council earlier in the year.

The project comprises two wind farms, North and South Caledonia, spanning 429 square kilometres with up to 140 turbines in water depths of 40-100 m. Caledonia North uses [fixed-bottom foundations](/technology/fixed-bottom-foundations.md), while Caledonia South mixes fixed-bottom and [floating](/technology/floating-wind.md) foundations. Offshore construction is expected in 2030, subject to a Contract for Difference (CfD) and final investment decision.
