# Summary

Shanghai Zhenhua Heavy Industries ([ZPMC](/companies/zpmc.md)) has laid the
keel for the second service operation vessel (SOV) it is building for French
shipowner [Louis Dreyfus Armateurs](/companies/louis-dreyfus-armateurs.md).
The vessel is chartered for the
[Nordlicht offshore wind farms](/projects/nordlicht.md), the 1.6 GW German
North Sea cluster [Vattenfall](/companies/vattenfall.md) took FID on in
January 2026 and expects online in 2028.

The item pairs a Chinese yard, a French owner and a Swedish developer's German
project: SOV tonnage for European waters increasingly ordered in Asia, in the
same weeks as [Cadeler's two foundation installation vessels at COSCO](/digests/2026-08-10-cadeler-two-foundation-installation-vessels-cosco.md)
and the run of Asian-built cable-laying vessels
([ZTT](/digests/2026-08-07-ztt-lan-hai-ling-hang-cable-laying-vessel.md),
[Taihan](/digests/2026-07-17-taihan-second-cable-laying-vessel.md)). It also
marks the point where Nordlicht's story turns from
[foundation fabrication and installation](/technology/fixed-bottom-foundations.md)
— [first monopile](/projects/nordlicht.md) in July, [first transition
piece](/digests/2026-08-04-nordlicht-i-first-transition-piece-installed.md) on
4 August — toward the vessels that will serve the farm through construction
and operation, alongside the wider North Sea and Baltic SOV/CSOV demand
tracked at [Baltica 2](/digests/2026-08-06-baltica-2-csov-contracts.md) and in
[50Hertz's SOV market consultation](/digests/2026-08-05-50hertz-sov-market-consultation-baltic.md).

Tagged `tenders` because the item turns on a charter — a contract award —
even though the award itself is reported only as background to the keel-laying.

The excerpt is truncated after the opening clause, so nothing further is
asserted: no vessel name, design or designer, no dimensions, accommodation
capacity, propulsion or hybrid/battery specification, no delivery date, no
charter counterparty, length or value, no status of the first vessel in the
series, and no split of service between Nordlicht I and Nordlicht II.
