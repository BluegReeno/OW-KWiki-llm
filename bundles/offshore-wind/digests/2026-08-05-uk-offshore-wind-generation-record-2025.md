# Summary

Offshore wind generated a record **17.7 per cent of the UK's electricity in
2025**, the highest share the technology has reached in the British power mix.

No new page was warranted. This is a market-level statistic rather than a new
company, project or tender, and the bundle already carries the framework that
produced the fleet behind it: the
[UK Contracts for Difference scheme](/policy/uk-contracts-for-difference-scheme.md),
whose successive allocation rounds have been the primary route to market for
new UK offshore wind capacity. That page gained a short generation-share note —
the record is the aggregate output of what the auctions have built, and it is a
durable, dated figure rather than news noise, so it belongs on the policy page
as outcome rather than as a page of its own.

Nothing was tagged `tenders`: no call for bids, auction round or award is
involved, though the figure is the backdrop against which the next UK rounds and
the [Morgan site re-tender](/tenders/crown-estate-morgan-site-retender-2026.md)
are run.

The item is a short excerpt truncated after the headline figure, so nothing
further was asserted: no total generation in TWh, no installed capacity or
load factor, no comparison figure for 2024 or any previous record year, no
breakdown by wind farm or operator, no share for onshore wind or renewables
overall, and no source body for the statistics.
