# OEG Secures Corrosion Protection Contract for Gennaker Offshore Wind Farm

[OEG](/companies/oeg.md) has secured a contract to provide a subsea corrosion
protection system for the [Gennaker](/projects/gennaker.md) offshore wind farm,
the 976.5 MW project [Skyborn Renewables](/companies/skyborn.md) is developing
in the German Baltic Sea off Mecklenburg-Vorpommern.

This is a contract award on a project under construction, so it was recorded as
a durable fact rather than left digest-only: a new [OEG](/companies/oeg.md)
company page was created — OEG had until now appeared in the bundle only as a
party to a [Korean BoP O&M MoU](/digests/2026-07-16-owc-klem-oeg-korea-bop-om-pact.md),
too thin for a page at the time — and the Gennaker page gained the award in its
supply chain section. Tagged `tenders` on both pages and this digest, as a
contract award.

It extends Gennaker's supply-chain thread beyond steel: a week after
[Dajin Heavy Industry started fabricating the project's 63 transition
pieces](/digests/2026-08-04-dajin-gennaker-transition-pieces-fabrication.md),
the item adds the protective system that keeps those
[fixed-bottom foundations](/technology/fixed-bottom-foundations.md) intact
over a 25-30 year design life — a scope the bundle has not tracked before,
sitting between foundation fabrication and the
[scour protection](/digests/2026-07-20-dogger-bank-c-scour-protection.md)
packages logged elsewhere.

The excerpt is truncated after the opening clause, so nothing further is
asserted here: no contract value, no system type (sacrificial anode or
impressed current), no number of foundations covered, no design life,
no delivery or installation schedule, no installation contractor, and no
statement on whether the scope covers monopiles, transition pieces or the
offshore substation.
