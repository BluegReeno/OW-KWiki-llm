# Summary

As part of its **first-half 2026 results**, [Ørsted](/companies/orsted.md) said
three of its offshore wind farms under construction are set to reach **full
commissioning in the second half of 2026**:

- **[Borkum Riffgrund 3](/projects/borkum-riffgrund-3.md)** (Germany, 913 MW,
  Ørsted and Nuveen Infrastructure) — more than **99% complete**, remaining
  turbines being commissioned, full commissioning expected in **Q3 2026**. Its
  83 [Siemens Gamesa](/companies/siemens-gamesa.md) 11 MW turbines were all
  installed by early 2026; first power went into the German grid in December
  2025 after DolWin5 grid-connection delays.
- **[Greater Changhua 2b & 4](/projects/changhua.md)** (Taiwan, 920 MW) —
  approximately **85% complete**, on track for **Q3 2026**. All 42 GC4 turbines
  are commissioned and generating; the GC2b export cable, damaged in 2025, is
  due to be completed shortly.
- **[Revolution Wind](/projects/revolution-wind.md)** (US, 704 MW, with
  [Skyborn Renewables](/companies/skyborn.md)) — more than **95% complete**,
  **61 of 65** Siemens Gamesa 11 MW turbines installed, delivering power to the
  New England grid since March 2026.

Later projects: **[Sunrise Wind](/projects/sunrise-wind.md)** (924 MW, New
York) is 50% complete with 20 of 84 turbines installed and foundations at 77
positions as of 30 June — turbine commissioning in H2 2026 but full operation
only in H2 2027; **[Baltica 2](/projects/baltica-2.md)** (Poland, 1.5 GW, with
PGE) is ~40% complete with 103 of 111 foundations installed, commissioning H2
2027; and **[Hornsea 3](/projects/hornsea-3.md)** (UK, 2.9 GW, with
Apollo-managed funds) is ~30% complete, commissioning Q4 2027 or Q1 2028, with
onshore grid reinforcement work still ongoing.
