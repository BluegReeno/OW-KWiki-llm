# Inch Cape Monopile Foundation Installation Completed, Jackets Up Next

offshoreWIND.biz reported on 11 August 2026 that installation of all 54
transition pieces for the monopile foundations at the 1.1 GW
[Inch Cape offshore wind farm](/projects/inch-cape.md) is complete,
closing out the monopile campaign, with the project's jacket foundations
up next. The article is filed under Seaway7, indicating the contractor
associated with the foundation installation scope; the excerpt itself
breaks off after the opening clause, so this is recorded as the article's
own filing rather than as a claim from the text, and no company page was
created for it.

Nothing beyond the milestone is asserted: no installation vessel, no
dates for the individual campaigns, no start date or duration for the
jacket programme, no fabricator for either foundation type, and no
change to the turbine installation or first power schedule.

The milestone completes the larger half of Inch Cape's split
[fixed-bottom foundation](/technology/fixed-bottom-foundations.md)
design — 54 monopiles plus 18 jackets carrying 72
[Vestas](/companies/vestas.md) V236-15.0 MW turbines — and follows the
[second export cable installation campaign completed in July](/digests/2026-07-03-inch-cape-export-cable-installation-completed.md),
leaving the ESB/Red Rock Renewables project with foundations and export
cables largely behind it ahead of first power expected late 2026. It
sits alongside the bundle's other live transition-piece threads at
[Nordlicht I](/digests/2026-08-04-nordlicht-i-first-transition-piece-installed.md)
and [Gennaker](/digests/2026-08-04-dajin-gennaker-transition-pieces-fabrication.md),
here at the completion rather than the opening end of the campaign. No
tender or award element, so nothing tagged `tenders`; the project page
keeps the tag it carries from its UK CfD awards.
