# Summary

offshoreWIND.biz reported on 19 August 2026 that
[China Three Gorges](/companies/china-three-gorges.md)' 800 MW Jiangsu
[Rudong](/projects/rudong.md) offshore wind project has generated more than
10 TWh of electricity.

Both pages are new. The milestone is a durable operating fact for an 800 MW
wind farm, and neither CTG — one of China's largest offshore wind developers —
nor Rudong had any entry in this bundle, whose Chinese coverage so far has been
equipment and construction events: the
[HV-TSU substation installed at Yuetuo Island](/projects/yuetuo-island.md) and
[CNOOC's Haiyou Anlan floating turbine](/projects/haiyou-anlan-lufeng.md). This
is the bundle's first cumulative-generation milestone of any kind, and it adds
an operating Chinese asset beside those under-construction entries.

No `tenders` tag: a generation milestone is not a call for bids, auction round
or contract award, and no offtake or tariff arrangement is stated. No
technology or policy page: the item describes no technical concept and no
regulatory change.

The excerpt is truncated after the opening clause, so nothing beyond it was
asserted: no commissioning date or years in service, no turbine supplier, model
or count, no foundation type, no water depth or distance from shore, no grid
connection or offtake arrangement, no annual output or capacity factor behind
the cumulative figure, no CO2 or household-equivalent framing, and no other CTG
offshore wind assets or capacity totals.
