# Summary

[Principle Power](/companies/principle-power.md) has been awarded the Operations and Maintenance (O&M) support contract for the floating foundations of the [Éoliennes Flottantes du Golfe du Lion (EFGL)](/projects/efgl-golfe-du-lion.md) floating wind farm, the 30 MW [floating offshore wind](/technology/floating-wind.md) project in the Gulf of Lion developed by [Ocean Winds](/companies/ocean-winds.md). The farm reached full power earlier in July 2026.
