# Summary

Dutch foundation manufacturer [Sif](/companies/sif.md) has loaded out the
**final monopiles** for the [Bałtyk 2 & 3](/projects/baltyk-2-3.md) offshore
wind farms, the 1.4 GW Polish Baltic Sea development of
[Equinor](/companies/equinor.md) and Polenergia, with turbines supplied by
[Siemens Gamesa](/companies/siemens-gamesa.md) and Smulders associated with
the foundation scope.

The load-out closes Sif's fabrication campaign for the projects, which started
offshore construction in February 2026 and expect first power in 2027. It is a
durable supply-chain milestone for one of the largest offshore wind projects
under construction in the Baltic.
