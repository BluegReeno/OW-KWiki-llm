# Boskalis's Windpiper Sails from Rotterdam for Its First Job at Baltic Power

[Boskalis](/companies/boskalis.md)'s new subsea rock installation (SRI)
vessel **Windpiper** has departed the Port of Rotterdam to begin work on
a Polish offshore wind project, [Baltic Power](/projects/baltic-power.md).
This is the vessel's first rock installation campaign, following its
christening on 3 July 2026 ([digest](/digests/2026-07-06-boskalis-windpiper-christening.md)),
where Boskalis said Windpiper — 227 m long, 45,500 tonnes of rock-carrying
capacity — was ready for a first project in the Baltic Sea.

Baltic Power, the ~1,140 MW ORLEN/Northland Power joint venture, delivered
Poland's first offshore wind electricity to the national grid on 10 July
2026 ([digest](/digests/2026-07-10-baltic-power-first-power-poland.md)), so
the rock installation work falls in the tail of its construction phase,
alongside [scour protection](/technology/fixed-bottom-foundations.md) and
cable protection scopes typical of an SRI vessel.

The excerpt is truncated after the vessel's departure from Rotterdam: it
gives no contract scope or value, no tonnage to be installed, no client
naming for the rock works, and no campaign duration, so nothing beyond the
mobilisation is asserted here.
