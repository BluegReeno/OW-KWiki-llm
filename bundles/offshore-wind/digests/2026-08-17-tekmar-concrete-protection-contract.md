# Tekmar Secures EUR 1 Million Concrete Protection Contract for 'Major' European Offshore Wind Project

[Tekmar Group](/companies/tekmar-group.md) has been awarded a contract valued at
around EUR 1 million to supply concrete protection for a European offshore wind
project the article describes only as 'major'. The item is filed under cable
protection systems, the product family Tekmar is best known for.

A company page was created: a named contract award with a stated value is a
durable corporate fact, and Tekmar had no entry in this bundle despite cable and
seabed protection being a recurring thread — [OEG](/companies/oeg.md)'s subsea
corrosion protection scope at [Gennaker](/projects/gennaker.md), and scour
protection at [Dogger Bank](/projects/dogger-bank.md) and
[Hollandse Kust West VI](/projects/hollandse-kust-west-vi.md). Tagged `tenders`
as a contract award; no project page, since the wind farm is not named.

The excerpt is truncated after the opening clause ("awarded a contract valued at
around EUR 1 million to [...]"), so nothing further was asserted: no client,
project, country or capacity, no scope volume or product type, no delivery or
installation schedule, no installation contractor, and no Tekmar ownership,
nationality, revenue or wider order book.
