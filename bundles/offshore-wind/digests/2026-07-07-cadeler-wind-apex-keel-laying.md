# Cadeler's Wind Apex Starts Taking Shape

[Cadeler](/companies/cadeler.md) and COSCO Shipping Offshore have held a
keel-laying ceremony for **Wind Apex**, one of Cadeler's offshore wind
installation vessel newbuilds being built at COSCO's yard. The ceremony
marks the point at which the vessel begins to take physical shape on the
slipway.
