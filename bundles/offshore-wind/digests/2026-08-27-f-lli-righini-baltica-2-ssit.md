# F.lli Righini Delivers Secondary Structure Installation Tools for Baltica 2

In a branded-content item published on 27 August 2026, [F.lli Righini](/companies/f-lli-righini.md)
S.p.A. said it had designed, tested and delivered advanced Secondary Structure Installation
Tools (SSIT) for [Van Oord](/companies/van-oord.md)'s work at the
[Baltica 2 offshore wind project](/projects/baltica-2.md) in the Polish Baltic Sea.

The equipment installs secondary steel on the
[monopile foundations](/technology/fixed-bottom-foundations.md) — boat landings and internal
platforms mounted on the piles — with a separate dedicated tool for external platform
operations. The complete systems, including offshore seafastening structures, are built for
safe, unmanned and remotely operated installation in demanding marine conditions; the stated
engineering challenge was handling different secondary steel components within strict
tolerances while keeping stability and positioning accuracy offshore. The company adds it
delivered two comparable Secondary Steel Installation Tools in 2023 for 7 m and 7.5 m
monopiles on German offshore wind farms, and that it supports clients through commissioning
and offshore installation with its own personnel.

The delivery sits at the tail of Van Oord's Baltica 2 foundation campaign, which ran from
May 2026 and closed with all 111 monopiles installed on 21 August 2026.

Not reported: contract value, date of award or delivery, number of tools, monopile diameter
at Baltica 2, the vessel or crane used, or which German wind farms took the 2023 tools.
