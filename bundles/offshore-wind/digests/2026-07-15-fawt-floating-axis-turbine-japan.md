# Experimental Floating Axis Wind Turbine Installed Offshore Japan

The Floating Axis Wind Turbine (FAWT) consortium, made up of six Japanese
companies, has installed an experimental Floating Axis Wind Turbine offshore
Japan. The FAWT concept is a variant of [floating offshore wind](/technology/floating-wind.md)
that departs from the conventional horizontal-axis design.

This is an early-stage, experimental deployment. The excerpt provides no
details on turbine capacity, location, the consortium members, or timeline,
so no durable concept page is warranted yet — digest-only.
