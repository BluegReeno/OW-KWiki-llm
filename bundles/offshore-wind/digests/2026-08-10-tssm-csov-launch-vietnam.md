# TSSM Launches New CSOV in Vietnam

offshoreWIND.biz reported on 10 August 2026 that
[Ta San Shang Marine](/companies/ta-san-shang-marine.md) (TSSM), a joint
venture involving [Mitsui O.S.K. Lines](/companies/mitsui-osk-lines.md)
(MOL), has launched a new commissioning service operation vessel (CSOV)
in Vietnam. The article is filed under Damen and Ha Long Shipyard,
indicating a Damen-associated design built at the Vietnamese yard.

The excerpt breaks off after the opening clause, so nothing beyond this
is asserted: the second joint venture partner is not named, and there is
no vessel name, design type, dimensions, berth count, propulsion or
hybrid specification, delivery or commissioning date, charter, or target
market for the vessel.

It adds a Vietnamese-built hull to the CSOV order book the bundle has
been tracking through [Windward Offshore](/companies/windward-offshore.md),
[Marco Polo Marine](/companies/marco-polo-marine.md),
[Bernhard Schulte Offshore](/companies/bernhard-schulte-offshore.md) and
[Pelagic Partners](/companies/pelagic-partners.md), and gives Vietnam a
second role in the sector beside
[PTSC M&C](/companies/ptsc-mc.md)'s fabrication work and the
[EOLOS/Enviros metocean partnership](/digests/2026-08-07-eolos-enviros-vietnam-metocean.md):
shipbuilding for offshore wind service tonnage. No tender or award
element, so nothing tagged `tenders`.
