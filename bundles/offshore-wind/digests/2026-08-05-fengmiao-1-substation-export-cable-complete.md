# Offshore Substation and Export Cable Works Completed at Taiwan's Fengmiao 1

Two offshore construction milestones have been completed at the 495 MW
[Fengmiao 1](/projects/fengmiao-1.md) offshore wind farm in Taiwan, developed by
[Copenhagen Infrastructure Partners](/companies/copenhagen-infrastructure-partners.md):
the offshore substation and the export cable works.

This is a durable milestone rather than progress noise, and it closes a chain
the bundle picked up a week earlier: on 29 July
[PTSC M&C loaded out the substation](/digests/2026-07-29-ptsc-mc-fengmiao-substation-loadout.md)
from the yard, and that structure is now offshore with its export cable in
place. With the [grid connection](/technology/hvdc-grid-connection.md) hardware
complete, the evacuation route exists before the turbines do — the same
sequence tracked in Europe at [Nordlicht](/projects/nordlicht.md) and
[Baltic Power](/projects/baltic-power.md). The item also confirms the project's
capacity at 495 MW, which the earlier item did not give.
