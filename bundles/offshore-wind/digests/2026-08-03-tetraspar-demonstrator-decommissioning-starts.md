# TetraSpar Demonstrator Decommissioning Starts

Decommissioning work has started on the
[TetraSpar Demonstrator](/projects/tetraspar-demonstrator.md) floating wind
project at the Marine Energy Test Centre (METCentre) off Norway, offshoreWIND.biz
reported on **3 August 2026**.

The demonstrator put a [Siemens Gamesa](/companies/siemens-gamesa.md) turbine of
roughly 3.6 MW on Stiesdal Offshore's modular tetrahedral floater with a
suspended keel, in the water since 2021, with RWE, Shell and TEPCO Renewable
Power alongside Stiesdal in the ownership consortium.

The excerpt is truncated after the location, so nothing further is asserted: no
removal contractor or vessel spread, no disassembly sequence, no schedule, no
tow destination, no reuse or recycling plan for the floater, keel and moorings,
and no stated reason for ending the campaign now.

The item is a rare end-of-life entry in a news flow otherwise dominated by
[floating wind](/technology/floating-wind.md) deployments — the
[Culzean pilot awaiting tow-out](/digests/2026-07-31-culzean-floating-turbine-ready-for-tow.md)
and [EFGL reaching full power](/digests/2026-07-10-efgl-full-power.md) — and the
first floating-prototype removal recorded in this bundle.
