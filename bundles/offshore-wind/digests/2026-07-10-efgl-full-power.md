# Summary

The 30 MW [Éoliennes Flottantes du Golfe du Lion (EFGL)](/projects/efgl-golfe-du-lion.md) [floating offshore wind](/technology/floating-wind.md) farm, located in the Gulf of Lion in the French Mediterranean and developed by [Ocean Winds](/companies/ocean-winds.md), has reached full power — all turbines now producing at rated capacity. It is one of France's pilot floating wind projects.
