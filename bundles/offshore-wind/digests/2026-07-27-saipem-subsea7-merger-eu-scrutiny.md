# Saipem-Subsea7 Merger Under Microscope as EU Flags Competition Concerns

A proposed merger between [Saipem](/companies/saipem.md) and
[Subsea 7](/companies/subsea-7.md) is facing deeper scrutiny in the
European Union, with the European Commission flagging competition
concerns.

Both are among the largest offshore engineering and installation
contractors serving oil and gas as well as offshore wind — Subsea 7
through its Seaway7 renewables arm — so a combination would materially
consolidate the pool of heavy installation capacity available to offshore
wind developers, alongside [DEME Offshore](/companies/deme-offshore.md),
[Van Oord](/companies/van-oord.md) and [Boskalis](/companies/boskalis.md).

A regulatory review of a merger of this scale is a durable corporate fact,
so company pages were created for both parties. The excerpt is truncated
and gives no procedural stage, review deadline, deal value, defined
markets of concern, or remedies, so nothing further was asserted.
