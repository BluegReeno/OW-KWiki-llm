# Summary

[Encomara](/companies/encomara.md), the Aberdeen-based floating wind and subsea engineering company, says its **Squid** connection system for [floating offshore wind](/technology/floating-wind.md) has reached **Technology Readiness Level 7/8** and commercialisation.

The step comes three weeks after the company began the first in-water trials of a Squid unit in inshore Scottish waters, reported on 7 August 2026. TRL 7/8 marks the move from prototype demonstration in an operational environment to a qualified, complete system — the point at which a connection product is offered commercially rather than only tested.

# Why It Matters

Mooring, dynamic cabling and quick-connect hardware are among the least standardised parts of the floating wind value chain, and connection time offshore is a direct cost driver for arrays that have to be hooked up — and, for tow-to-port maintenance, disconnected and reconnected — repeatedly. A UK connection product crossing from trial to commercial availability is a small but durable supply-chain fact for a segment where most items recorded here are still demonstrators.
