# Summary

Encomara, an Aberdeen-based floating wind and subsea engineering company, has begun preparations to trial the first unit of its Squid floating wind connection system inshore in Scotland. It is the concept's first in-water test campaign, a de-risking step ahead of demonstration on a working floating platform.

The item is a technology-development milestone rather than a project or contract event: no client, site or delivery commitment was reported.
