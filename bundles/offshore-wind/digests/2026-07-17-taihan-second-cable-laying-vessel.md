# Summary

South Korean cable manufacturer [Taihan Cable & Solution](/companies/taihan-cable-solution.md)
has completed the delivery of its second cable-laying vessel (CLV), the
*Skandi Connector*, expanding its subsea cable-installation fleet. The
available excerpt does not disclose vessel specifications, delivering
shipyard, charter terms or the projects it will serve. The move mirrors
fellow South Korean cable maker [LS Cable & System](/companies/ls-cable-system.md)'s
build-out of its own cable-laying capability.
