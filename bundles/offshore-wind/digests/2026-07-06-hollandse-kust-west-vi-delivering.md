# New Dutch Offshore Wind Farm Starts Delivering

The 760 MW [Hollandse Kust West VI](/projects/hollandse-kust-west-vi.md)
offshore wind farm in the Dutch North Sea is now connected to the grid and
has started delivering power. The project is developed and operated by
[Ecowende](/companies/ecowende.md), a joint venture of Shell, Eneco, and
Chubu Electric Power, awarded via a subsidy-free Dutch tender in December
2022. New project and company pages were created to capture this milestone.
