# Summary

The first electricity generated by the Nordseecluster A offshore wind farm has
been fed into the German grid, [RWE](/companies/rwe-offshore-wind.md) reported.
Nordseecluster A is the 660 MW first phase of RWE's 1.6 GW
[Nordseecluster](/projects/nordseecluster.md) development in the German North
Sea, using [Vestas](/companies/vestas.md) turbines; the 900 MW Nordseecluster B
phase follows.

First power is a durable project milestone, so a dedicated
[Nordseecluster](/projects/nordseecluster.md) project page was created and the
RWE company page updated. The excerpt is truncated and gives no turbine count
at the time of first power, no grid-connection or commissioning date, and no
statement on full commercial operation, so the page stays lean on those points.
