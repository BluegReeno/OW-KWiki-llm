# Cadeler acquires Menck for EUR 501 million

[Cadeler](/companies/cadeler.md) has acquired **[Menck](/companies/menck.md)**,
a provider of equipment and technology for offshore foundation
installation, for **EUR 501 million** (12 August 2026).

## Why it matters here

The purchase moves Cadeler from owning the tonnage that installs
[fixed-bottom foundations](/technology/fixed-bottom-foundations.md) to
owning the equipment and technology used to install them. It lands two
days after Cadeler
[ordered two dedicated foundation installation vessels at COSCO's Qidong
yard](/digests/2026-08-10-cadeler-two-foundation-installation-vessels-cosco.md),
and follows the [Wind Apex keel laying](/digests/2026-07-07-cadeler-wind-apex-keel-laying.md)
and the [Wind Ace delivery](/digests/2026-07-17-cadeler-wind-ace-delivery.md):
a foundation-installation build-out on both the vessel and the equipment
side within one month, against the fabrication bottleneck the bundle has
tracked through [Haizea's XXL monopiles for Hornsea 3](/digests/2026-08-06-haizea-hornsea-3-xxl-monopiles.md),
[Steelwind's Baltica 2 deliveries](/digests/2026-08-05-steelwind-baltica-2-monopile-deliveries.md)
and [CS Wind Offshore's Nordlicht transition pieces](/digests/2026-07-30-cs-wind-offshore-nordlicht-i-transition-pieces.md).
It is also the largest corporate transaction in the bundle's marine
contractor thread, beside the [Saipem–Subsea7 merger under EU
scrutiny](/digests/2026-07-27-saipem-subsea7-merger-eu-scrutiny.md).

Tagged `tenders` as a corporate transaction of contract-award weight; no
`tenders/` page was created, since no call for bids or auction round is
involved.
