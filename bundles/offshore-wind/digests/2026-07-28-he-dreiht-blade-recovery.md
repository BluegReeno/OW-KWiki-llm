# Summary

The German Federal Police (Bundespolizei) recovered a detached wind turbine
rotor blade that was drifting at sea. The blade came off a turbine at the
He Dreiht offshore wind farm in the German North Sea, developed by EnBW with
turbines supplied by [Vestas](/companies/vestas.md).

# Why it is digest-only

The excerpt is truncated and supports only the recovery itself. It does not
state when the blade detached, which turbine or turbine model it came from,
the cause, whether the wind farm's construction or operation was affected, or
what investigation or remediation follows. He Dreiht has no project page and
EnBW no company page in this bundle yet; a single recovery operation, without
any of the surrounding facts, is not a durable change of project status,
company role or technology assessment. This follows the same treatment as the
[Wind Orca allision at Port of Tyne](/digests/2026-07-27-port-of-tyne-wind-orca-allision.md).

Blade structural failures are nevertheless a recurring reliability theme for
the current generation of very large offshore rotors — see
[turbine scaling](/technology/turbine-scaling.md). If follow-up reporting
establishes the cause or a fleet-wide implication for the machines involved,
a He Dreiht project page and an update to the
[Vestas](/companies/vestas.md) page would be warranted.
