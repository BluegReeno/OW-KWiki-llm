# Summary

PosHYdon — described as the first project in the world to combine offshore wind power, offshore gas production and offshore hydrogen production — has produced its first green hydrogen on an operational offshore platform in the North Sea. Named partners in the announcement include Eni and [DEME](/companies/deme-offshore.md).

This is the milestone the pilot was built for: running an electrolyser inside the operating envelope of a live offshore hydrocarbon facility, rather than on a purpose-built demonstrator. If replicable, it opens a route to reusing existing platforms and gas pipelines for hydrogen export instead of building dedicated offshore infrastructure — a variant of the centralized [Power-to-X](/technology/power-to-x.md) architecture that avoids the capital cost of a new platform.

The source item is a short excerpt, so production volumes, electrolyser capacity and the operating schedule are not stated here.
