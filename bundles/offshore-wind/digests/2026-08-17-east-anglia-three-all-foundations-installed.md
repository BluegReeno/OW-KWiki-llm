# All Foundations In at East Anglia Three

Foundation installation has been completed at the **1.4 GW East Anglia THREE**
offshore wind farm off Suffolk, developed by ScottishPower Renewables
([Iberdrola](/companies/iberdrola.md)) with Masdar as minority partner. A
[project page](/projects/east-anglia-three.md) was created: a completed
foundation campaign on a 1.4 GW build is a durable construction milestone, and
the project had no entry in the bundle despite East Anglia Two already appearing
here through a [supply-chain
digest](/digests/2026-07-23-smulders-palfinger-east-anglia-two-davit-cranes.md).

It is the third UK-waters foundation campaign to close this month, after
[Inch Cape's monopiles](/projects/inch-cape.md) and alongside
[Hornsea 3](/projects/hornsea-3.md), which is still installing.

The publisher files the article under
[Haizea Wind Group](/companies/haizea-wind-group.md), Navantia-Windar and
Seaway7 ([Subsea 7](/companies/subsea-7.md)), indicating fabrication and
installation roles; the excerpt does not state the scope split, so no company
page was changed. Nothing beyond the excerpt was asserted: no foundation type or
count, no turbine supplier, no installation vessels, no campaign dates, and no
first power or commissioning schedule.
