# Summary

[Vattenfall](/companies/vattenfall.md) has launched a **tender** seeking
contractors to provide remotely operated vehicle (ROV) inspections and seabed
surveys for its **European offshore wind fleet**.

No new page was created: Vattenfall already has a company page, and a services
procurement of this kind does not describe a project, a technology or a market
framework of its own. It is nonetheless a durable fact rather than news noise —
it is a call for bids, so the item is tagged `tenders`, and the company page
gained a fleet operations and procurement section recording it.

What stands out is the **fleet-wide** framing. Vattenfall now operates across
the UK, the Netherlands, Denmark, Germany and Sweden, with new capacity coming
at [Nordlicht](/projects/nordlicht.md) and, from the August 2026
[Danish CfD awards](/tenders/denmark-2-8gw-cfd-tender-2026.md), at
[North Sea I Mid](/projects/nordsoen-midt.md) and [Hesselø](/projects/hesselo.md).
Buying subsea inspection and seabed survey once for the whole portfolio, rather
than project by project, is the O&M-side counterpart to the pre-procurement
sounding [50Hertz ran for an SOV](/digests/2026-08-05-50hertz-sov-market-consultation-baltic.md)
and to survey procurement on the development side, such as
[RVO's DoordeWind benthic survey tender](/digests/2026-07-29-rvo-doordewind-benthic-survey-tender.md)
and the [Bellrock export cable corridor survey](/digests/2026-07-21-bellrock-export-cable-corridor-survey.md).

The item is a short excerpt truncated after the opening clause, so nothing
further was asserted: no contract value, duration or number of lots, no named
wind farms or countries covered, no bid deadline or award timetable, no split
between inspection and survey scopes, no vessel or ROV requirements, and no
incumbent or candidate contractors.
