# EEW Group Expanding South Korean Foundation Plant

[EEW Group](/companies/eew-group.md) is expanding its South Korean
foundation plant, **EEW KHPC**, in Gwangyang, Jeollanam-do. The expansion
grows the group's [monopile foundation](/technology/fixed-bottom-foundations.md)
manufacturing capacity in South Korea.
