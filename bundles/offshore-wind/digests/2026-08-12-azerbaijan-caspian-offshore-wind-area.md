# Summary

Azerbaijan's Cabinet of Ministers has formally designated a **711.4-square-kilometre
water area** in the country's sector of the **Caspian Sea** for offshore wind.

This is a durable regulatory fact rather than news noise — a government act that
creates the spatial basis for any future leasing, permitting or allocation — so a
new [Azerbaijan Offshore Wind Framework (Caspian Sea)](/policy/azerbaijan-offshore-wind-framework.md)
policy page was created. It is the bundle's first Caspian entry and its second
inland-sea framework after
[Romania's Black Sea regime](/policy/romanian-offshore-wind-framework.md).

No `tenders` tag: the excerpt describes an area designation only, with no call for
bids, auction round or award.
