# Summary

PKR Offshore (PKRO), the offshore wind subsidiary of Singapore-based
[Marco Polo Marine](/companies/marco-polo-marine.md), has signed a framework agreement
under which [Siemens Gamesa](/companies/siemens-gamesa.md) books two commissioning
service operation vessels (CSOVs) for offshore wind work in Taiwan.

# Why it matters

Siemens Gamesa has a long-standing Taiwanese footprint — a nacelle-assembly facility in
Taichung and turbine supply to Ørsted's [Changhua](/projects/changhua.md) project — and
CSOVs are the workhorses for turbine commissioning and O&M campaigns offshore. Locking
in two vessels from a regional owner secures Asia-Pacific marine capacity for that work,
in a segment where European owners such as
[Bernhard Schulte Offshore](/companies/bernhard-schulte-offshore.md) have led fleet
build-out. The booking lands the same day Siemens Energy announced the
[Omterra rebrand](/digests/2026-07-22-siemens-energy-siemens-gamesa-omterra-rebrand.md)
covering Siemens Gamesa.

# What the source does not say

The item available is a short excerpt. It gives no contract value, no framework
duration, no vessel names or specifications, no start date, and does not name the
Taiwanese wind farms the CSOVs will serve. No project page was created or changed for
