# Summary

Up to 180 new jobs are being created in the Scottish Highlands to support delivery
of the 3.6 GW [Dogger Bank Wind Farm](/projects/dogger-bank.md), the world's largest
offshore wind farm under construction. The roles are based at
[Maraen Port of Nigg](/companies/maraen.md) near Inverness, selected in May 2026 as
the marshalling harbour where turbine components are prepared before being shipped
offshore for the Dogger Bank B and C phases.

The positions — project managers, port operators, and mechanical and electrical
technicians — cover pre-assembly of [GE Vernova](/companies/ge-vernova-offshore-wind.md)
Haliade-X 13 MW and 14 MW components. Recruitment has been underway for several
months and the roles are expected to run through to the project's expected completion
in mid-2028. The news followed a visit to the deepwater port and designated Green
Freeport by Scottish Government Energy Minister Stephen Gethins.

Dogger Bank is a GBP 9-billion joint venture between SSE (40%),
[Equinor](/companies/equinor.md) (40%) and Vårgrønn (20%), built in three 1.2 GW
phases with 277 turbines in total. Turbine installation is ongoing at Phase B after
the Phase A campaign was completed earlier in 2026; offshore installation works at
Phase C begin in 2027. An independent impact report published in 2025 put the
project's lifetime contribution to the UK economy at GBP 6.1 billion, and once
operational the wind farm is expected to cover around six per cent of current UK
electricity demand. Nigg has supported four consecutive offshore wind campaigns since
2018, including SSE's Beatrice and Seagreen; earlier Scottish supply-chain benefit
cited includes the 2021 service operation vessel contracts with Aberdeen-based
[North Star](/companies/north-star.md), around 170 UK jobs.
