# Philippines Publishes Recalibrated GEA-5 Timeline

The Philippine **Department of Energy (DOE)** published on 29 July 2026 an
indicative timeline for the recalibration of
[GEA-5](/tenders/philippines-gea-5-offshore-wind-auction.md), the fifth
round of its Green Energy Auction Program and the country's first offshore
wind auction, covering **3.3 GW of fixed-bottom capacity**.

The round was launched with a Notice of Auction and Terms of Reference on
25 November 2025 and had bidder registration opening in March 2026, with
bidding originally due end-July to end-September 2026. The DOE **suspended
GEA-5 on 4 July 2026** to review the framework with other agencies and
industry, aligning it with infrastructure readiness, permitting,
environmental and port-related costs, grid connection and possible global
supply chain disruptions.

Under the revised schedule, the DOE and the PPA, DENR, PNOC, IEMOP,
TransCo, NGCP and ERC review port, environmental and grid-readiness
requirements through **2 September 2026**; a Department Circular setting the
recalibration policy framework is targeted for signing by **24 August** and
publication in early September; and a Supplemental Terms of Reference is due
for final approval by **2 October 2026** after public consultation. GEA-5
then resumes with an updated schedule, auction activities in Q4 2026, the
auction itself **indicatively on 1 December 2026**, and bid validation,
Notices of Award and Certificates of Award between 2 December 2026 and
30 June 2027. All dates remain indicative, and the suspension stands until
the Circular and Supplemental TOR are out.

Four projects are named as advancing through GEA-5: the **901 MW San Miguel
Bay** farm owned by
[Copenhagen Infrastructure Partners](/companies/copenhagen-infrastructure-partners.md)
and ACEN Corporation — billed as Southeast Asia's first offshore wind
project, and the subject of a late-June meeting between CIP and the
Department of Finance on milestones and financing — plus the **450 MW
Frontera Bay**, **600 MW Guimaras Strait** and **600 MW Guimaras Strait II**
projects of the Seawind Asia / Stream Invest Holdings / Triconti ECC
Renewables consortium, which secured grid connection agreements in early
2026. Awarded capacity is expected to be operating between 2028 and 2030.

This is the bundle's first Philippine entry, adding a Southeast Asian market
alongside the Asia-Pacific rounds already tracked
([South Korea 2026](/tenders/south-korea-offshore-wind-auction-2026.md)) and
CIP's other regional assets ([Fengmiao 1](/projects/fengmiao-1.md) in
Taiwan). No project pages were created for the four named farms: beyond
capacity and ownership, the article carries no siting, turbine, schedule or
contractor detail to build them on.
