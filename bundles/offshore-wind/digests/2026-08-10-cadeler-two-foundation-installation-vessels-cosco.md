# Cadeler Orders Two New Wind Foundation Installation Vessels at COSCO

[Cadeler](/companies/cadeler.md) has signed firm contracts with the
**COSCO Shipping Offshore** shipyard in **Qidong, China**, for two new
wind **foundation installation vessels**.

The order extends a newbuild programme already running at the same yard,
where Cadeler and COSCO held a
[keel-laying ceremony for Wind Apex](/digests/2026-07-07-cadeler-wind-apex-keel-laying.md)
in July 2026, a month before Cadeler
[took delivery of Wind Ace](/digests/2026-07-17-cadeler-wind-ace-delivery.md),
its eleventh installation vessel. Cadeler is currently installing
[foundations](/technology/fixed-bottom-foundations.md) at
[Hornsea 3](/projects/hornsea-3.md) with Wind Ally.

The excerpt is truncated after the opening clause, so nothing further is
asserted here: no contract value, no vessel design, lifting capacity or
dimensions, no delivery dates, no option vessels, and no named projects
or charters for the pair.
