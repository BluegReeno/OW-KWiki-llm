# Crown Estate Launches Accelerated Tender for 1.5 GW Morgan Site

The Crown Estate has launched an **accelerated tender process** to select a
new developer for the up to 1.5 GW [Morgan](/projects/morgan.md) offshore
wind project in the Irish Sea, after the previous developer discontinued
it earlier this year.

Morgan was awarded through **Offshore Wind Leasing Round 4 in 2021** and
was held by an EnBW / [JERA Nex BP](/companies/jera-nex-bp.md) joint
venture. EnBW decided in January 2026 to exit both Morgan and Mona in the
UK; JERA Nex BP took over EnBW's stake in the 1.5 GW Mona project, while
the JV discontinued Morgan and returned the lease rights to The Crown
Estate, which announced in March 2026 that it would re-tender the site.

The site comes with substantial development work already done: a
generation **Development Consent Order granted in August 2025**, a grid
connection agreement with the **National Energy System Operator (NESO)**,
and a pending decision (expected September 2026) on the transmission DCO
submitted jointly with the transmission infrastructure for the 480 MW
Morecambe offshore wind farm. The Crown Estate says the streamlined
process is designed to preserve that progress so a new developer can build
on it and maintain delivery momentum.

Developers will compete through a **transparent rising clock auction**,
bidding a **Site Exclusivity Fee** payable only once the project enters
lease — bid as a lump sum but paid over 20 years from lease start. Bids
are expected over the summer, with the auction and preferred bidder
selection later in 2026 and development rights awarded by year-end.
