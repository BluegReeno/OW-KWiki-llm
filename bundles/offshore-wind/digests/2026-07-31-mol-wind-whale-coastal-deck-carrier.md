# MOL Completes Wind Whale, Japan's First Coastal Deck Carrier for Offshore Wind Foundations

[Mitsui O.S.K. Lines (MOL)](/companies/mitsui-osk-lines.md), MOL Drybulk
and MOL Coastal Shipping held a naming and completion ceremony on 29 July
2026 in Nagasaki for **Wind Whale**, billed as Japan's first coastal deck
carrier purpose-built to transport offshore wind turbine foundation
components. The vessel was built by China's Taizhou Sanfu Ship
Engineering under a 2024 contract with MOL Drybulk.

The 12,709 dwt, 149.9-metre carrier has a flush deck letting monopiles,
towers, blades, nacelles and floating foundations be loaded directly from
the stern or sides with multi-axle transporters, and a dynamic
positioning system for direct transfers to wind turbine installation
vessels offshore — which MOL contrasts with non-self-propelled barges.
It is jointly owned by MOL and MOL Drybulk and operated by MOL Coastal
Shipping.

Its declared task is domestic logistics: carrying
[monopiles](/technology/fixed-bottom-foundations.md) made at JFE
Engineering's Kasaoka facility in Okayama Prefecture to construction
sites around the Japanese coast, supporting projects such as
[Oga-Katagami-Akita](/projects/oga-katagami-akita.md).
