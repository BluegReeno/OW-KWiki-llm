# Summary

EnBW expects to soon complete the installation of all wind turbines at the
960 MW [He Dreiht](/projects/he-dreiht.md) offshore wind farm in the German
North Sea.

# What changed in the bundle

A [He Dreiht](/projects/he-dreiht.md) project page was created. The bundle had
carried the wind farm only through the [July 2026 blade recovery
digest](/digests/2026-07-28-he-dreiht-blade-recovery.md), which explicitly left
the page open pending follow-up reporting. Nearing the end of a turbine
installation campaign is a durable construction milestone with a capacity
figure attached, unlike a single recovery operation, so the page is now
warranted — and it gives the earlier incident somewhere to sit.

It closes a run of German North Sea construction milestones next to
[Nordlicht](/projects/nordlicht.md), where the first transition piece went in
on [4 August](/digests/2026-08-04-nordlicht-i-first-transition-piece-installed.md),
and [Nordseecluster A](/digests/2026-07-22-nordseecluster-a-first-power.md),
which fed first power in July — He Dreiht being the furthest along of the
three. It is also the second turbine-installation campaign approaching
completion this month, after [Thor passed halfway in
July](/digests/2026-07-17-thor-turbine-installation-halfway.md), and the
counterpart on the turbine side to the foundation campaign closed out at
[Inch Cape](/digests/2026-08-11-inch-cape-monopile-foundations-completed.md)
the same day.

No EnBW company page was created: the excerpt supports only that EnBW owns and
is building this wind farm, which is too thin for a company concept, and EnBW
otherwise appears in the bundle only as a relinquishing party at
[Morgan](/projects/morgan.md). No `tenders` tag: no call for bids, auction
round or contract award is involved.
