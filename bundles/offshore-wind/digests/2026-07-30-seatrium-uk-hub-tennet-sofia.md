# Seatrium Starts Building UK Hub for TenneT 2 GW and Sofia Projects

Seatrium Offshore Renewable Services (ORS), the renewables arm of Singapore's
[Seatrium](/companies/seatrium.md), has started construction of a new offshore
wind facility in the UK. offshorewind.biz reports the hub is to support delivery
of the TenneT 2 GW programme — the standardised 2 GW
[HVDC](/technology/hvdc-grid-connection.md) offshore grid connections rolled out
by the Dutch-German transmission operator in the North Sea — and of the Sofia
offshore wind farm, [RWE](/companies/rwe-offshore-wind.md)'s 1.4 GW project on
Dogger Bank.

A new company page was created for Seatrium, which had no entry in the bundle
despite being a converter-platform and offshore-structures contractor of the
same class as [Aker Solutions](/companies/aker-solutions.md),
[Saipem](/companies/saipem.md) and [Subsea 7](/companies/subsea-7.md). Building
out a dedicated UK footprint for named programmes is a durable corporate fact,
and it adds to a run of 2026 supply-chain localisation moves recorded here —
[EEW's Gwangyang expansion](/digests/2026-07-07-eew-group-south-korea-plant-expansion.md),
[WIND Group's Oskarshamn cable hub](/digests/2026-07-13-wind-group-oskarshamn-baltic-hub.md)
and [BW Ideol and NGE's floating foundation plant in France](/digests/2026-07-23-bw-ideol-nge-floating-foundation-facility.md).

The excerpt is truncated after "a new offshore wind operations […]", so no
location, footprint, investment, headcount, opening date or exact function of
the facility was asserted, and no Sofia project page or TenneT page was created
on this item alone. No tender or contract award is reported, so nothing is
tagged `tenders`.
