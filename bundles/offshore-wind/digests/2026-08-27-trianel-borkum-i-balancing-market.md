# Trianel's Borkum I Offshore Wind Farm Enters German Balancing Market

[Trianel Windpark Borkum I](/projects/trianel-windpark-borkum-i.md) (TWB I), the 200 MW
North Sea wind farm operated by [Trianel](/companies/trianel.md), has prequalified around
**70 MW** of capacity for Germany's balancing energy market and began supplying
**negative secondary control reserve** in July 2026. Marketing of the balancing capacity
started on **22 July 2026**, adding a grid-stabilisation service alongside plain
electricity generation.

Negative secondary control reserve lets the transmission system operator automatically
reduce a participating plant's output; per Trianel the adjustment must be carried out
**within five minutes**. A dedicated **remote-control infrastructure** built for the wind
farm transmits TSO requests in real time and implements the required output change
automatically, on a **redundant architecture** designed to meet balancing-market
technical requirements.

Trianel says the prequalification creates an additional revenue opportunity alongside the
wind farm's existing power sales, including PPAs. Lars Becker, Head of Virtual Power
Plant at Trianel GmbH, framed it as showing that "renewable energies can offer far more
than just electricity production".

TWB I is described as Germany's first **municipal** offshore wind farm: 40 Adwen AD5-116
turbines about 45 km northwest of Borkum, in operation since 2015 and past 6.59 billion
kWh of cumulative generation.
