[RWE](/companies/rwe-offshore-wind.md) has reached a milestone in the construction of the 1.1 GW [Thor offshore wind farm](/projects/thor.md), having installed 36 of the 72 [Siemens Gamesa](/companies/siemens-gamesa.md) turbines off Denmark's west coast near Jutland — passing the halfway point of turbine installation.

Each turbine is rated at up to 15 MW, stands around 148 metres above sea level and carries 115-metre rotor blades. Installation is being carried out from the Port of Esbjerg with Fred. Olsen Windcarrier's WTIV Brave Tern. The remaining 36 turbines are due to be installed by the end of 2026, with testing and full commercial operation expected by spring 2027. Thor — a JV of RWE (51%) and Norges Bank Investment Management (49%) — will be Denmark's largest offshore wind farm and is set to be the first in the world to use carbon-reduced steel towers (36 units) alongside 120 recyclable rotor blades on 40 turbines.
