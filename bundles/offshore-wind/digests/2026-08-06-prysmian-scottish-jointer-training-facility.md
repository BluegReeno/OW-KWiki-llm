# Prysmian Buys Scottish Training Facility for Cable Jointers

Reported 6 August 2026. [Prysmian](/companies/prysmian.md), the Italian
cabling company, has acquired a training facility for high-voltage cable
jointers in Scotland.

A [Prysmian](/companies/prysmian.md) company page was created: the bundle
tracked the cable supply chain through [NKT](/companies/nkt.md),
[LS Cable & System](/companies/ls-cable-system.md) and
[Taihan Cable & Solution](/companies/taihan-cable-solution.md) without any
entry for the sector's largest cable maker, and an acquisition is a
durable corporate fact rather than news noise. What stands out is what is
being bought: not a factory or a cable-laying vessel, as in the recent
fleet and plant items ([NKT Eleonora](/digests/2026-07-21-nkt-eleonora-romania-to-norway.md),
[Taihan's second CLV](/digests/2026-07-17-taihan-second-cable-laying-vessel.md),
[LS Cable's CLV at Tersan](/digests/2026-07-06-ls-cable-clv-tersan-shipyard.md)),
but the training capacity for the scarce craft skill that terminates and
splices [high-voltage cable systems](/technology/hvdc-grid-connection.md) —
a supply-chain investment on the labour side, in a UK market whose
build-out pipeline runs through the [CfD scheme](/policy/uk-contracts-for-difference-scheme.md).

No tender or award element is involved, so nothing was tagged `tenders`.
The excerpt is truncated after the opening clause, so nothing further was
asserted: no facility name or location in Scotland, no seller, no
transaction value, no trainee capacity or course offering, no headcount,
and no projects served.
