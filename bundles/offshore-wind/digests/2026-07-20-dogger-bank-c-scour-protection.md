# Summary

[DEME Offshore](/companies/deme-offshore.md)'s fallpipe vessel (FPV) Flintstone
is about to commence rock installation work at Phase C of the
[Dogger Bank Wind Farm](/projects/dogger-bank.md) in the UK North Sea. The
scour protection campaign marks the start of seabed works at Phase C, the
final ~1.2 GW phase of the 3.6 GW project, which has not yet begun turbine
installation.

The excerpt is brief and does not give the volume of rock, the number of
foundations to be protected, or a completion schedule.
