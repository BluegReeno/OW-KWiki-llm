# Summary

The **State Government of Victoria** has launched **Australia's first
offshore wind auction**, opening a **Request for Proposal (RFP)** process
for the state's first **2 GW** of offshore wind capacity. Developers can
bid for contracts for projects off the **Gippsland coast**, with proposals
assessed on **value for money**, **deliverability** and **benefits for
local workers, businesses and communities**.

Contracts are expected to be integrated into the national **Electricity
Services Entry Mechanism (ESEM)**, presented as the pathway towards
Victoria's longer-term **9 GW** offshore wind target. The RFP stays open
until **August 2027**, with contracts expected to be awarded in **2028**.

Victoria's Minister for Energy and Resources **Jaclyn Symes** called the
auction "a giant leap towards getting Australia's first offshore wind
projects built", citing investment, jobs and replacement capacity "as coal
retires".

The tender had originally been planned for September 2025 but was
postponed over setbacks in feasibility licence approvals, the rejection of
the initial referral for the **Port of Hastings**, and global uncertainty;
in January 2026 then-Minister **Lily D'Ambrosio** set the August 2026 date
for an initial 2 GW.
