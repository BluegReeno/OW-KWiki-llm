# Summary

[Van Oord](/companies/van-oord.md) has completed the installation of all **111 monopile
foundations** at the ~1.5 GW [Baltica 2](/projects/baltica-2.md) offshore wind farm in the
Polish Baltic Sea, the 50/50 joint venture between PGE Polska Grupa Energetyczna and
[Ørsted](/companies/orsted.md).

The milestone closes a [fixed-bottom foundation](/technology/fixed-bottom-foundations.md)
campaign that started in May 2026. It lands one day after Ørsted's H1 2026 results, which
put the project at roughly 40 per cent complete with 103 of the 111 positions installed —
107 turbine foundations plus four
[offshore substation](/technology/offshore-substations.md) foundations — so the remaining
eight have now been placed. Commissioning is scheduled for the second half of 2027.

The monopiles themselves were supplied by
[Steelwind Nordenham](/companies/steelwind-nordenham.md), which
[completed its deliveries earlier in August 2026](/digests/2026-08-05-steelwind-baltica-2-monopile-deliveries.md);
the article also lists [EEW Group](/companies/eew-group.md)'s EEW SPC among the parties, but
the excerpt states no scope for it.
