# CAPE Holland and GBM Works Partner to Commercialise Vibrojet

[CAPE Holland](/companies/cape-holland.md) and
[GBM Works](/companies/gbm-works.md) have signed a **Memorandum of
Understanding** to jointly commercialise the **Vibrojet** for offshore
wind, offshoreWIND.biz reported on 13 August 2026.

# What Changed in the Bundle

Both companies were new to the bundle and now have pages: an MoU between
two named parties around a named product is a durable corporate fact
rather than news noise, even though the excerpt stops after the opening
clause. [Fixed-bottom foundations](/technology/fixed-bottom-foundations.md)
gained a **Pile Installation Methods** section, the first place in the
bundle where how a pile goes into the seabed — rather than who
fabricates, ships or protects it — is treated as a subject.

That section is where this item earns its place. The bundle's foundation
thread has so far been fabrication ([Haizea](/companies/haizea-wind-group.md)'s
[Hornsea 3 XXL monopiles](/digests/2026-08-06-haizea-hornsea-3-xxl-monopiles.md),
[Steelwind's Baltica 2 deliveries](/digests/2026-08-05-steelwind-baltica-2-monopile-deliveries.md),
[CS Wind Offshore's Nordlicht I transition pieces](/digests/2026-07-30-cs-wind-offshore-nordlicht-i-transition-pieces.md),
[Dajin's Gennaker transition pieces](/digests/2026-08-04-dajin-gennaker-transition-pieces-fabrication.md)),
installation tonnage
([Cadeler's two foundation installation vessels at COSCO](/digests/2026-08-10-cadeler-two-foundation-installation-vessels-cosco.md)),
and what protects the steel once it is down
([scour protection at Dogger Bank C](/digests/2026-07-20-dogger-bank-c-scour-protection.md),
[OEG's corrosion protection at Gennaker](/digests/2026-08-11-oeg-gennaker-corrosion-protection-contract.md)).
It arrives a day after [Cadeler bought Menck](/digests/2026-08-12-cadeler-acquires-menck.md),
the pile-driving equipment maker, for EUR 501 million: within one news
cycle, the incumbent hammer business is consolidated into a vessel owner
while two smaller specialists team up around an alternative method.

No `tenders` tag: an MoU is a cooperation agreement, not a call for
bids, an auction round or a contract award, and no client, project or
order is named.

# What the Item Does Not Say

The excerpt is truncated after "have signed a Memorandum of
Understanding (MoU) to jointly [...]", so nothing beyond the fact of the
MoU and its object was asserted: no description of the Vibrojet's
working principle, performance or claimed advantages; no statement of
which party owns or contributes which technology; no split of scope,
revenue or intellectual property; no exclusivity or duration; no
nationality, size or history of either company; no target market,
foundation type, water depth or turbine class; no test, demonstration or
qualification record; and no named projects, clients or commercial
awards.
