# Ørsted Awards Baltica 2 Helicopter Medevac Contract

[Ørsted](/companies/orsted.md), acting on behalf of the 50/50 joint venture with PGE Polska Grupa
Energetyczna, has awarded the helicopter **medical evacuation (medevac)** contract for the
[Baltica 2](/projects/baltica-2.md) offshore wind farm to
[Northern Helicopter](/companies/northern-helicopter.md).

The award comes days after [Van Oord completed the project's 111 monopile
foundations](/digests/2026-08-21-van-oord-baltica-2-monopiles-complete.md) and adds an emergency
response service to a project supply chain that already includes CSOVs from
[Bernhard Schulte Offshore](/companies/bernhard-schulte-offshore.md) and
[Pelagic Partners](/companies/pelagic-partners.md). Commissioning of the ~1.5 GW wind farm remains
scheduled for the second half of 2027.

Tagged `tenders`: this is a contract award.

Nothing beyond the article was asserted: no contract duration, value or scope beyond medevac,
no aircraft type or number, no operating base or airfield, no start date, and no separate
search-and-rescue or crew-transfer arrangement.
