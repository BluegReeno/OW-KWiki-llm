# Sumitomo Acquires Stake in Gwynt Glas Floating Wind Project

[Sumitomo Corporation](/companies/sumitomo-corporation.md) has joined the
[Gwynt Glas](/projects/gwynt-glas.md) floating offshore wind project in the Celtic Sea,
acquiring a stake in the up-to-1.5 GW scheme off Wales. The project is developed by a
joint venture between EDF Power Solutions and [ESB](/companies/esb.md), with DP Energy
as exclusive development partner; it secured a Crown Estate Round 5 site in 2025 and
submitted its [EIA Scoping Report](/digests/2026-07-01-gwynt-glas-eia-scoping-report.md)
in July 2026.

The move is a counterpoint to Sumitomo's
[exit from three Belgian offshore wind farms](/digests/2026-07-06-sumitomo-exits-belgian-offshore-wind.md)
announced in July 2026: operating fixed-bottom assets sold, an early-stage
[floating wind](/technology/floating-wind.md) development bought — the portfolio
rotation the company describes under its Medium-Term Management Plan 2026, now visible
on both sides of the ledger.

The excerpt is truncated after the opening clause, so nothing further is asserted here:
no stake size or percentage, no transaction value, no seller (whether the stake comes
from EDF Power Solutions, ESB or DP Energy), no completion date or regulatory
conditions, no change to the development partnership structure, and no schedule for
consent, turbine selection or financial close.
