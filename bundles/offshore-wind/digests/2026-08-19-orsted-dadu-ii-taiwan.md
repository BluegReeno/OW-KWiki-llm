# Summary

[Ørsted](/companies/orsted.md) is developing Dadu II, a new offshore wind
project of up to 2,002 MW off Changhua County, Taiwan, now entering the
environmental assessment process. According to Taiwan's Ministry of
Environment database, the project would cover roughly 234.6 km² of sea
area, with its nearest point about 38 km from shore, and would use
turbines in the 14 MW to 22 MW range; the final turbine number and
configuration are undetermined. The environmental assessment is at the
public consultation stage, with a public meeting held on 13 August 2026
in Changhua County.

Ørsted is also developing Dadu I off Taiwan, listed in the same database
with a potential capacity of up to 924 MW; its Environmental Impact
Statement was recently completed.
