# Van Oord Completes Nature-Inclusive Scour Protection at Hollandse Kust West VI

[Van Oord](/companies/van-oord.md) has completed the rock installation works
at the 760 MW [Hollandse Kust West VI](/projects/hollandse-kust-west-vi.md)
offshore wind farm, owned and developed by [Ecowende](/companies/ecowende.md)
(a Shell/Eneco/Chubu Electric Power JV) around 53 km
off IJmuiden in the Dutch North Sea.

Using its subsea rock installation vessels (SRIVs) Bravenes and Nordnes, Van
Oord placed 300,000 tonnes of rock to protect turbine foundations and cable
infrastructure. The campaign, started in September 2025, included
stabilisation of all cable protection systems and the installation of
eco-rock berms. The nature-inclusive design incorporates ecological bays,
habitat-forming crevices and varied rock sizes — including rocks up to 450 kg,
described as the largest ever installed by an SRIV — to create habitats for
marine life.

The wind farm, said to be the most ecological offshore wind farm yet, will
comprise 52 [Vestas](/companies/vestas.md) V236-15.0 MW turbines and
delivered first power earlier in July 2026. It is expected to be fully
operational by the end of 2026.
