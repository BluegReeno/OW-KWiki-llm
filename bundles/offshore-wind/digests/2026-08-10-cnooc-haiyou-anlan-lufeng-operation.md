# Summary

China National Offshore Oil Corporation ([CNOOC](/companies/cnooc.md)) has started operating its 16 MW **Haiyou Anlan** [floating wind](/technology/floating-wind.md) turbine, which powers the company's **Lufeng oilfield**. See [Haiyou Anlan Floating Wind Turbine (Lufeng)](/projects/haiyou-anlan-lufeng.md).

Two things make it durable rather than news noise. First the unit rating: at 16 MW this is among the largest floating turbines in operation anywhere, well above the ratings on European floating demonstrators the bundle tracks — the 3 MW [Culzean](/projects/culzean-floating-wind.md) pilot, the [TetraSpar demonstrator](/projects/tetraspar-demonstrator.md) now being decommissioned. Second the role: the output electrifies an offshore oil production facility instead of feeding a grid under a support scheme, the same off-grid application as Culzean under the UK's INTOG round, but reached here at commercial turbine scale and outside any auction framework.

No tender or award element, so nothing is tagged `tenders`.
