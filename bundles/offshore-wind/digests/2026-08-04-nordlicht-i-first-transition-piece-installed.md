# Summary

The first transition piece (TP) has been installed at
[Vattenfall](/companies/vattenfall.md)'s 980 MW
[Nordlicht I](/projects/nordlicht.md) offshore wind farm in the German North
Sea, following the installation of the first monopile at the beginning of
July 2026. Nordlicht I needs 68 monopiles and as many TPs: the monopiles are
fabricated by [EEW](/companies/eew-group.md) Special Pipe Constructions in
Rostock, the TPs by [CS Wind Offshore](/companies/cs-wind-offshore.md) in
Aalborg — whose [first batch shipped on 30 July](/digests/2026-07-30-cs-wind-offshore-nordlicht-i-transition-pieces.md)
— and [DEME Offshore](/companies/deme-offshore.md) holds the transport and
installation contract for both components at Nordlicht I and Nordlicht II.
The milestone closes the loop of the
[fixed-bottom foundation](/technology/fixed-bottom-foundations.md) chain from
fabrication through delivery to offshore installation of a complete
foundation.

The excerpt is truncated after the opening clause, so nothing further is
asserted here: no installation vessel is named, no exact installation date
beyond the 4 August report, no TP weight or dimensions, no marshalling port,
and no rate or schedule for the remaining 67 foundations.
