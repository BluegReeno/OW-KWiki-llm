# Summary

*NKT Eleonora*, the new cable-laying vessel (CLV) of Danish cable
manufacturer [NKT](/companies/nkt.md), has departed Romania for Norway,
where its construction continues at shipbuilder Vard. The excerpt notes
the vessel will be able to run on dual fuel but is truncated before the
fuels are named, and gives no delivery date, cable-capacity figures or
target projects.

The build adds to a wider wave of new cable-installation tonnage entering
the offshore wind market, alongside vessels ordered or delivered by
[LS Cable & System](/companies/ls-cable-system.md) and
[Taihan Cable & Solution](/companies/taihan-cable-solution.md).
