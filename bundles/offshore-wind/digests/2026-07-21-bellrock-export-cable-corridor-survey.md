# Summary

Uncrewed surface vessel operator XOCEAN is set to begin a geophysical survey
of a potential offshore export cable corridor for the
[Bellrock floating wind farm](/projects/bellrock.md), developed by Nadara.

The survey is an early site-investigation step toward defining Bellrock's
export cable route and grid connection, and follows Nadara's appointment of
[Aker Solutions](/companies/aker-solutions.md) earlier in July 2026 to carry
out the project's electrical infrastructure studies. The available excerpt is
truncated and gives no survey dates, corridor coordinates, landfall option or
vessel details.
