# Summary

All wind turbines have now been installed at EnBW's 960 MW
[He Dreiht](/projects/he-dreiht.md) offshore wind farm in the German North Sea.
The turbines are supplied by [Vestas](/companies/vestas.md).

# What changed in the bundle

The [He Dreiht](/projects/he-dreiht.md) project page was updated: the status
entry that recorded EnBW's 11 August statement that installation would finish
"shortly" is now followed by the completed milestone, and the page's headline
description and citation were moved to this article. This is the confirmation
of [yesterday's digest](/digests/2026-08-11-he-dreiht-turbine-installation-nearly-complete.md),
so it belongs on the same page rather than a new one.

He Dreiht is now the furthest advanced of the German North Sea construction
projects tracked here, ahead of [Nordlicht](/projects/nordlicht.md), which
installed its first transition piece on
[4 August](/digests/2026-08-04-nordlicht-i-first-transition-piece-installed.md),
and [Nordseecluster A](/projects/nordseecluster.md), which reached
[first power in July](/digests/2026-07-22-nordseecluster-a-first-power.md).

No [Cadeler](/companies/cadeler.md) page update: the article is filed under
Cadeler by the publisher, but the excerpt does not state its role at He Dreiht,
so nothing was added to the company page. No EnBW company page was created, for
the same reason as yesterday — the reporting supports only ownership of this one
wind farm. No `tenders` tag: no call for bids, auction round or contract award
is involved.
