# Sumitomo Corporation Exits Three Belgian Offshore Wind Projects

[Sumitomo Corporation](/companies/sumitomo-corporation.md) is divesting its equity stakes in three Belgian offshore wind farms as part of a portfolio rotation strategy under its Medium-Term Management Plan 2026. Two transactions are already complete: the sale of its 39.02% stake in [Nobelwind](/projects/nobelwind.md) and its 30% stake in [Northwester 2](/projects/northwester-2.md), both to existing partner [JERA Nex BP](/companies/jera-nex-bp.md) (previously reported [here](/digests/2026-07-03-jera-nex-bp-northwester-2-nobelwind.md)). A third divestment is progressing: Sumitomo's stake in the 216 MW [Northwind](/projects/northwind.md) offshore wind farm is being sold to Publiwind, a consortium of Belgian investment firms and public institutions (Ethias, SOCOFE, SFPIM), with closing expected during fiscal year 2026 subject to regulatory approval.
