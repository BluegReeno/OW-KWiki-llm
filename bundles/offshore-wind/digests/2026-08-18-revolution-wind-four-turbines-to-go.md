# Four Turbines to Go at Revolution Wind

The **704 MW Revolution Wind** offshore wind farm off Rhode Island, a joint
venture of [Ørsted](/companies/orsted.md) and
[Skyborn Renewables](/companies/skyborn.md) supplying Rhode Island and
Connecticut, is reported to be **more than 95 per cent complete**, with four
turbines still to be installed.

A [project page](/projects/revolution-wind.md) was created: a build entering the
last handful of turbine lifts is a durable construction milestone, and Revolution
Wind had no entry of its own despite being named on the
[Ørsted](/companies/orsted.md), [Skyborn](/companies/skyborn.md) and
[US BOEM leasing process](/policy/us-boem-leasing-process.md) pages. It is the
second US build to report progress here this month, after
[Coastal Virginia Offshore Wind at 81%](/projects/coastal-virginia-offshore-wind.md),
and notable because both were under the December 2025 stop-work orders that
courts later ruled unlawful — the American construction thread has moved from
litigation back to installation.

No `tenders` tag: a construction progress report is not a call for bids, auction
round or award. Nothing beyond the excerpt was asserted: no turbine supplier,
model or total count, no installation vessel or contractor, no remaining
commissioning steps, and no first-power or full-operations date.
