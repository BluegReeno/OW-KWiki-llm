# Vattenfall and Project Enki Explore Wind-Powered Offshore Data Centres

[Vattenfall](/companies/vattenfall.md) and Project Enki, a Dutch AI
infrastructure company, have joined forces to explore the potential of
offshore data centres powered by offshore wind.

The item available is an excerpt only: it names the two partners and the
subject of the collaboration, but gives no location, capacity, site,
timeline, commercial terms or partnership structure. Nothing indicates a
committed project, an offtake contract or a tender.

Recorded as digest-only. Exploratory co-operation between a developer and a
prospective large electricity consumer is not yet a durable fact for
[Vattenfall](/companies/vattenfall.md)'s company page, and no data centre or
Project Enki concept page is warranted on this evidence. It is worth
watching as a variant of the "offshore energy conversion at sea" theme
alongside [Power-to-X](/technology/power-to-x.md): both route offshore wind
output into an on-site industrial load rather than a shore grid connection.
