# Summary

Poland's [Port of Darłowo](/companies/port-of-darlowo.md) has completed two
infrastructure projects with a combined value of more than PLN 174 million
(EUR 40.3 million), around PLN 144 million (EUR 33.3 million) of which came
from Poland's National Recovery and Resilience Plan (KPO).

The first, run by the Maritime Office in Szczecin (approx. PLN 130 million /
EUR 30.1 million), reconstructed the 356.5-metre western breakwater and added
a 100-metre spur to calm wave activity in the outer harbour, plus new lighting
and a navigation beacon. Contractor DORACO signed in February 2025 and
finished in July 2026, using some 980 t of reinforcing steel, 8,000 m³ of
hydraulic concrete, 16,000 m² of sheet piling and 189,000 t of rock.

The second, led by the City of Darłowo (PLN 44+ million / EUR 10.2 million),
targets the offshore wind service base role directly: nearly 400 metres of the
Southern, Gdynia and Szczecin quays were modernised and connected to a new
229-metre Transhipment Quay dimensioned for specialised offshore wind service
vessels. The upgraded port adds Polish O&M capacity alongside projects such as
[Baltic Power](/projects/baltic-power.md) and
[Bałtyk 2 & 3](/projects/baltyk-2-3.md).
