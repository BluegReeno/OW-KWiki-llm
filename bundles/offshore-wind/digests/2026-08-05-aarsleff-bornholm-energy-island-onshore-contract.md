# Summary

Danish construction company **Per Aarsleff A/S**
([Aarsleff](/companies/aarsleff.md)) has been awarded a **design-and-build
contract worth EUR 495 million** by the Danish transmission system operator
[Energinet](/companies/energinet.md) for the onshore facilities of
[Bornholm Energy Island](/projects/bornholm-energy-island.md).

Three pages were created. Bornholm Energy Island is Denmark's Baltic
energy-island project and had no entry at all, despite the bundle tracking the
Danish market through the [2.8 GW CfD tender](/tenders/denmark-2-8gw-cfd-tender-2026.md)
and [Thor](/projects/thor.md); Energinet is the Danish TSO and the counterpart
to [50Hertz](/companies/50hertz.md), added a day earlier for the German Baltic;
and Aarsleff is the bundle's first pure civil-construction contractor. A
contract award of this size is the most durable class of fact this bundle
tracks, so all three are tagged `tenders`.

What stands out is where the money lands: nearly half a billion euros of an
offshore wind grid-connection project going to a **civil construction
contractor** rather than a converter OEM or a marine installer. The onshore
works of an energy island — sites, buildings, groundworks around the converter
and substation plant — are a contract class of their own, the landward end of
the [HVDC grid-connection](/technology/hvdc-grid-connection.md) chain that this
bundle has so far followed mostly offshore (converter platforms such as
[BalWin5](/projects/balwin5.md), cables, cable-laying vessels).

The item is a short excerpt truncated after the opening clause, so nothing
further was asserted: no scope breakdown of the onshore facilities, no
construction start or completion dates, no site on Bornholm, no procurement
route or competing bidders, no converter or cable suppliers, no project
capacity, and no commissioning date for the energy island. The project's
planned Danish-German hybrid character is noted on the project page as
background, not as a claim from this item.
