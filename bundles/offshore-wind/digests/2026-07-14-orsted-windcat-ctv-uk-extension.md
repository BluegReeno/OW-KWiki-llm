# Summary

Crew transfer vessel (CTV) operator Windcat and [Ørsted](/companies/orsted.md) have signed contracts for the long-term supply of CTV services to support Ørsted's offshore wind operations in the UK. The deal extends an existing chartering relationship between the two companies.
