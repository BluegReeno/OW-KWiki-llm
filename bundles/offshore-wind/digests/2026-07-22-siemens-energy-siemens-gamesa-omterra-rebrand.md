# Summary

Siemens Energy has announced plans to transition to a new corporate brand,
**Omterra**. The change also applies to [Siemens Gamesa](/companies/siemens-gamesa.md),
the wind turbine manufacturer Siemens Energy has wholly owned since 2023 and one of
the two dominant offshore turbine suppliers in Europe alongside
[Vestas](/companies/vestas.md), competing globally with
[GE Vernova](/companies/ge-vernova-offshore-wind.md).

# Why it matters

Siemens Gamesa turbines are installed across a large share of the European offshore
fleet — including [Hornsea 2](/projects/hornsea-2.md),
[Saint-Brieuc](/projects/saint-brieuc.md), [Changhua](/projects/changhua.md) and,
currently under installation, [Thor](/projects/thor.md) — so a name change to the
supplier is a naming change that will propagate through supply contracts, service
agreements and project documentation across the industry.

# What the source does not say

The item available is a short excerpt. It gives no rebrand timetable or effective
date, no indication of whether turbine model designations (the `SG` prefixes) change,
and no statement on corporate structure, ownership, plants or existing orders. Nothing
here indicates a change of ownership or a restructuring — only a corporate brand
transition. The [Siemens Gamesa](/companies/siemens-gamesa.md) page was updated with
the announcement but kept at its current filename until the new brand is in actual
use; no separate Omterra company page was created, to avoid a duplicate concept for
the same entity.
