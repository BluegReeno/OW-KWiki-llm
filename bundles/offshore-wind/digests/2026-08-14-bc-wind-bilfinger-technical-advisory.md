# Summary

Industrial services provider [Bilfinger](/companies/bilfinger.md) has been selected by [Ocean Winds](/companies/ocean-winds.md) to deliver technical advisory services during the execution phase of the [BC-Wind](/projects/bc-wind.md) offshore wind project in the Polish Baltic Sea.

BC-Wind comprises 26 turbines for a total planned capacity of up to 390 MW, on a site roughly 23 km from the Polish coastline, and is expected to supply electricity to almost half a million households. Ocean Winds — the 50-50 joint venture of EDP Renewables and ENGIE — reached financial close at the end of 2025, onshore construction of the substation and export cable route (P&Q, with cables from Tele-Fonika Kable) started in 2026, and first power is expected in 2028.

Bilfinger's scope is supervision rather than execution: oversight of turbine installation, offshore structures and export cable installation, and compliance with technical requirements, permits and applicable regulations, alongside quality, safety and operational efficiency across the project lifecycle. The company had already supported Ocean Winds on the onshore part of the works, which its managers framed as the basis for the offshore appointment.
