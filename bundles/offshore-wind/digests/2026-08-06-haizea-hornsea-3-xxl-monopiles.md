# Summary

[Haizea Wind Group](/companies/haizea-wind-group.md) has delivered what it says
are the largest monopiles ever manufactured in Southern Europe, for
[Hornsea 3](/projects/hornsea-3.md) in the UK North Sea. Produced at the
company's facility at the **Port of Bilbao**, they measure over 105 metres in
length, 11 metres in diameter, and weigh more than 2,300 tonnes each. Haizea
says they are the largest structures ever manufactured in the Iberian
Peninsula, rank among the largest monopiles produced in Europe, and that it is
currently the only manufacturer in Spain able to build monopiles of this size.

Load-out took place at the Port of Bilbao with logistics support from Grúas
Aguado: [Cadeler](/companies/cadeler.md) was responsible for the load-out and
load-in operations, while Mammoet moved the monopiles within the port on
self-propelled modular transporters. The project's XXL monopiles come from
Haizea and [Dajin Heavy Industry](/companies/dajin-heavy-industry.md), and are
installed by Cadeler's vessel *Wind Ally*, which began offshore work in May
2026.

Hornsea 3 — 2.9 GW, around 120 km off Norfolk, owned by
[Ørsted](/companies/orsted.md) and Apollo-managed funds, joined in May 2026 by
Mubadala Investment Company — will comprise 197
[Siemens Gamesa](/companies/siemens-gamesa.md) SG 14-236 DD turbines of 14 MW
(excluding power boost) and is expected operational in 2027, when it would
become the world's largest single offshore wind farm.

# Why it matters

A second European [fixed-bottom](/technology/fixed-bottom-foundations.md)
supply node at XXL scale: the Iberian Peninsula now builds monopiles in the
same class as northern-European yards ([EEW Group](/companies/eew-group.md),
[Steelwind Nordenham](/companies/steelwind-nordenham.md)), feeding the UK's
largest project in parallel with Chinese fabrication. The 11 m diameter and
2,300 t unit weight also mark where [turbine scaling](/technology/turbine-scaling.md)
has pushed foundation and load-out logistics.

Not reported: monopile count per supplier, contract value or award date,
transport vessel for this consignment, and the remaining delivery schedule.
