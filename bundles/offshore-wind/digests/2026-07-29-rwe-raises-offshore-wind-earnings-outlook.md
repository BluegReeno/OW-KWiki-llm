# Summary

[RWE](/companies/rwe-offshore-wind.md) has raised the full-year 2026 earnings outlook for its
Offshore Wind division, and lifted its 2027 outlook as well, after first-half 2026 results that
the company characterised as exceeding market expectations.

The available excerpt is truncated: it does not give the revised earnings guidance figures, the
previous guidance, the half-year adjusted EBITDA or EBIT for the division, the drivers behind the
beat (wind resource, availability, commissioning of new capacity, or realised prices), or group-level
guidance. Only the direction of the revision — upward, for both 2026 and 2027, for the Offshore Wind
division specifically — is supported by the item.

# Why It Matters

RWE describes itself as the world's number 2 offshore wind operator, with more than 4 GW under
construction in Europe and a 10 GW by 2030 target. An upward revision of divisional earnings
guidance is a signal about the operating performance of that fleet in a year when several RWE
assets are ramping up — Sofia commissioning, [Thor](/projects/thor.md) turbine installation past
the halfway mark, and [Nordseecluster](/projects/nordseecluster.md) A feeding first power into the
German grid in July 2026 — and while the group's US pipeline remains suspended.
