# Summary

Nadara has appointed [Aker Solutions](/companies/aker-solutions.md) to
undertake electrical infrastructure studies for the
[Bellrock](/projects/bellrock.md) floating offshore wind project. The award
extends Aker Solutions' offshore wind engineering scope beyond its
established HVDC substructure work into electrical infrastructure design for
a floating development. The excerpt does not disclose Bellrock's capacity,
location, or the value or schedule of the studies.
