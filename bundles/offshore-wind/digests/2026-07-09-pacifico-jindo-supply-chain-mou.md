# Pacifico Energy Korea Signs Local Supply Chain MOU for 3.2 GW Jindo Cluster

[Pacifico Energy Korea](/companies/pacifico-energy-korea.md), a U.S.-based
offshore wind developer, has signed a memorandum of understanding with three
suppliers in the Gwangyang Bay Area of South Korea's Jeonnam-Gwangju region
to establish a tailored local supply chain for its 3.2 GW
[Jindo Offshore Wind Project Cluster](/projects/jindo.md).

Under the MOU, Pacifico Energy Korea leads overall investment and project
management; [EEW KHPC](/companies/eew-group.md) supplies foundation
components including pin piles; KMC Line provides marine logistics,
installation support and O&M vessels; and CS Energy supplies key foundation
components. The parties also agreed to develop local talent and support job
creation in Jindo County and the surrounding region.
