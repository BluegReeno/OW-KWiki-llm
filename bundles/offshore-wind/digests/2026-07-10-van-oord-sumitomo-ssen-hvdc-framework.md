# Van Oord and Sumitomo Electric Sign HVDC Subsea Cable Framework with SSEN Transmission

SSEN Transmission has signed a long-term framework agreement with Sumitomo
Electric Industries, Ltd. and [Van Oord](/companies/van-oord.md) Offshore Wind
UK Ltd for the engineering, supply, transport and installation of
[HVDC subsea cable systems](/technology/hvdc-grid-connection.md), supporting
expansion and reinforcement of the electricity transmission network in the
north of Scotland.

The framework is intended to deliver future strategic subsea transmission
reinforcements, including the proposed **Shetland 2 HVDC link** — the anchor
project supporting Sumitomo Electric's investment in its new cable
manufacturing facility at Nigg in the Highlands, which is establishing
domestic HVDC cable manufacturing capability in the UK for the first time.
It combines Sumitomo Electric's cable manufacturing with Van Oord's offshore
transport and installation capabilities, and marks Van Oord's entry into the
offshore HVDC market.

Note: **Sumitomo Electric Industries** (the cable manufacturer in this deal)
is a distinct entity from the trading conglomerate
[Sumitomo Corporation](/companies/sumitomo-corporation.md).
