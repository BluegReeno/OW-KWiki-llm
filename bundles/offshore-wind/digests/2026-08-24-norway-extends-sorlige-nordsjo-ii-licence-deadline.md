# Norway Extends Licence Application Deadline for First Commercial Offshore Wind Farm

The Norwegian Ministry of Energy has granted [Ventyr](/companies/ventyr.md) —
a consortium of [JERA Nex BP](/companies/jera-nex-bp.md) and Ingka Investments
— a twelve-month extension of the deadline to submit a licence application for
[Sørlige Nordsjø II](/projects/sorlige-nordsjo-ii.md), Norway's first
commercial offshore wind farm. The new deadline for the up to 1.5 GW project is
**15 October 2027**.

Ventyr requested the extension in June 2026, saying it had experienced a number
of unexpected challenges in the development and planning of the project as well
as the difficult macroeconomic environment in offshore wind, which had slowed
some development activities. The extra time will be used in part to assess an
alternative landfall and [grid connection](/technology/hvdc-grid-connection.md)
option, referred to as **G 2.0**, involving new infrastructure near the existing
Lista substation at Alcoa's aluminium plant, in parallel with construction of the
Sørlige Nordsjø II converter station. Ventyr called it potentially attractive
with significant societal and environmental benefits, but in need of further
studies.

The consortium won the zone in March 2024 with a bid of NOK 1.15/kWh
(EUR 0.099/kWh) in Norway's first offshore wind auction, which offered 1.5 GW of
fixed-bottom capacity. Separately, Norway's first
[floating wind](/technology/floating-wind.md) tender, for Utsira Nord, drew two
applicants — a Deep Wind Offshore/EDF Renewables joint venture and an
[Equinor](/companies/equinor.md)/Vårgrønn consortium — both of which the
Ministry said in December 2025 would be awarded a project area.
