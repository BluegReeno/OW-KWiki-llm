# Windward Offshore Takes Delivery of Third CSOV from Vard

[Windward Offshore](/companies/windward-offshore.md) has taken delivery of
**Windward Munich**, the third of four commissioning service operation vessels
(CSOVs) that [Vard](/companies/vard.md) is building for the German company. The
vessel was handed over from Vard's shipyard in Vung Tau, Vietnam.

Windward describes Windward Munich as an advanced hybrid CSOV combining high
levels of comfort with energy-efficient technology, including an electrically
driven, integrated motion-compensated gangway and crane, a helideck, and
accommodation for up to 120 people on board. It has secured a first charter
assignment due to commence very soon, with no further details revealed.

The delivery follows Windward Paris at the beginning of 2026 and Windward
Athens in October 2025. One vessel remains under construction, Windward
Hamburg; once complete, the four-vessel series will form the backbone of
Windward Offshore's integrated service offering to the offshore wind sector.

It is the second CSOV fleet milestone in the bundle this month, after
[Bernhard Schulte Offshore's Windea Clarke](/digests/2026-07-03-bso-windea-clarke-csov-delivery.md)
and [Siemens Gamesa's charter of two Marco Polo Marine CSOVs for Taiwan](/digests/2026-07-22-siemens-gamesa-marco-polo-marine-csovs-taiwan.md).
