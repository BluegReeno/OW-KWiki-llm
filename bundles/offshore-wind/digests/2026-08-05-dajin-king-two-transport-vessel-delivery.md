# Summary

[Dajin Heavy Industry](/companies/dajin-heavy-industry.md) has delivered
**King Two**, the second vessel in its self-developed King-class series of
offshore wind transport vessels.

That makes the Chinese fabricator a shipowner as well as a supplier of
foundation components: it is currently producing the 63 transition pieces
for [Skyborn Renewables](/companies/skyborn.md)' 976.5 MW
[Gennaker](/projects/gennaker.md) wind farm in the German Baltic Sea, and a
dedicated transport fleet is the logistics half of moving
[fixed-bottom foundations](/technology/fixed-bottom-foundations.md) from
Chinese yards to European sites.

The item is a short excerpt cut off after the opening clause, so nothing
beyond the delivery was asserted: no vessel dimensions, deck area or cargo
capacity, no builder or yard, no delivery date or current deployment of the
first King-class vessel, no first assignment or charter for King Two, and no
link to any specific project cargo.

No tender or award element is involved, so nothing was tagged `tenders`.
This continues the 2026 vessel-fleet build-out thread alongside
[MOL's Wind Whale coastal deck carrier](/digests/2026-07-31-mol-wind-whale-coastal-deck-carrier.md),
[Windward Munich](/digests/2026-07-31-windward-offshore-third-csov-windward-munich.md)
and [Cadeler's Wind Ace](/digests/2026-07-17-cadeler-wind-ace-delivery.md),
this time on the component-transport rather than the service-vessel side.
