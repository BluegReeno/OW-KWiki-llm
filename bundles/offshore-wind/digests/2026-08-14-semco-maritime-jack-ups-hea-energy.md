# Semco Maritime Sells Offshore Wind Jack-Ups to HEA Energy

[HEA Energy](/companies/hea-energy.md) has acquired the jack-up vessels
**Thor** and **Wind Lift 1** from Denmark's
[Semco Maritime](/companies/semco-maritime.md), moving two offshore wind
jack-ups from a services group to a vessel owner.

A change of ownership of named vessels is a durable asset fact rather than
news noise, so both parties — neither of which had a page in this bundle,
though Semco Maritime appeared in July's
[Wind Multiplikator rebrand digest](/digests/2026-07-23-wind-multiplikator-semco-maritime-rebrand.md)
— now have one. It is the second vessel-side ownership move recorded this
month after [Cadeler's acquisition of Menck](/digests/2026-08-12-cadeler-acquires-menck.md)
and [Taihan's purchase of the CLV Skandi Connector from DOF](/digests/2026-08-13-taihan-skandi-connector-yeonggwang-nakwol.md),
each shifting existing tonnage or equipment between owners rather than
adding newbuild capacity.

No `tenders` tag: an asset sale between two parties is not a call for
bids, auction round or contract award.

The excerpt is truncated after the opening clause, so nothing further was
asserted: no purchase price or transaction structure, no closing date, no
vessel specifications, build years or classification, no existing charters
or projects attached to the vessels, no statement of HEA Energy's
nationality, ownership or existing fleet, and no indication of what Semco
Maritime retains of its marine assets or why it sold.
