# Gasunie Launches Tender for 10-Year Offshore Wind PPA in Netherlands

Dutch gas infrastructure company Gasunie has launched a public tender seeking a long-term power
purchase agreement from a new-build offshore wind farm, as part of its target to source 40 per
cent of its energy from carbon-free sources by 2030
([tender page](/tenders/gasunie-offshore-wind-ppa-2026.md)).

Gasunie is procuring **40 MW of capacity for ten years**, starting either 1 January 2029 or
1 January 2030. The electricity must be physically delivered together with the associated and
traceable Guarantees of Origin, according to the contract notice published in the Supplement to
the Official Journal of the European Union on 24 July 2026. The contract will be awarded on the
basis of the **lowest all-in price** for electricity and GoOs. Bids close at 11:00 CET on
30 September 2026, with requests for additional information accepted until 4 September 2026.

Two optional capacity increases are attached. The first would add 10 MW from 2029/2030 until
2036 — potentially to the end of the contract — subject to a final investment decision on the
EemsEnergyTerminal project in the first half of 2027; the Eemshaven LNG import terminal has run
since September 2022 under an original five-year contract, and in June 2026 Gasunie and partner
Vopak took a conditional investment decision on extending it beyond 2027. The second option
allows up to 10 MW more during the contract term by mutual agreement.
