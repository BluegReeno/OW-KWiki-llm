# What Happened

Germany's **Federal Maritime and Hydrographic Agency (BSH)** has launched a
**tender for preliminary geophysical surveys** for offshore wind.

The BSH is the authority that carries out **site pre-investigation** for German
offshore wind areas — the seabed, geophysical and environmental survey work whose
results are handed to bidders and, ultimately, to the developer awarded the site
under the [German Wind Energy at Sea Act (WindSeeG)](/policy/german-offshore-wind-act-windseeg.md).
A survey tender is therefore an upstream, services-side procurement rather than an
auction of sea area, but it sits on the same pipeline: sites cannot be tendered to
developers before the state-run investigation that de-risks them is done.

# Why It Matters

German site pre-investigation has been under scrutiny since the **August 2025**
zero-bid round for the **N-10.1 and N-10.2** sites, whose re-tender was pushed to
**2027** and whose area design the BSH proposed revising in **June 2026**. Survey
procurement is the practical machinery behind that timetable, and it runs in
parallel with the **August 2026 BMWE draft amendment** to the WindSeeG that would
introduce bilateral CfDs and 2-4.8 GW annual auctions.

# What Is Not Known

The excerpt names no survey areas or sites, no contract value, lot structure,
scope beyond "preliminary geophysical", no bid deadline or award date, no vessel
or equipment requirements, and no bidders. None of that was assumed here.
