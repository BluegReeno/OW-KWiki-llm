# SK oceanplant to Build the Jacket Foundation for BalWin5

South Korean offshore wind foundation manufacturer
[SK oceanplant](/companies/sk-oceanplant.md) has secured a contract from
[Seatrium](/companies/seatrium.md) to manufacture and supply the jacket foundation
and piles for TenneT's [BalWin5](/projects/balwin5.md) HVDC offshore converter
platform in the German North Sea.

The contract is valued at approximately USD 110.7 million (around EUR 96 million)
and covers construction engineering, procurement, fabrication and load-out of one
jacket and 20 piles for the offshore substation. The jacket substructure will weigh
approximately 15,000 tonnes. SK oceanplant says the award is the first time a Korean
supplier has secured a complete jacket supply agreement for a large-scale
[HVDC](/technology/hvdc-grid-connection.md) offshore substation connected to the
European grid — Korean firms had previously supplied only certain structural
components. The company entered the European market through the DolWin4 and BorWin4
projects, contracted in April 2025 with key components delivered in May 2026.

BalWin5 is a 2.2 GW-class converter platform within TenneT's next-generation German
offshore transmission programme, planned for commissioning in 2033: an offshore
converter platform in the North Sea and an onshore converter in Bremen, linked by
around 210 km of subsea cable — passing under the island of Langeoog — and around
115 km of land cable. The GE Vernova-Seatrium consortium won the platform contract in
December 2025, with GE Vernova's Electrification Systems supplying the HVDC
technology and both converter stations, and Seatrium designing, building,
transporting and installing the offshore platform.

The article does not name the fabrication yard, delivery or load-out dates, the
installation contractor, or the wind farms BalWin5 will connect, so those are not
recorded in the bundle.
