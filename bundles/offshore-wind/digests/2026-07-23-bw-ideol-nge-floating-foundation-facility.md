# Summary

[BW Ideol](/companies/bw-ideol.md) and French construction group NGE have signed an exclusive partnership agreement to build a [floating offshore wind](/technology/floating-wind.md) foundation manufacturing facility in Southern France. The deal pairs BW Ideol's Damping Pool floater design with NGE's construction capability to add local serial-fabrication capacity for floating foundations.
