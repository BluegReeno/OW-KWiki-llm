# EOLOS Partners with Enviros to Support Offshore Wind Metocean Campaigns in Vietnam

Spain-based floating LiDAR specialist [EOLOS](/companies/eolos.md) has
partnered with Enviros, a Vietnamese marine services company, to support
offshore wind metocean measurement campaigns in Vietnam.

Floating LiDAR buoys measure wind resource and metocean conditions at
prospective offshore wind sites, the earliest measurable stage of project
development and the input to yield and design assumptions for both
[fixed-bottom](/technology/fixed-bottom-foundations.md) and
[floating](/technology/floating-wind.md) sites. Pairing a European measurement
technology provider with a local marine services operator is the usual route
into an emerging market, and it puts a second kind of Vietnamese offshore wind
capability on the map alongside the fabrication work of
[PTSC M&C](/companies/ptsc-mc.md), which built the offshore substation for
Taiwan's [Fengmiao 1](/projects/fengmiao-1.md).
