# TEPCO Starts EIA for New Choshi Offshore Wind Farm as Japan Re-Tenders Mitsubishi's Round 1 Sites

[TEPCO Renewable Power](/companies/tepco.md) has begun the environmental
assessment process for a planned offshore wind farm of up to **420 MW** off
**Choshi, Chiba Prefecture**. It submitted the planning-stage environmental
consideration document on **20 August 2026**, and Chiba Prefecture opened the
public consultation on **21 August**, running until **25 September 2026**.

The [project](/projects/choshi-offshore-wind.md) would use fixed-bottom
turbines across roughly **4,000 hectares**, with up to **27 units rated between
15 MW and 20 MW**; the environmental assessment area also partly covers Asahi.
Construction is planned for **2031 or later** and operation for **2032 or
later**, subject to subsequent development and permitting stages.

Choshi is one of the three areas of
[Japan's Round 1 fixed-bottom auction](/tenders/japan-round-1-fixed-bottom-auction.md),
won in December 2021 by a Mitsubishi Corporation-led consortium for a 390.6 MW
wind farm — beating a bid by [Ørsted](/companies/orsted.md) and TEPCO.
Mitsubishi decided in August 2025 not to proceed with Choshi or its two Akita
projects, citing material changes in the macroeconomic environment, and the
government has since moved to re-tender all three areas: Choshi,
Noshiro–Mitane–Oga and Yurihonjo.
