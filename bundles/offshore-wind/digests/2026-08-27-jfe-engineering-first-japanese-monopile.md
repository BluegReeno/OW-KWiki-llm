# JFE Engineering Rolls Out First 'Made in Japan' Monopile

Tokyo-based [JFE Engineering Corporation](/companies/jfe-engineering.md) has
produced what it says is Japan's first domestically manufactured monopile for
an offshore wind farm. The monopile is destined for the 315 MW
[Oga–Katagami–Akita](/projects/oga-katagami-akita.md) project, owned by a
consortium of [JERA Nex BP](/companies/jera-nex-bp.md), Electric Power
Development (J-POWER), Tohoku Electric Power and Itochu.

JFE Engineering was awarded the contract for foundation manufacturing and
transportation services in December 2025 by Kajima Corporation, which the
developer had contracted in October 2025 for manufacturing, procurement,
transportation and installation of the turbine foundations.

The monopiles are being made at JFE's Kasaoka Monopile Plant, built in March
2024 and, per the company, the only monopile manufacturing base in Japan. They
have a maximum diameter of approximately 11 metres, a length of approximately
80 metres and a weight of approximately 2,000 tonnes.

The project's 21 [Vestas](/companies/vestas.md) V236-15.0 MW turbines are due
to begin arriving in 2026, with installation and commissioning planned for
2027 and full commissioning in 2028. It is among the first fixed-bottom
developments in Japan's general sea areas to advance through the public tender
process — see [fixed-bottom foundations](/technology/fixed-bottom-foundations.md).
