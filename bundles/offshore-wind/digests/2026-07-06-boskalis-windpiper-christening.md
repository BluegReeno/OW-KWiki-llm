# Boskalis Christens Windpiper, Industry's Largest Subsea Rock Installation Vessel

[Boskalis](/companies/boskalis.md) has christened **Windpiper**, its new
subsea rock installation (SRI) vessel, described by the company as the
largest in the industry. The vessel was converted from a purchased hull
over a year and a half (acquisition announced January 2025), giving it a
45,500-tonne rock-carrying capacity, 227 m length, 40 m beam, over
31,000 kW of installed power, and more than a hundred single-occupancy
cabins. Christened on 3 July 2026, Windpiper is now ready for its first
rock installation project, in the Baltic Sea.
