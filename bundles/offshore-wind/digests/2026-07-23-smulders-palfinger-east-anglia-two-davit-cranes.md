# Smulders Taps Palfinger for East Anglia Two Davit Cranes

Palfinger has secured a contract from fabricator Smulders to supply 64 davit
cranes for ScottishPower's East Anglia Two offshore wind farm in the UK. The
cranes are deck equipment for the project's foundations/topsides being built by
Smulders. Routine supply-chain award, digest-only.
