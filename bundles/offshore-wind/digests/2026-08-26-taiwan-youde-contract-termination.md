# Summary

Taiwan's **Energy Administration** is processing the termination of the
administrative contract for the **700 MW Youde** offshore wind project,
developed by [Shinfox Energy](/companies/shinfox-energy.md). Per Taiwan's
Central News Agency, the agency confirmed on **24 August 2026** that the
developer had not completed payment of the outstanding **performance bond**:
half was paid at contract signature, the remaining half was due one year
later, payment was requested on **18 May** and **12 June 2026**, and a final
deadline in August passed unpaid. The developer has submitted a response
within the termination process.

Youde was one of five projects awarded capacity in the **Phase 3.2**
selection of [Taiwan's Offshore Wind Block Development
programme](/tenders/taiwan-block-development-phase-3.md) in August 2024,
alongside the 800 MW Formosa 6 (Synera Renewable Energy), the 600 MW
Fengmiao 2 ([Copenhagen Infrastructure Partners](/companies/copenhagen-infrastructure-partners.md)),
the 360 MW Formosa 3 / Haiding 1 (Corio Generation and TotalEnergies) and
the 240 MW Deshuai (Enervest). MOEA has already cancelled the development
rights of the latter two for failing to meet requirements, so Youde's
termination would leave three of the five 3.2 winners out.

Vacated sites now have a route back to market: after a **2 July 2026**
offshore wind meeting the Energy Administration said terminated-contract
sites would be included in the **expansion capacity** scope, and on
**23 July 2026** MOEA published the Phase 3 administrative contract
framework with separate contracts for allocated and expansion projects. The
round covers **3.6 GW** of original allocation plus those sites, with
selection targeted for completion by 2026.

Shinfox's affiliate **Foxwell Energy** is building the 300 MW Taipower
Offshore Wind Project Phase II won from Taipower in 2020, and Shinfox itself
was **delisted from the Taiwan Stock Exchange in June 2026** after its net
worth turned negative.
