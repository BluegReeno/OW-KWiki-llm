# Bernhard Schulte, Pelagic Partners Win Baltica 2 CSOV Contracts

Elektrownia Wiatrowa Baltica – 2, the project company developing the 1.5 GW
[Baltica 2](/projects/baltica-2.md) offshore wind farm in the Polish Baltic Sea, has awarded
Commissioning Service Operation Vessel (CSOV) contracts to
[Bernhard Schulte Offshore](/companies/bernhard-schulte-offshore.md) and
[Pelagic Partners](/companies/pelagic-partners.md).

This is a contract award, so the digest and the pages it touches are tagged `tenders`. It marks
the move from foundation supply — [Steelwind Nordenham](/companies/steelwind-nordenham.md)
completed its monopile deliveries the day before — to the offshore construction and commissioning
phase, where accommodation and walk-to-work vessels carry the technicians. Baltica 2 is the PGE /
[Ørsted](/companies/orsted.md) joint venture that follows [Baltic Power](/projects/baltic-power.md)
into the Polish market, and the award adds a second Baltic CSOV/SOV demand signal after
[50Hertz's SOV market consultation](/digests/2026-08-05-50hertz-sov-market-consultation-baltic.md).

The excerpt is truncated after the opening clause, so nothing further is asserted: no vessel names,
no charter durations or start dates, no contract values, no scope split between the two owners, and
no indication of whether the vessels come from existing fleets or newbuilds.
