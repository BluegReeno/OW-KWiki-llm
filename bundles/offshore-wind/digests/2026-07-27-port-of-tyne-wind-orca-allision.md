# Port of Tyne Launches Major Clean-Up After Wind Orca Allision

Port of Tyne, on England's north-east coast, has launched what it
describes as a major recovery and environmental clean-up operation after
an allision involving the offshore wind installation vessel **Wind
Orca**. The contact damaged a crane at the port and left one person
injured.
