# Seaway7's Alfa Lift to Start Inch Cape Jacket Pin Piles, Cadeler's Wind Mover to Install Turbines in November

**Seaway7**, the renewables arm of [Subsea 7](/companies/subsea-7.md), is
set to begin installing the **pin piles** for the jacket
[foundations](/technology/fixed-bottom-foundations.md) at the **1.1 GW**
[Inch Cape](/projects/inch-cape.md) offshore wind farm off Scotland's
Angus coast, using the heavy transport and installation vessel
**Alfa Lift**. [Cadeler](/companies/cadeler.md)'s installation vessel
**Wind Mover** is due to begin **turbine installation in November 2026**.

The jacket campaign follows the completion of Inch Cape's monopile
foundation installation earlier in August 2026 — 54 monopiles and their
transition pieces, with 18 jackets remaining. The November turbine start
is consistent with the project's stated target of first power in late
2026 and full commercial operation in 2027.

Only the headline and a short excerpt were available: no start date for
the pin pile campaign, no number of piles per jacket, no jacket
fabricator, no contract values, and no turbine installation duration or
completion date are asserted here.
