# 'World's Most Powerful' Subsea Trencher Nears First Testing

[NKT](/companies/nkt.md) reported in mid-August 2026 that its new subsea
trencher, the **NKT T-3600**, is approaching final assembly and entering
its first round of testing. The Danish cable manufacturer and installer
called it "this subsea giant" and framed the milestone as the result of an
intensive programme with its two partners on the tool.

- **Capability**: 3,600 horsepower, burial of cables to depths of up to
  5.5 metres below the seabed, with both jetting and cutting functions so
  it can operate across a wide range of soil conditions — see
  [subsea cable installation and burial](/technology/subsea-cable-installation.md).
- **Builder**: UK original equipment supplier [Osbit](/companies/osbit.md)
  is in charge of the design and construction of the trencher and of its
  launch and recovery system (LARS).
- **Operator**: [Helix Robotics Solutions](/companies/helix-robotics-solutions.md),
  the UK robotics division of Helix Energy Solutions Group, holds a
  four-year frame agreement signed in July 2025 covering installation,
  offshore operations, project engineering and maintenance, with the tool
  deployed from a Helix-provided support vessel.
- **Timeline**: investment announced June 2025; frame agreement July 2025;
  arrival at the UK's Port of Blyth in February 2026 for fit-out and
  commissioning; first testing August 2026.

The article states no delivery date, no contract values, no support vessel
name and no first project for the trencher.
