# NextOcean to Equip Esvagt SOV with WavePredictor Technology

[NextOcean](/companies/nextocean.md) has secured a contract to equip
[ESVAGT](/companies/esvagt.md)'s service operation vessel **Esvagt Njord**
with its **WavePredictor** vessel-motion prediction technology, supporting
O&M operations at the [Dudgeon](/projects/dudgeon.md) offshore wind farm in
the UK, where the vessel is deployed. The motion radar and accompanying
software were scheduled for installation in **August 2026**.

WavePredictor uses motion radar and software to give crews real-time
predictions of vessel movements minutes ahead, so personnel transfers can be
judged in challenging sea states — safer transfers and, potentially, more
workable days. NextOcean CEO **Karel Roozen** framed it as backing the dozens
of daily go/no-go calls made on an SOV with verified predictions rather than
feel.

Esvagt Njord was first deployed on Dudgeon in **autumn 2016**, shortly after
delivery to ESVAGT; the initial contract carried options for up to five
additional years and was **extended in 2021**. Dudgeon itself is **402 MW**,
32 km off North Norfolk, with **67 [Siemens Gamesa](/companies/siemens-gamesa.md)
6 MW turbines**, fully commissioned in late 2017 and owned by Dudgeon Offshore
Wind Limited — a JV of [Equinor](/companies/equinor.md), Masdar and China
Resources (Holdings) — with Equinor as operator.

Three pages were created: NextOcean and ESVAGT on the equipment-supplier
threshold already used for [MacGregor](/companies/macgregor.md),
[Stillstrom](/companies/stillstrom.md) and [Menck](/companies/menck.md) — a
named supplier, a named contract, a named vessel — and Dudgeon because a
402 MW operating UK wind farm had appeared here only as a passing mention on
the Equinor page. ESVAGT in particular had been referenced twice before
(the Stillstrom charging trial, this article) with no entry of its own.
`tenders` tag applied throughout: a contract award is squarely in scope.

What stands out is where this sits against the bundle's other O&M items:
[MacGregor's DNV floating-to-floating gangway certification](/companies/macgregor.md)
extends *where* a transfer can be made, while WavePredictor extends *when* —
both attack the same weather-window constraint that governs every SOV entry
here ([North Star](/companies/north-star.md),
[Windward Offshore](/companies/windward-offshore.md),
[Bernhard Schulte Offshore](/companies/bernhard-schulte-offshore.md)).

No Masdar or China Resources pages: both appear only as Dudgeon co-owners
with no scope of their own. Nothing beyond the article was asserted: no
contract value, duration or scope beyond the single vessel, no radar
specification, range or accuracy figures, no quantified gain in workable
days, no prior WavePredictor installations or references, no ESVAGT fleet
details beyond Esvagt Njord, no end date for the Dudgeon charter, and no
Dudgeon water depth, foundation type, grid connection or output figures.
