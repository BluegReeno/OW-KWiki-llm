# Marco Polo Marine Reports Growth as Offshore Wind CSOV Pipeline Expands

[Marco Polo Marine](/companies/marco-polo-marine.md), the Singapore-based marine
logistics and shipyard group whose subsidiary PKR Offshore charters commissioning
service operation vessels to the offshore wind industry, reported higher revenue and
gross profit for the first nine months of its financial year, with an expanding
offshore wind CSOV pipeline given as the frame for the result.
