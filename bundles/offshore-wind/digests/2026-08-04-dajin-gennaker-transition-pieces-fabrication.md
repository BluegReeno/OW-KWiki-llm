# Summary

[Dajin Heavy Industry](/companies/dajin-heavy-industry.md) has started
fabrication of 63 transition pieces (TPs) for the 976.5 MW
[Gennaker](/projects/gennaker.md) offshore wind farm, the German Baltic Sea
project developed by [Skyborn Renewables](/companies/skyborn.md). One TP per
foundation implies 63 foundation positions at the site, and the start of
fabrication marks the point where the
[fixed-bottom foundation](/technology/fixed-bottom-foundations.md) supply
chain begins feeding the project — the same stage
[CS Wind Offshore](/companies/cs-wind-offshore.md) reached for
[Nordlicht I](/projects/nordlicht.md) before its
[first TP shipment on 30 July 2026](/digests/2026-07-30-cs-wind-offshore-nordlicht-i-transition-pieces.md).

The item is notable for the supplier as much as the milestone: a Chinese
fabricator is producing the full TP set for a large German project.

The excerpt is truncated after the opening clause, so nothing further is
asserted: no contract value or award date, no fabrication yard, no TP
dimensions or weights, no delivery or shipping schedule, no monopile or
turbine supplier, no transport and installation contractor, and no offshore
construction or commissioning dates.
