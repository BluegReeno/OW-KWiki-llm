# OWC, KLEM and OEG Form Korean Offshore Wind BoP O&M Pact

Renewable energy consultancy OWC, Korea Leading Energy Management (KLEM) and
OEG have entered into a tripartite memorandum of understanding to collaborate
on offshore wind balance of plant (BoP) operations and maintenance (O&M)
services in South Korea.

Under the framework, KLEM is expected to act as the main contractor for BoP
O&M services, with OWC providing technical assurance and asset management
services, and OEG providing marine, subsea and offshore operational services.
The trio aims to offer offshore wind operators a single-team, integrated
approach to BoP O&M as more Korean projects enter the operational phase.
OWC CEO William Cleverly framed the pact against South Korea's target of
25 GW of offshore wind by 2035.

Early-stage MoU between service providers, with no specific project or
contract value attached, so digest-only — no durable company or project page.
Relates to the wider build-out of a Korean offshore wind
[supply chain](/companies/pacifico-energy-korea.md).
