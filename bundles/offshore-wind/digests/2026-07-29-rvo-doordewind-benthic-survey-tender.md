# Summary

The Dutch Ministry of Economic Affairs has launched a tender for a benthic
survey of the [Doordewind offshore wind zone](/projects/doordewind.md),
commissioned by the Netherlands Enterprise Agency (RVO). The survey will collect
factual benthic data to verify the findings of a desktop reef detection study —
whose results are expected in July 2026 — and establish a baseline of the
benthic environment across the area.

Per the contract notice in the Official Journal of the EU (OJEU/TED), the work
supports preparation of site data for the future offshore wind farms planned in
the zone, part of a Dutch offshore wind roadmap currently targeting 23 GW of
installed capacity.

Terms: estimated value **EUR 700,000 excluding VAT**, bids due **1 October
2026**, work running **10 December 2026 to 31 December 2027**, with an option to
extend to 1 May 2028 if unforeseen external circumstances cause delay.

Doordewind — one of three zones designated by the Dutch government in 2022 — lies
about 77 km off the northern Dutch coast, covers roughly 580 km², is planned for
4 GW across two 2 GW sites, and will be the first zone to connect to Eemshaven.
