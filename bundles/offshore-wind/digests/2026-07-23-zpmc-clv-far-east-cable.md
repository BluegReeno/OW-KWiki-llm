# ZPMC Starts Building Multipurpose CLV for Far East Cable

Chinese heavy-equipment and shipbuilding group ZPMC has started
construction of a new large-capacity multipurpose cable-laying vessel
(CLV) for Chinese cable manufacturer Far East Cable.

The excerpt is truncated and gives no vessel specifications (cable
carousel capacity, dimensions), delivery schedule, or target projects.
Neither ZPMC nor Far East Cable currently has a concept page in this
bundle, and a CLV newbuild construction start — while part of the wider
build-out of offshore cable-installation capacity also seen at
[NKT](/companies/nkt.md) (NKT Eleonora),
[Taihan Cable & Solution](/companies/taihan-cable-solution.md)
(Skandi Connector) and
[LS Cable & System](/companies/ls-cable-system.md) — is not on its own a
durable fact warranting a new company page. Recorded here as context for
the growing cable-vessel fleet serving offshore wind.
