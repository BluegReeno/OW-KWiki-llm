# Gasunie 10-Year Offshore Wind PPA Tender (2026)

On 24 July 2026, Dutch gas infrastructure company Gasunie launched a public tender seeking a
long-term power purchase agreement (PPA) from a **new-build offshore wind farm**. The contract
notice was published the same day in the Supplement to the Official Journal of the European
Union. The procurement supports Gasunie's target of sourcing 40 per cent of its energy from
carbon-free sources by 2030.

This is an *offtake* tender — a buyer procuring offshore wind power — rather than a seabed or
capacity auction such as the [Dutch government's own offshore wind tenders](/projects/hollandse-kust-west-vi.md).

## Terms

* **Volume**: 40 MW of capacity from a new offshore wind project.
* **Term**: ten years, starting either 1 January 2029 or 1 January 2030.
* **Delivery**: electricity must be physically delivered together with the associated and
  traceable Guarantees of Origin (GoOs).
* **Award criterion**: lowest all-in price for electricity and GoOs.
* **Bid deadline**: 11:00 CET on 30 September 2026; requests for additional information
  accepted until 4 September 2026.

## Optional capacity increases

Two options sit on top of the base 40 MW:

1. **+10 MW from 2029/2030 until 2036**, potentially extending to the end of the contract,
   conditional on a final investment decision on the **EemsEnergyTerminal** project in the
   first half of 2027. The Eemshaven LNG import terminal has been operational since September
   2022 and was originally contracted for five years; in June 2026 Gasunie and partner Vopak
   took a conditional investment decision (cFID) on extending its operations beyond 2027.
2. **Up to +10 MW during the contract term** by mutual agreement, to support Gasunie's
   carbon-free energy objectives.

## Context

Corporate and industrial PPAs are an increasingly common route to market for European offshore
wind alongside state support schemes such as the
[UK Contracts for Difference](/policy/uk-contracts-for-difference-scheme.md) — see also
[Good Energy's PPA for Ørsted's UK offshore wind farms](/digests/2026-07-21-good-energy-orsted-ppa.md).
For Gasunie, a gas transmission and infrastructure operator, the PPA also ties offshore wind
directly to energy infrastructure assets, adjacent to
[Power-to-X](/technology/power-to-x.md) style industrial offtake.
