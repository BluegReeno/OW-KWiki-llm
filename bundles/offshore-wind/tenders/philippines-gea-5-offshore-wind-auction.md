# Overview

**GEA-5** is the fifth round of the Philippine **Green Energy Auction
Program**, run by the **Department of Energy (DOE)**, and the country's
**first auction for offshore wind**. It procures **3.3 GW of fixed-bottom**
offshore wind capacity, with the awarded projects expected to enter
operation between **2028 and 2030**.

The DOE issued the Notice of Auction and Terms of Reference on
**25 November 2025**, formally launching the round. A registration period
for potential bidders opened in **March 2026**, with qualification running
to early July, bidding from end-July to end-September 2026, and provisional
winning bidders due in September.

## Suspension and recalibration

On **4 July 2026** the DOE **suspended GEA-5** to review the auction
framework together with other government agencies and industry
stakeholders, so that the round would better reflect:

* infrastructure readiness,
* permitting requirements,
* environmental and port-related costs,
* grid connection considerations,
* potential global supply chain disruptions.

The suspension remains in effect until the Department Circular and
Supplemental Terms of Reference are released.

## Revised indicative timeline (published 29 July 2026)

| Date | Step |
|---|---|
| through 2 September 2026 | Inter-agency review and validation of port, environmental and grid-readiness requirements (DOE with PPA, DENR, PNOC, IEMOP, TransCo, NGCP, ERC) |
| by 24 August 2026 | Department Circular setting the recalibration policy framework targeted for signing |
| early September 2026 | Publication of the Department Circular |
| by 2 October 2026 | Supplemental Terms of Reference finalised after public consultation |
| after Supplemental TOR | GEA-5 resumes; updated auction schedule and revised instructions to qualified bidders |
| Q4 2026 | Auction activities, including publication of qualified bidders |
| 1 December 2026 | Auction (indicative) |
| 2 December 2026 - 30 June 2027 | Bid validation, Notices of Award, post-auction evaluation, award of Certificates of Award |

The DOE stressed that **all dates remain indicative** and may change with
the outcome of inter-agency coordination, legal review and approvals.

# Projects advancing through GEA-5

| Project | Capacity | Owner / developer |
|---|---|---|
| San Miguel Bay | 901 MW | [Copenhagen Infrastructure Partners](/companies/copenhagen-infrastructure-partners.md) and ACEN Corporation |
| Frontera Bay | 450 MW | Seawind Asia / Stream Invest Holdings / Triconti ECC Renewables consortium |
| Guimaras Strait | 600 MW | same consortium |
| Guimaras Strait II | 600 MW | same consortium |

The consortium's three projects (1.65 GW combined) secured **grid connection
agreements** in early 2026. CIP met the Philippine Department of Finance at
the end of June 2026 to discuss project milestones, financing arrangements
and auction preparations for San Miguel Bay, described as Southeast Asia's
first offshore wind project.
