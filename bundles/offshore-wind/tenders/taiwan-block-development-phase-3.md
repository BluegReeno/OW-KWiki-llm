# Overview

Taiwan allocates offshore wind capacity through the **Offshore Wind Block
Development** programme, administered by the **Ministry of Economic Affairs
(MOEA)** and its **Energy Administration**. Successful developers sign an
**administrative contract** with the Energy Administration, which carries
obligations including a **performance bond** — half payable on signature,
half one year later.

# Phase 3.2 selection (August 2024)

Five projects, **2.7 GW combined**, were awarded capacity. [Ørsted](/companies/orsted.md)
was notably not on the list.

| Project | Capacity | Developer | Status as of August 2026 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Formosa 6 | 800 MW | Synera Renewable Energy | Allocated |
| Youde | 700 MW | [Shinfox Energy](/companies/shinfox-energy.md) | Contract termination being processed |
| Fengmiao 2 | 600 MW | [Copenhagen Infrastructure Partners](/companies/copenhagen-infrastructure-partners.md) | Allocated |
| Formosa 3 / Haiding 1 | 360 MW | Corio Generation and TotalEnergies | Development rights cancelled |
| Deshuai | 240 MW | Enervest | Development rights cancelled |

All five developers submitted their contract applications in **May 2025**.
MOEA subsequently **cancelled the development rights** for Formosa 3 and
Deshuai for failing to meet requirements.

## Youde contract termination (2026)

The Energy Administration confirmed on **24 August 2026** that it is
processing termination of the Youde administrative contract because the
developer had not completed payment of the outstanding **50 per cent of the
performance bond**, due one year after signature. Payment was requested on
**18 May** and **12 June 2026**, a final deadline in **August 2026** passed
unpaid, and the termination process then began; the developer has submitted
a response within that process.

# The 2026 round and expansion capacity

Taiwan has since written vacated or terminated sites into its offshore wind
policy framework:

* After an offshore wind meeting on **2 July 2026**, the Energy
  Administration said sites whose contracts were terminated would be
  **included in the scope of expansion capacity**, with changes to the
  Phase 3 selection mechanism and administrative contracts being finalised.
* On **23 July 2026**, MOEA published the **Phase 3 administrative contract
  framework**, establishing separate contracts for **allocated** and
  **expansion** projects and setting out the rights, obligations and
  liability arrangements applying to developers.
* The round covers an original allocation of **3.6 GW** plus the
  terminated-contract sites as expansion capacity. MOEA said developers
  "with proven track records and contract fulfilment capabilities" would be
  selected, targeting **completion of the selection by 2026**.
