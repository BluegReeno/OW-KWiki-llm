# Overview

Türkiye will hold its **first offshore wind tender** under the
**Renewable Energy Resource Area (YEKA)** framework in the **first quarter
of 2027**. Energy and Natural Resources Minister **Alparslan Bayraktar**
said on 18 August 2026 that the tender will be **announced in September
2026**, ahead of the **COP31** UN Climate Change Conference in Antalya in
November 2026, and invited domestic and foreign investors to take part.

The minister stated a national target of **5,000 MW of offshore wind
capacity by 2035**, and said he believes it will be reached before then.

# Candidate areas

Four candidate offshore wind areas in northwestern Türkiye were formally
announced in **May 2026**, following ministry work to identify suitable
development zones:

* Saros Bay
* Gökçeada
* Bozcaada
* Edremit

The government has said detailed work on the areas would continue before
the tender.

# Draft tender rules (June 2026)

Draft specifications for the first large-scale tender were published in
**June 2026**; the consultation period closed on **17 August 2026** after
stakeholders and investors were invited to comment to the Ministry of
Energy and Natural Resources.

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Volume | 1,000 MW of grid connection capacity |
| Concession | 49-year YEKA usage and concession period |
| Mechanism | Reverse auction |
| Price corridor | USD 0.07-0.11 per kWh |
| Pre-licence phase | 3 years for site investigations, measurements and permitting |
| Construction phase | 3 years following the generation licence |
| Withdrawal rights | Developer may withdraw if wind assessments show a capacity factor below 40%, or seabed investigations identify significant technical difficulties |
