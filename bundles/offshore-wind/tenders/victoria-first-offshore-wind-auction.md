# Overview

The **State Government of Victoria** launched **Australia's first offshore
wind auction** on **25 August 2026**, opening a **Request for Proposal
(RFP)** process for the state's first **2 GW** of offshore wind capacity.
Developers can now bid for contracts for projects off the **Gippsland
coast**.

Announcing the launch, Victoria's Minister for Energy and Resources
**Jaclyn Symes** framed the auction as a step towards "getting Australia's
first offshore wind projects built", attracting investment and delivering
replacement capacity "as coal retires".

# Key Facts

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Organizer | State Government of Victoria |
| Country | Australia |
| Volume | 2 GW (initial tranche) |
| Zone | Off the Gippsland coast |
| Mechanism | Request for Proposal (RFP), contracts awarded to selected bidders |
| Award criteria | Value for money; deliverability; benefits for local workers, businesses and communities |
| RFP open until | August 2027 |
| Contract award | Expected 2028 |
| Longer-term target | 9 GW of offshore wind capacity in Victoria |

Contracts from the auction are expected to be integrated into the national
**Electricity Services Entry Mechanism (ESEM)**, described as the pathway
towards Victoria's longer-term **9 GW** offshore wind target.

# Timeline

* **September 2025** — Victoria's planned first offshore wind tender was
  postponed. The stated reasons were setbacks in the approval of
  feasibility licences, the rejection of the initial referral for the
  **Port of Hastings**, and global uncertainty.
* **January 2026** — then-Minister for Energy and Resources **Lily
  D'Ambrosio** announced the tender would open in **August 2026** for an
  initial 2 GW.
* **25 August 2026** — RFP launched.
* **August 2027** — RFP closes.
* **2028** — contracts expected to be awarded.
