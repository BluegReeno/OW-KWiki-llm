# Belgium's Princess Elisabeth Zone Site 1 Tender (PEZ 1)

PEZ 1 is the tender for the first of three offshore wind sites in Belgium's Princess Elisabeth
Zone (PEZ), the country's second offshore wind zone. The first site will accommodate around
700 MW of capacity; the two further sites still to be tendered are each planned at 1,400 MW,
for a zone total of up to 3.5 GW. All three will connect to the Princess Elisabeth energy
island (MOG II) — described as the world's first artificial energy island — being built by
Belgian TSO Elia some 45 km off the Belgian coast.

## Cancellation of the original tender (July 2025)

The original PEZ 1 tender was cancelled in July 2025 after the Belgian government identified
legal and regulatory uncertainties around the support mechanism, state aid approval, the
delivery schedule for the energy island, and the required onshore grid reinforcements.

## New regulatory framework (July 2026)

On 24 July 2026 offshoreWIND.biz reported that the federal government had approved a new
regulatory framework for relaunching the tender. Key changes:

* **Support mechanism**: a single two-sided [Contract for Difference](/policy/uk-contracts-for-difference-scheme.md)-style
  CfD, with the lowest strike price bid winning the tender.
* **Strike price cap removed.**
* **Construction period** extended from four to five years maximum.
* **Citizen participation** no longer mandatory, but allowed on a voluntary basis.
* **NZIA alignment**: binding prequalification criteria under the EU's Net-Zero Industry Act
  covering cybersecurity, sustainability, responsible business conduct and supply chain
  resilience. At least 75% of the project's wind turbines cannot be manufactured or assembled
  in China, with further limits on Chinese-made critical turbine components and permanent
  magnets.

Federal Energy Minister Mathieu Bihet presented the framework on 18 July 2026, framing the
one-year delay as the price of "a tender that holds up legally, gives investors confidence,
and protects the consumer".

## Grid and schedule

A separate royal decree approved by the Council of Ministers sets **1 October 2031** as the
latest date for the first phase of the Princess Elisabeth energy island to enter service.
The offshore grid infrastructure and the PEZ 1 tender will now progress in parallel rather
than sequentially, to avoid further delays.

Before the auction can be relaunched, the revised rules must be reviewed by the Belgian
Council of State and notified to the European Commission under EU state aid rules — a
sequence consistent with the [EU offshore wind strategy](/policy/eu-offshore-wind-strategy.md)
and NZIA framework.

Belgium's first offshore wind zone, off Zeebrugge, hosts operating farms such as
[Northwester 2](/projects/northwester-2.md), [Nobelwind](/projects/nobelwind.md) and
[Northwind](/projects/northwind.md).
