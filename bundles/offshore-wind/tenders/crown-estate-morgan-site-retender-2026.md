# Overview

On **27 July 2026** The Crown Estate launched an **accelerated tender
process** to select a new developer for the up to **1.5 GW
[Morgan](/projects/morgan.md)** offshore wind project in the Irish Sea.
The site was awarded in **Offshore Wind Leasing Round 4 (2021)** to an
EnBW / [JERA Nex BP](/companies/jera-nex-bp.md) joint venture; after EnBW
decided to exit Morgan and Mona in January 2026, the JV discontinued
Morgan and handed the lease rights back. The Crown Estate signalled the
re-tender in March 2026.

What is on offer is not a bare seabed site but a **consented project**:
Morgan holds a generation Development Consent Order granted in August 2025
and a grid connection agreement with the National Energy System Operator
(NESO), with a decision on the transmission DCO (submitted jointly with
transmission infrastructure for the 480 MW Morecambe offshore wind farm)
expected in September 2026. The Crown Estate has framed the streamlined
process around preserving that progress so the incoming developer can
maintain delivery momentum.

Unlike the UK CfD rounds
([AR7](/tenders/uk-cfd-allocation-round-7.md)), this is a **seabed/development-rights
tender**, closer in nature to a leasing round such as
[ScotWind](/tenders/scotwind-leasing-round.md); a winner would still need
to secure revenue support separately.
