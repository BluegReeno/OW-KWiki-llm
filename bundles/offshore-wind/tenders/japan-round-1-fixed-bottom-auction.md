# Japan Round 1 Fixed-Bottom Offshore Wind Auction (and 2026 Re-Tender)

Japan's first public auction round for fixed-bottom offshore wind in the
country's general sea areas, decided in December 2021. Consortia led by
Mitsubishi Corporation won all three areas on offer, with GE turbines proposed
across the sites.

## The three Round 1 areas
- **Choshi**, Chiba Prefecture — 390.6 MW as awarded
- **Noshiro–Mitane–Oga**, Akita Prefecture
- **Yurihonjo**, Akita Prefecture

Other bidders included a joint bid by [Ørsted](/companies/orsted.md) and
[TEPCO](/companies/tepco.md) for the Choshi area, which lost.

## Collapse and re-tender
In **August 2025** Mitsubishi announced it would not proceed with any of the
three wind farms, after a review of the business plans "due to material changes
in the macroeconomic environment". Following the withdrawals, the Japanese
government moved to **re-tender the three Round 1 areas**.

Ahead of the re-tender, [TEPCO Renewable Power](/companies/tepco.md) filed a
planning-stage environmental consideration document in August 2026 for a new,
up to 420 MW project on the [Choshi area](/projects/choshi-offshore-wind.md).

Later Japanese rounds have produced projects such as
[Oga–Katagami–Akita](/projects/oga-katagami-akita.md).
