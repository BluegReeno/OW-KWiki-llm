# Tenders

* [France's first offshore wind tender (CRE AO1)](french-ao1.md) - 3,000 MW across five zones launched in 2011; four zones awarded in 2012, seeding Saint-Nazaire, Saint-Brieuc, Fécamp and Courseulles-sur-Mer.
* [Dunkerque offshore wind tender (CRE AO3)](french-ao3-dunkerque.md) - 600 MW French tender, won by EDF Renouvelables/Innogy(RWE)/Enbridge in 2019 at €44/MWh.
* [Centre Manche 1 offshore wind tender (CRE AO4)](french-ao4-centre-manche.md) - ~1000 MW French tender off Normandy, won by EDF Renouvelables/Maple Power in 2023.
* [Sud Atlantique offshore wind tenders (Oléron — AO7, AO9, AO10)](french-ao9-sud-atlantique.md) - Troubled zone: AO7 failed with zero bids in 2025; folded into the pending AO10 mega-tender, no winner yet.
* [UK CfD Allocation Round 6 (AR6)](uk-cfd-allocation-round-6.md) - UK offshore wind rebounded to ~5.3 GW in September 2024 after AR5's zero-bid failure.
* [UK CfD Allocation Round 7 (AR7)](uk-cfd-allocation-round-7.md) - Record 8.4 GW of UK offshore wind awarded in January 2026, led by RWE with 6.9 GW.
* [ScotWind leasing round](scotwind-leasing-round.md) - Crown Estate Scotland seabed leasing round, January 2022, ~24.8 GW awarded across 17 projects.
* [South Korea Offshore Wind Auction (2026)](south-korea-offshore-wind-auction-2026.md) - Five projects (~1,786 MW combined) selected; Copenhagen Infrastructure Partners won 1 GW across two of them.
* [Belgium's Princess Elisabeth Zone Site 1 Tender (PEZ 1)](belgium-princess-elisabeth-zone-pez-1.md) - ~700 MW first site of Belgium's 3.5 GW Princess Elisabeth Zone; cancelled in 2025, relaunched under a new two-sided CfD framework approved in July 2026.
* [Gasunie 10-Year Offshore Wind PPA Tender (2026)](gasunie-offshore-wind-ppa-2026.md) - Dutch gas infrastructure operator's public tender for a ten-year, 40 MW PPA from a new-build offshore wind farm; lowest all-in price wins, bids close 30 September 2026.
* [Crown Estate Morgan Site Re-Tender (2026)](crown-estate-morgan-site-retender-2026.md) - Accelerated tender for a new developer of the consented, up to 1.5 GW Morgan site in the Irish Sea; rising clock auction on a Site Exclusivity Fee, award by end of 2026.
* [Philippines Green Energy Auction 5 (GEA-5) — Offshore Wind](philippines-gea-5-offshore-wind-auction.md) - The country's first offshore wind auction, 3.3 GW fixed-bottom; suspended in July 2026 for recalibration, bidding now indicatively targeted for 1 December 2026.
* [Denmark's 2.8 GW CfD Offshore Wind Tender (2025-2026)](denmark-2-8gw-cfd-tender-2026.md) - Three sites under a redesigned two-sided CfD framework; Vattenfall won North Sea I Mid (DKK 504/MWh) and Hesselø (DKK 542/MWh) in August 2026, Nordsøen Syd still open until October 2028.
* [Türkiye's First Offshore Wind Tender (YEKA)](turkiye-first-yeka-offshore-wind-tender.md) - 1,000 MW of grid connection capacity on a 49-year concession, reverse auction with a USD 0.07-0.11/kWh corridor; announcement September 2026, tender Q1 2027.
* [Victoria's First Offshore Wind Auction (Australia)](victoria-first-offshore-wind-auction.md) - Australia's first offshore wind auction: RFP for an initial 2 GW off the Gippsland coast launched 25 August 2026, open until August 2027, contracts expected in 2028.
* [Japan Round 1 Fixed-Bottom Offshore Wind Auction (and 2026 Re-Tender)](japan-round-1-fixed-bottom-auction.md) - Japan's first fixed-bottom auction, swept by Mitsubishi-led consortia in December 2021 across Choshi, Noshiro-Mitane-Oga and Yurihonjo; all three dropped in August 2025 and now being re-tendered.
* [Taiwan Offshore Wind Block Development — Phase 3.2 and the 2026 Selection Round](taiwan-block-development-phase-3.md) - 2.7 GW allocated to five projects in August 2024; Formosa 3 and Deshuai lost their rights and the 700 MW Youde contract entered termination in August 2026, with vacated sites folded into the expansion capacity of a 3.6 GW round due to be selected in 2026.
* [Lithuania's First Offshore Wind Tender (2023)](lithuania-first-offshore-wind-tender.md) - 700 MW Baltic Sea site awarded to an Ignitis Renewables/Ocean Winds JV, seeding the Curonian Nord offshore wind farm.
