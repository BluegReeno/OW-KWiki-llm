# Vietnam Power Development Plan VIII (PDP8)

Vietnam's Power Development Plan VIII is the national plan that lists the
power generation projects the country intends to build. For offshore wind, a
project's inclusion in the plan's project list is the gateway step developers
seek before a scheme can advance.

## Capacity trajectory

According to the **Global Wind Energy Council (GWEC)**, under the **revised**
Power Development Plan VIII Vietnam is targeting:

* between **6 GW and 17 GW** of offshore wind capacity by the **2030-2035**
  period;
* between **113 GW and 139 GW** by **2050**.

## Projects seeking inclusion

* [Can Gio 2](/projects/can-gio-2.md) — a proposed 6 GW development off Ho Chi
  Minh City by a [Xuan Cau Holdings](/companies/xuan-cau-holdings.md) /
  [Trung Nam](/companies/trung-nam.md) joint venture, being proposed for
  inclusion in the revised PDP8 project list; its feasibility study was
  reviewed by the city's Standing People's Committee on 20 August 2026.
