# Overview

Romania has no offshore wind farms yet. Its framework rests on **Law No. 121/2024**
on offshore wind energy, adopted in April 2024, which set out the regime for
developing wind farms in the Romanian sector of the **Black Sea** but could not be
applied until the state designated the sea areas eligible for concession. That
designation arrived in July 2026.

# Identification of Concessionable Perimeters (2025-2026)

The Ministry of Energy launched a call in May 2025 to identify potential offshore
wind areas, and commissioned a study to delimit them. The delimitation weighed:

- wind potential in the Romanian sector of the Black Sea,
- geotechnical characteristics of the marine environment,
- environmental impact and biodiversity,
- grid connection options,
- compatibility with other uses of the maritime space — navigation, fishing,
  extraction, and military areas.

On **22 July 2026** the Ministry published, for public consultation, a list of
**six offshore wind perimeters** that may be concessioned, together with the
government decision approving the list. The Ministry frames this as the essential
step for starting investments, operationalising Law No. 121/2024, and conditioning
the launch of **competitive concession procedures**.

# The Six Areas — Phased Development

The six perimeters are estimated to accommodate a combined **11.5 GW** of installed
capacity, split into three phases reflecting short-, medium- and long-term
opportunity:

| Phase | Areas | Capacity | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 3 | **3.1 GW** (one ~1.9 GW site, two 600 MW sites) | First to be put out to tender; meets the target of Romania's Energy Strategy 2025-2035 |
| 2 | 2 | **2.6 GW** (1.8 GW + 800 MW) | Medium term |
| 3 | 1 | **6 GW** | Suitable for a mix of fixed-bottom and floating turbines |

The phase-1 total of 3.1 GW is what the government plans to have installed by
**2035**. The phase-3 perimeter is the first official indication that
[floating wind](/technology/floating-wind.md) is expected to play a role in deeper
Romanian waters, alongside
[fixed-bottom foundations](/technology/fixed-bottom-foundations.md) nearer shore.

# Caveats

The Ministry stressed that the sites ultimately put out to tender remain subject to
the **concession, authorisation and environmental assessment procedures** required
by law — the published list is a planning instrument in public consultation, not a
final set of tender lots. No tender timetable, concession duration or support
mechanism has been published with the list.
