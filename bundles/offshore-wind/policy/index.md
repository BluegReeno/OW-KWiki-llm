# Policy

* [EU Offshore Renewable Energy Strategy](eu-offshore-wind-strategy.md) - European Commission strategy setting EU-wide offshore wind targets of 60 GW by 2030 and 300 GW by 2050
* [UK Contracts for Difference (CfD) Scheme](uk-contracts-for-difference-scheme.md) - The UK's auction mechanism guaranteeing renewable generators a strike price, underpinning successive offshore wind allocation rounds — and the record 17.7% share of UK electricity offshore wind reached in 2025
* [French Programmation Pluriannuelle de l'Énergie (PPE)](french-energy-policy-ppe.md) - France's multi-year energy programming law setting the national offshore wind capacity trajectory and tender calendar
* [US BOEM Offshore Wind Leasing Process](us-boem-leasing-process.md) - The federal process run by the Bureau of Ocean Energy Management for leasing and permitting offshore wind on the Outer Continental Shelf, and the 2026 series of lease termination settlements unwinding it
* [Swedish Offshore Wind Permitting and Defence Screening](swedish-offshore-wind-permitting.md) - Sweden's government-level EEZ permitting regime, where Armed Forces objections over areas of national interest for defence have driven mass rejections, ahead of a planned move to an auction-based system
* [Romanian Offshore Wind Framework (Black Sea)](romanian-offshore-wind-framework.md) - Law No. 121/2024 plus the six Black Sea perimeters approved for concession in July 2026 — 11.5 GW across three phases, with a first 3.1 GW tranche to be tendered toward a 2035 target
* [France 2030 Floating Wind Port Funding (ADEME)](france-2030-floating-wind-port-funding.md) - The EUR 258.5 million France 2030 award to Marseille-Fos, Nantes-Saint-Nazaire, Brest, Port-la-Nouvelle and Cherbourg for quays, storage areas and access infrastructure serving floating offshore wind, with per-port amounts and project scopes
* [German Wind Energy at Sea Act (WindSeeG)](german-offshore-wind-act-windseeg.md) - Germany's offshore wind law and the August 2026 BMWE draft amendment introducing bilateral CfDs, 2-4.8 GW annual tenders, a 35-year operating period and cross-border projects, after the 2025 zero-bid tender; also records BSH site pre-investigation and its August 2026 preliminary geophysical survey tender
* [Azerbaijan Offshore Wind Framework (Caspian Sea)](azerbaijan-offshore-wind-framework.md) - Azerbaijan's emerging offshore wind framework in the Caspian Sea, opened by the Cabinet of Ministers' August 2026 designation of a 711.4-square-kilometre water area
* [Vietnam Power Development Plan VIII (PDP8)](vietnam-power-development-plan-8.md) - Vietnam's national power development plan and the gateway for offshore wind projects; targets of 6-17 GW by 2030-2035 and 113-139 GW by 2050 under its revised version, with the 6 GW Can Gio 2 project seeking inclusion
* [French Offshore Wind Permitting Process](french-permitting-eolien-en-mer.md) - The French administrative process for authorizing offshore wind farms, from public debate through DUP and the single environmental authorization
