# Overview

Dadu I and Dadu II are two offshore wind projects under development by
[Ørsted](/companies/orsted.md) off Changhua County, Taiwan — the same
county as the developer's operating [Greater Changhua](/projects/changhua.md)
zone. Both are at the environmental permitting stage, recorded in the
environmental assessment database of Taiwan's Ministry of Environment
(MOENV).

# Key Facts

| | Dadu I | Dadu II |
|---|---|---|
| Potential capacity | up to 924 MW | no more than 2,002 MW |
| Sea area | not stated | ~234.6 km² |
| Distance from shore | not stated | ~38 km at the nearest point |
| Turbine class | not stated | 14 MW to 22 MW units |
| Status (August 2026) | Environmental Impact Statement recently completed | entering environmental assessment, in public consultation |

The final turbine count and layout for Dadu II have not been determined.
A public meeting on Dadu II was held on 13 August 2026 in Changhua County.

# Significance

At up to 2 GW, Dadu II would be substantially larger than Ørsted's
existing 1,820 MW Greater Changhua complex taken as a whole, and its
14–22 MW turbine range sits at the upper end of current
[turbine scaling](/technology/turbine-scaling.md) — well above the 14 MW
machines installed at Greater Changhua and at nearby
[Hai Long](/projects/hai-long.md).
