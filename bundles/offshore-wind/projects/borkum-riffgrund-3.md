# Overview

Borkum Riffgrund 3 is a 913 MW offshore wind farm in the German North Sea and
[Ørsted](/companies/orsted.md)'s largest project in Germany. It is owned by
Ørsted together with Nuveen Infrastructure and comprises 83
[Siemens Gamesa](/companies/siemens-gamesa.md) 11 MW turbines on
[fixed-bottom foundations](/technology/fixed-bottom-foundations.md).

# Key Facts

| Status | More than 99% complete (August 2026); full commissioning expected Q3 2026 |

# Construction timeline

- **December 2024** — commercial operation date pushed back from end-2025 to
  early 2026 after delays to the **DolWin5** grid connection.
- **December 2025** — the wind farm feeds first power into the German grid.
- **Early 2026** — turbine installation completed.
- **20 August 2026** — in its first-half 2026 results, Ørsted reports the
  project **more than 99 per cent complete**, with commissioning of the
  remaining turbines continuing and **full commissioning expected in Q3 2026**.
