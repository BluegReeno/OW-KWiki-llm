# Overview

**BC-Wind** is [Ocean Winds](/companies/ocean-winds.md)' first offshore wind project in Poland, located in the Polish Baltic Sea approximately 23 kilometres from the coastline. It comprises 26 wind turbines for a total planned capacity of up to 390 MW, and is expected to supply electricity to almost half a million Polish households.

Ocean Winds, a 50-50 joint venture between EDP Renewables and ENGIE, reached financial close on the 390 MW wind farm at the end of 2025, moving the project into its construction phase. First power is expected in 2028.

# Contracts

- **Technical advisory, execution phase (August 2026)** — [Bilfinger](/companies/bilfinger.md) was selected to provide technical advisory services during the execution phase, supervising turbine installation, offshore structures and export cable installation, and ensuring compliance with technical requirements, permits and applicable regulations. Bilfinger had already supported the client on the onshore part of the project.
- **Onshore substation and export cable route** — design and construction by the Polish company P&Q; onshore construction began in 2026.
- **Export cables** — designed and installed by Tele-Fonika Kable (TFK), based in Bydgoszcz.
