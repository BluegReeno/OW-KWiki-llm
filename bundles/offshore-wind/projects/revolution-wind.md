# Overview

Revolution Wind is a 704 MW offshore wind farm under construction in US federal
waters off Rhode Island, developed as a joint venture between
[Ørsted](/companies/orsted.md) and [Skyborn Renewables](/companies/skyborn.md)
(a Global Infrastructure Partners portfolio company), which took over
Eversource's share when it exited US offshore wind in 2024. The wind farm is
contracted to supply power to both Rhode Island and Connecticut, and comprises
65 [Siemens Gamesa](/companies/siemens-gamesa.md) 11 MW turbines.

# Status

As of 18 August 2026 the project is reported to be **more than 95 per cent
complete**, with **four turbines left to install**.

Ørsted's first-half 2026 results (20 August 2026) confirm the same picture:
**61 of the 65 turbines installed**, commissioning work underway, and **full
commissioning expected in the second half of 2026** — one of three Ørsted wind
farms due to be fully commissioned this year alongside
[Borkum Riffgrund 3](/projects/borkum-riffgrund-3.md) and
[Greater Changhua 2b & 4](/projects/changhua.md). The project began delivering
electricity to the New England grid in **March 2026**.

Construction was interrupted in December 2025, when the Trump administration
issued stop-work orders on five US projects under construction — Revolution
Wind, [Sunrise Wind](/projects/sunrise-wind.md),
[Coastal Virginia Offshore Wind](/projects/coastal-virginia-offshore-wind.md),
[Empire Wind 1](/projects/empire-wind-1.md) and
[Vineyard Wind 1](/projects/vineyard-wind-1.md) — citing national security and
radar interference concerns. Courts ruled the suspensions unlawful and work
resumed; see [US BOEM leasing process](/policy/us-boem-leasing-process.md).

The installation contractors and vessels have not been reported in the sources
used here.
