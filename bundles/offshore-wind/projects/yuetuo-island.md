# Overview

The Guodian Tangshan Leting Yuetuo Island Offshore Wind Farm (Phase I) is a
304 MW project in northern China, off Leting county in Tangshan, Hebei
province. It is the first offshore wind project anywhere to take Siemens
Energy's lightweight integrated offshore substation design.

# Offshore Substation (August 2026)

On 18 August 2026 it was reported that the project's offshore substation had
been installed — the first deployment at an offshore wind project of
[Siemens Energy](/companies/siemens-energy.md)'s High-Voltage TranSwitch Unit
(HV-TSU), a design that integrates key high-voltage components into a compact
package to cut topside weight. The 300 MW topside module measures 45 m long,
38 m wide and 23 m high and weighs approximately 1,700 tonnes. ZPMC built the
topside module, the jacket foundation and the foundation pin piles.

The reporting available covers only the substation: it names no turbine
supplier, model or count, no foundation or installation contractors for the
array, and no first-power or commissioning date.
