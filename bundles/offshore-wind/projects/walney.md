# Walney 1 & 2 Offshore Wind Farms

Walney 1 and Walney 2 are two adjacent offshore wind farms in the **Irish Sea**,
**15 km west of Barrow-in-Furness, Cumbria**, UK. Combined capacity is
**367 MW**, delivered by **102 Siemens 3.6 MW turbines** —
an early-generation machine size compared with the 14 MW-class units in
[Ørsted](/companies/orsted.md)'s current construction portfolio, see
[turbine scaling](/technology/turbine-scaling.md).

Walney 1 was **fully operational in May 2011**; construction of Walney 2 was
completed at the end of **2011**, and the entire project entered **commercial
operation in 2012**.

Ørsted **majority-owns and operates** the wind farms. **Offshore Partners
Walney (OPW)** holds a **24.8% ownership stake**.

# Offtake

- **August 2026** — **TotalEnergies Gas & Power Limited** entered into a
  **long-term power purchase agreement** with **OPW**, buying
  **REGO-backed electricity** annually from OPW's 24.8% share of generation
  **through to 2030**. No volume or price was disclosed. See
  [TotalEnergies](/companies/totalenergies.md).
- **July 2026** — UK renewable energy provider **Good Energy** signed a PPA
  with Ørsted for Walney 1 & 2: a **two-year** agreement for **200 GWh a
  year**, around one-sixth of the wind farms' output, and the largest deal of
  its kind for Good Energy.
