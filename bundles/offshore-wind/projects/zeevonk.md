# Overview

Zeevonk is an offshore wind project in the Dutch North Sea, in the IJmuiden Ver
zone, developed by the **Zeevonk joint venture** of
[Vattenfall](/companies/vattenfall.md) and
[Copenhagen Infrastructure Partners (CIP)](/companies/copenhagen-infrastructure-partners.md).

# Status

In July 2026, [DEME Offshore](/companies/deme-offshore.md) was awarded the
contract for **foundation installation of the first phase** of the Zeevonk
offshore wind farm. This is a tender/contract-award milestone; the excerpt does
not disclose the project's capacity, turbine count, foundation type, contract
value, or installation schedule.
