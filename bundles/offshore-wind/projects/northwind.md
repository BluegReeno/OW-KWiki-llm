# Northwind Offshore Wind Farm

216 MW offshore wind farm located 37 km off the Belgian coast in the North Sea, comprising 72 Vestas 3 MW turbines. Commissioned in 2014.

Originally co-owned by [Sumitomo Corporation](/companies/sumitomo-corporation.md) and Parkwind. In March 2024, Aspiravi Offshore acquired Parkwind's share (Parkwind is now part of [JERA Nex BP](/companies/jera-nex-bp.md)). In July 2026, Sumitomo announced it is divesting its remaining stake to Publiwind, a consortium of Belgian investment firms and public institutions — insurer Ethias SA, Walloon energy-transition investment vehicle SOCOFE SA, and sovereign wealth fund SFPIM. The transaction is expected to close during fiscal year 2026, subject to regulatory approval.

## Facts
- **Capacity**: 216 MW
- **Turbines**: 72 x Vestas 3 MW
- **Location**: Belgian North Sea, 37 km offshore
- **Commissioned**: 2014
- **Owners**: Aspiravi Offshore and Sumitomo Corporation (Sumitomo's stake being sold to the Publiwind consortium, pending regulatory approval, expected close FY2026)
