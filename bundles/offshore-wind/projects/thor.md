# Overview

Thor is a 1.1 GW offshore wind farm off Denmark's west coast near Jutland (North Sea) — set to be Denmark's largest offshore wind farm. It is a joint project between [RWE](/companies/rwe-offshore-wind.md) (51%) and Norges Bank Investment Management (49%), with RWE in charge of construction and operations throughout the project's lifecycle. When fully operational, Thor is expected to supply the equivalent of more than one million Danish households.

# Turbines and Technology

The wind farm uses 72 [Siemens Gamesa](/companies/siemens-gamesa.md) turbines, each rated at up to 15 MW, standing around 148 metres above sea level with 115-metre-long rotor blades. Thor is set to be the first offshore wind farm in the world to use 36 carbon-reduced steel turbine towers from Siemens Gamesa, and 40 of the turbines will feature a total of 120 recyclable rotor blades — a step toward lifecycle sustainability in line with RWE's ambitions. Siemens Gamesa's Le Havre (France) plant produced the first 115-metre blades shipped to Thor.

# Construction Status

- **March 2026** — first turbine installed; Thor fed its first power into the Danish grid; RWE opened its O&M base.
- **July 2026** — turbine installation reached the halfway point with 36 of 72 turbines installed. Installation works run out of the Port of Esbjerg using Fred. Olsen Windcarrier's WTIV Brave Tern.
- The remaining 36 turbines are due to be installed by the end of 2026, with testing and full commercial operation expected by spring 2027.
