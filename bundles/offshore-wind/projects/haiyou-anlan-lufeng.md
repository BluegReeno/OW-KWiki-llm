# Overview

**Haiyou Anlan** is a single 16 MW [floating offshore wind](/technology/floating-wind.md) turbine owned and operated by [CNOOC](/companies/cnooc.md), supplying power to the company's **Lufeng oilfield** offshore China. CNOOC reported the unit as being **in operation on 10 August 2026**.

At 16 MW it is among the largest floating wind turbines deployed anywhere to date — floating projects in Europe still run at a fraction of that unit rating — and it is an off-grid application: the output electrifies an offshore hydrocarbon production facility rather than feeding a national grid under a support scheme.
