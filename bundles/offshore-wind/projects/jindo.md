# Jindo Offshore Wind Project Cluster

The Jindo Offshore Wind Project Cluster is a planned 3.2 GW offshore wind
development off Jindo County in South Korea's Jeonnam-Gwangju
(Jeollanam-do) region, led by U.S.-based developer
[Pacifico Energy Korea](/companies/pacifico-energy-korea.md), which is
responsible for overall investment and project management.

## Phases and developer selection

The cluster is built out in three phases:

* **Phase 1 — Myeong Ryang (420 MW)**: secured its Electricity Business
  Licence in September 2025 and completed its preliminary Environmental
  Impact Assessment report in March 2026.
* **Phase 2 — Manho (990 MW) and Jindo Baram (1.8 GW)**: together form the
  2.13 GW Jindo Offshore Wind Integrated Cluster Phase 2 as reported by
  offshoreWIND.biz. Both projects have applied for Electricity Business
  Licences, still under review as of August 2026.

South Korea's Ministry of Climate, Energy and Environment designated the
area off Jindo County as an Integrated Cluster in March 2026, after
Jeonnam-Gwangju Special Metropolitan City (formerly South Jeolla Province)
finalised the development plans in 2025 through consultation with government
agencies and two Public-Private Councils. The regional government is the
implementing institution.

In June 2026 Jeonnam-Gwangju established an evaluation committee to assess
developer suitability, examining the development capabilities and
implementation plans of prospective developers. On 20 August 2026
[Pacifico Energy Korea](/companies/pacifico-energy-korea.md) was announced
as the **sole developer of Phase 2**, following the signing of an
Implementation Management Agreement between the two projects,
Jeonnam-Gwangju Special Metropolitan City and Jindo County.

The regional government is also pursuing designation of the Honam
Semiconductor Cluster; the developers note the offshore wind cluster could
supply renewable electricity to energy-intensive industry, including future
semiconductor manufacturing.

## Local supply chain

Pacifico Energy Korea is building a tailored local supply chain anchored in
the Gwangyang Bay Area. In July 2026 it signed a memorandum of
understanding (MOU) with three South Korean suppliers to cooperate on the
cluster's development:

* **[EEW KHPC](/companies/eew-group.md)** — offshore wind foundation
  manufacturer (Gwangyang plant of Germany's EEW Group), supplying
  foundation components including pin piles.
* **KMC Line Co., Ltd.** — marine logistics and offshore wind vessel
  operator, providing component transportation, installation support and
  dedicated O&M vessel operations.
* **CS Energy Co., Ltd.** — supplier of key offshore wind foundation
  components.

The parties aim to activate the Gwangyang Bay Area offshore wind supply
chain, develop local talent, and support job creation in Jindo County and
the surrounding region. An earlier (June 2026) cooperation with Korea's
Hana Group covers financing of the 3.2 GW cluster.
