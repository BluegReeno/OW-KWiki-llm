# Trianel Windpark Borkum I (TWB I)

Offshore wind farm in the German North Sea, developed and operated by
[Trianel](/companies/trianel.md) and described as Germany's first **municipal** offshore
wind farm.

# Key facts

- **Location**: North Sea, about **45 km northwest of Borkum**, Germany.
- **Capacity**: **200 MW**.
- **Turbines**: **40 Adwen AD5-116** (formerly Areva Wind M5000).
- **Status**: in operation since **2015**.
- **Cumulative generation**: more than **6.59 billion kWh**, per Trianel (August 2026).

# Balancing market participation

In July 2026 the wind farm entered Germany's balancing energy market:

- Around **70 MW** of capacity **prequalified** for the balancing market.
- Supply of **negative secondary control reserve** began in July 2026; marketing of the
  balancing capacity started on **22 July 2026**.
- Negative secondary control reserve lets the TSO automatically reduce the wind farm's
  output when needed; per Trianel the adjustment must be made **within five minutes**.
- Enabled by a dedicated **remote-control infrastructure** with a **redundant
  architecture**, transmitting TSO requests in real time and implementing output changes
  automatically.
- Trianel frames this as an added revenue stream alongside existing power sales,
  including PPAs.
