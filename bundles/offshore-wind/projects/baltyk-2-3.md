# Bałtyk 2 & 3 Offshore Wind Farms

Bałtyk 2 and Bałtyk 3 are two neighbouring offshore wind farms in the Polish
Baltic Sea totalling **1.4 GW**, developed as a 50/50 joint venture between
[Equinor](/companies/equinor.md) and the Polish energy group **Polenergia**.
Turbines are supplied by [Siemens Gamesa](/companies/siemens-gamesa.md). The
same partnership is also developing **Bałtyk 1** (up to 1,560 MW, targeted
commercial operation 2032).

The projects use [monopile foundations](/technology/fixed-bottom-foundations.md)
fabricated by the Dutch manufacturer [Sif](/companies/sif.md), with the
Belgian steel contractor **Smulders** associated with the foundation scope.

## Timeline

- **February 2026**: Offshore construction starts; first foundations installed
  during 2026.
- **20 August 2026**: Sif loads out the **final monopiles** for Bałtyk 2 and 3,
  completing its fabrication and load-out campaign for the projects.
- **2027**: First power expected.
