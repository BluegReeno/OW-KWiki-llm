# Overview

Bellrock is a floating offshore wind project developed by Nadara. It joins
the growing pipeline of European [floating offshore wind](/technology/floating-wind.md)
developments moving through early engineering and design.

# Recent Developments

- **July 2026 — export cable corridor survey**: XOCEAN is set to begin a
  geophysical survey along a potential offshore export cable corridor for
  Bellrock, an early site-investigation step towards defining the project's
  grid connection route. The source excerpt gives no survey dates, corridor
  location or landfall details.

- **July 2026 — electrical infrastructure studies**: Nadara appointed
  [Aker Solutions](/companies/aker-solutions.md) to undertake electrical
  infrastructure studies for Bellrock. This adds an electrical
  infrastructure engineering scope to Aker Solutions' offshore wind
  portfolio, alongside its HVDC substructure work.
