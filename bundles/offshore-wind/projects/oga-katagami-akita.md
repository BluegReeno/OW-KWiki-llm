# Oga–Katagami–Akita Offshore Wind Project

A 315 MW fixed-bottom offshore wind farm to be built off Akita Prefecture,
Japan. It is owned and developed by Oga Katagami Akita Offshore Green Energy,
a consortium of [JERA Nex BP](/companies/jera-nex-bp.md), Electric Power
Development (J-POWER), Tohoku Electric Power and Itochu.

The project is among the first fixed-bottom offshore wind developments in
Japan's general sea areas to advance through the country's public tender
process, and one of the first there to use turbines above 10 MW.

## Key Facts
- **Capacity**: 315 MW — 21 turbines of 15 MW each
- **Turbine supplier**: [Vestas](/companies/vestas.md) (V236-15.0 MW class)
- **Foundations**: monopiles with transition pieces; preliminary and detailed design by COWI with Kajima Corporation and Kobori Research Complex; monopiles manufactured by [JFE Engineering](/companies/jfe-engineering.md) (max diameter ~11 m, length ~80 m, weight ~2,000 t)
- **Installation**: [DEME Offshore](/companies/deme-offshore.md) contracted to install the turbines
- **Schedule**: turbine deliveries expected to begin 2026, commissioning planned 2027, full commissioning June 2028

## Timeline
- **October 2025**: Kajima Corporation signed the foundation contract.
- **July 2026**: Received a certificate of conformity from Bureau Veritas Japan, confirming the design of the wind power generation facilities complies with Japanese technical standards and regulatory requirements. The conformity assessment covered site condition assessments, integrated load analysis and detailed design verification across all project elements, with Kajima Corporation, Vestas Japan / MHI Vestas Japan and Ove Arup & Partners Japan involved. Per COWI, the farm is designed to withstand extremely rare earthquakes, making it the world's first 15 MW-class offshore wind facility with such seismic design considerations.
- **December 2025**: Kajima Corporation awarded [JFE Engineering](/companies/jfe-engineering.md) the contract for foundation manufacturing and transportation services.
- **August 2026**: JFE Engineering rolled out the first monopile for the project at its Kasaoka Monopile Plant — described as Japan's first domestically manufactured offshore wind monopile.
