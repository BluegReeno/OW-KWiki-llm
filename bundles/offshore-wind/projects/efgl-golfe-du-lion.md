# Overview

The **Éoliennes Flottantes du Golfe du Lion (EFGL)** is a 30 MW [floating offshore wind](/technology/floating-wind.md) farm in the Gulf of Lion, in the French Mediterranean. It is developed by [Ocean Winds](/companies/ocean-winds.md). As one of France's pilot floating wind projects, it demonstrates floating technology in Mediterranean conditions ahead of larger commercial floating farms planned in the region.

# Status

In July 2026 the farm reached **full power**, meaning all of its turbines were producing at rated capacity — a key operational milestone for the project. Shortly after, [Principle Power](/companies/principle-power.md) was awarded the Operations and Maintenance (O&M) support contract for the project's floating foundations.
