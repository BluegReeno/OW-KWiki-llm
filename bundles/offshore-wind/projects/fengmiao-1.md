# Fengmiao 1 Offshore Wind Farm

Fengmiao 1 is a 495 MW offshore wind farm in Taiwan developed by
[Copenhagen Infrastructure Partners](/companies/copenhagen-infrastructure-partners.md),
adding to the developer's Asia-Pacific portfolio alongside its European and US
projects. It is the second Taiwanese project tracked in this bundle after
Ørsted's [Greater Changhua](/projects/changhua.md) cluster.

## Offshore substation

In July 2026, [PTSC M&C](/companies/ptsc-mc.md) completed the construction and
loadout of the Fengmiao offshore substation. Loadout — moving the completed
topside or jacket from the fabrication yard onto a transport barge or vessel —
marks the end of onshore fabrication and the start of the offshore transport
and installation campaign, so the substation is the piece of the project's
[grid connection](/technology/hvdc-grid-connection.md) that is now ready to go
to sea.

Semco Maritime appears among the parties associated with the article, but the
available excerpt does not state its role, and no substation weight, dimensions,
sail-away date, installation contractor or installation window was given.

## Construction timeline

* **July 2026** - [PTSC M&C](/companies/ptsc-mc.md) completes construction and
  loadout of the offshore substation.
* **5 August 2026** - Two offshore construction milestones are reported
  complete: the offshore substation works and the export cable works. The
  substation therefore went from yard loadout to installed offshore in about a
  week of reporting, and the project's
  [grid connection](/technology/hvdc-grid-connection.md) is structurally in
  place ahead of turbine installation.

[DEME Offshore](/companies/deme-offshore.md), Jan De Nul and Copenhagen
Offshore Partners — CIP's dedicated development and construction management arm
— are listed among the parties associated with the August 2026 article, but the
available excerpt does not state which company performed which scope, and no
cable length, voltage, landfall, installation vessel, completion dates or
turbine installation schedule was given.
