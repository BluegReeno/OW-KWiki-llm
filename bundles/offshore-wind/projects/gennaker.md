# Overview

Gennaker is a 976.5 MW offshore wind farm in the German Baltic Sea, off the
coast of Mecklenburg-Vorpommern, developed by
[Skyborn Renewables](/companies/skyborn.md). It is one of the largest
offshore wind projects in the German Baltic and Skyborn's second German
asset in this bundle alongside the operating
[Nordergründe](/projects/nordergrunde.md) wind farm.

# Foundations and supply chain

The wind farm uses [fixed-bottom foundations](/technology/fixed-bottom-foundations.md)
with 63 transition pieces (TPs) — the steel section between a monopile and
the turbine tower — implying 63 foundation positions.

- **August 2026**: [Dajin Heavy Industry](/companies/dajin-heavy-industry.md)
  started fabrication of the 63 transition pieces.
- **August 2026**: [OEG](/companies/oeg.md) secured a contract to provide a
  subsea corrosion protection system for the wind farm. Contract value, system
  type, the number of structures covered and the delivery schedule were not
  reported.

Turbine supplier, monopile supplier, marshalling port, transport and
installation contractor, offshore construction window and commissioning date
were not reported in the source items and are not asserted here.
