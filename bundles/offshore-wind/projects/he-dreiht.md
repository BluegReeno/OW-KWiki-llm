# He Dreiht Offshore Wind Farm

He Dreiht is a 960 MW offshore wind farm in the German North Sea developed by
EnBW. Its turbines are supplied by [Vestas](/companies/vestas.md).

# Status

* **12 August 2026** — all wind turbines have been installed at the 960 MW wind
  farm, completing the turbine installation campaign.
* **11 August 2026** — EnBW said it expected to complete the installation of all
  wind turbines shortly.
* **July 2026** — the German Federal Police recovered a rotor blade that had
  detached from a He Dreiht turbine and was drifting at sea; the cause was not
  reported (see
  [digest](/digests/2026-07-28-he-dreiht-blade-recovery.md)).

# Context

At 960 MW, He Dreiht is one of the larger German North Sea projects under
construction, alongside [Nordlicht](/projects/nordlicht.md) (1.6 GW,
Vattenfall) and [Nordseecluster](/projects/nordseecluster.md) (1.6 GW, RWE),
and the Baltic [Gennaker](/projects/gennaker.md). Its turbines are among the
very large machines tracked under [turbine scaling](/technology/turbine-scaling.md).
