# Can Gio 2 Offshore Wind Project

Can Gio 2 is a proposed offshore wind development with an installed capacity
of **6 GW**, located between **10 and 18 kilometres** off the Can Gio district
of Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam. It is put forward by a joint venture between the
Vietnamese renewable energy developers [Xuan Cau Holdings](/companies/xuan-cau-holdings.md)
and [Trung Nam](/companies/trung-nam.md).

## Status

* **20 August 2026** — Ho Chi Minh City's **Standing People's Committee**
  reviewed the project's feasibility study. Vice Chairman of the Ho Chi Minh
  City People's Committee **Nguyen Cong Vinh** chaired a meeting with the
  relevant departments to hear the feasibility study report for investment in
  the project. The meeting was recorded in the city government's bulletin on
  the Standing People's Committee's direction and administration activities
  for that day.
* The project is also being proposed for inclusion in the list of projects
  under Vietnam's revised
  [Power Development Plan VIII](/policy/vietnam-power-development-plan-8.md).

## Investment

The city government's 20 August bulletin gives no investment value,
development schedule or technical specifications. Vietnamese media report a
**preliminary investment estimated at VND 587.73 trillion** (approximately
**EUR 19.2 billion**), with financing expected to comprise equity, commercial
loans and export credit agency financing.
