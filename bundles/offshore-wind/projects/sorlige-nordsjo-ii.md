# Sørlige Nordsjø II Offshore Wind Farm

Sørlige Nordsjø II (Southern North Sea II) is Norway's first commercial
offshore wind farm, a fixed-bottom project of up to 1.5 GW in the southern
North Sea. It is being developed by [Ventyr](/companies/ventyr.md), a
consortium of [JERA Nex BP](/companies/jera-nex-bp.md) and Ingka Investments.

## Award

Ventyr won the rights to develop the zone in **March 2024** with a bid of
**NOK 1.15/kWh (EUR 0.099/kWh)** in Norway's first offshore wind auction,
which offered 1.5 GW in the fixed-bottom project development zone.

## Licensing and grid connection

- **June 2026**: Ventyr applied to the Norwegian Ministry of Energy for an
  extension of the licence application deadline, saying it had "experienced a
  number of unexpected challenges in the development and planning of Sørlige
  Nordsjø II as well as confronting the difficult macroeconomic environment in
  offshore wind", which had slowed some development activities. It said the
  extra time would be used in part to assess an alternative landfall and
  [grid connection](/technology/hvdc-grid-connection.md) option.
- The alternative route, referred to as **G 2.0**, involves new infrastructure
  near the existing Lista substation at Alcoa's aluminium plant, built in
  parallel with the Sørlige Nordsjø II converter station. Ventyr described it
  as potentially attractive with significant societal and environmental
  benefits, but needing further studies to mature.
- **24 August 2026**: The Ministry of Energy granted a **twelve-month
  extension**, setting the new deadline for submitting the licence application
  at **15 October 2027**.

## Context

Sørlige Nordsjø II is the fixed-bottom half of Norway's offshore wind
programme. The Ministry launched the country's first
[floating wind](/technology/floating-wind.md) tender for the Utsira Nord area
in May 2025, received two applications for the 500 MW areas in September 2025,
and said in December 2025 that both applicants — a Deep Wind Offshore/EDF
Renewables joint venture and an [Equinor](/companies/equinor.md)/Vårgrønn
consortium — would be awarded a project area.
