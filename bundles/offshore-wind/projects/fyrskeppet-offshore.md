# Overview

**Fyrskeppet Offshore** is an offshore wind project in Sweden's exclusive economic
zone, located around 54 kilometres east of Söderhamn in the southern Gulf of
Bothnia, developed by [Skyborn Renewables](/companies/skyborn.md).

# Permitting and Schedule

On 24 July 2026 the Swedish government **approved** the project, one of only two
approvals in a batch of 13 decisions (eleven were rejected over defence concerns —
see [Swedish Offshore Wind Permitting and Defence Screening](/policy/swedish-offshore-wind-permitting.md)).

The permit authorises up to **93 wind turbines**, with the government citing an
expected annual generation of **8 TWh to 11 TWh**. Earlier project information had
described a larger layout of 187 turbines totalling around **2.8 GW**, which had
received a county recommendation in April 2025.

Conditions attached to the approval:

- The wind farm must be **completed within ten years, by 2036**.
- Separate permits are still required for the **subsea export cables** connecting
  the project to Sweden's onshore transmission grid before construction can start.
