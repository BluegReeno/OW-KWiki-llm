# Overview

Hesselø is a Danish offshore wind farm site in the Kattegat sea between
Denmark and Sweden, with a **minimum installed capacity of 800 MW**. It is one
of three sites in
[Denmark's 2.8 GW CfD offshore wind tender](/tenders/denmark-2-8gw-cfd-tender-2026.md),
launched in November 2025 after the country's previous, unsupported 3 GW tender
drew no bids.

# Status

* **20 May 2026** — bidding closed; the Danish Energy Agency received five bids.
* **4 August 2026** — [Vattenfall](/companies/vattenfall.md) named winning
  bidder with the lowest bid, a two-sided Contract for Difference strike price
  of **DKK 542/MWh (~EUR 72.70)**, guaranteed for 20 years from commissioning.
  The contract was awarded to Vattenfall Vindkraft Offshore Wind DK P/S.
  Award is subject to formal verification before contract signature.
* Target: **fully operational by 2032**. Consent runs 30 years, extendable by
  10. Grid connection is reserved, with the connection point roughly 50 km
  inland.

No turbine supplier, foundation type, turbine count, layout or construction
schedule has been reported.
