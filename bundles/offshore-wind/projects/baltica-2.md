# Baltica 2 Offshore Wind Farm

Baltica 2 is an offshore wind farm under construction in the Polish Baltic Sea, developed as a
50/50 joint venture between PGE Polska Grupa Energetyczna and [Ørsted](/companies/orsted.md). It
is the first and larger phase of the two-phase Baltica complex (Baltica 2 and Baltica 3), at
roughly 1.5 GW, and follows [Baltic Power](/projects/baltic-power.md) as one of the projects
opening the Polish offshore wind market.

The wind farm uses [fixed-bottom monopile foundations](/technology/fixed-bottom-foundations.md).

# Construction status

- **August 2026** — [Steelwind Nordenham](/companies/steelwind-nordenham.md) completed the
  delivery of all monopiles supplied under its Baltica 2 contract, closing out its part of the
  project's foundation supply.
- **August 2026** — Elektrownia Wiatrowa Baltica – 2, the project company, awarded Commissioning
  Service Operation Vessel (CSOV) contracts to
  [Bernhard Schulte Offshore](/companies/bernhard-schulte-offshore.md) and
  [Pelagic Partners](/companies/pelagic-partners.md). Vessel names, charter durations and values
  were not reported.
- **20 August 2026** — Ørsted's first-half 2026 results put the project at approximately
  **40 per cent complete**. Foundation installation started in May 2026 and has reached
  **103 of the project's 111 positions** (107 wind turbine foundations plus four offshore
  substation foundations); the monopiles for the
  [offshore substations](/technology/offshore-substations.md) are all installed and
  manufacturing of the four substations is progressing. Commissioning is scheduled for the
  **second half of 2027**.
- **21 August 2026** — [Van Oord](/companies/van-oord.md) completed the installation of all
  **111 monopile foundations** at the project, closing the foundation installation campaign
  that started in May 2026. The count matches the 111 positions reported the previous day
  (107 turbine foundations plus four offshore substation foundations).
- **25 August 2026** — [Ørsted](/companies/orsted.md), acting on behalf of the joint venture with
  PGE, awarded a **helicopter medical evacuation (medevac) contract** for the project to
  [Northern Helicopter](/companies/northern-helicopter.md). Contract duration, value and the
  operating base were not reported.
- **27 August 2026** — [F.lli Righini](/companies/f-lli-righini.md) said it had designed, tested
  and delivered **Secondary Structure Installation Tools (SSIT)** for Van Oord's Baltica 2 work:
  tooling for boat landings and internal platforms mounted on the monopiles, a dedicated tool for
  external platform operations, and offshore seafastening structures, designed for unmanned,
  remotely operated installation. Contract value, number of tools and dates were not reported.
