# Overview

North Sea I Mid (Nordsøen Midt) is a Danish offshore wind farm site located
approximately **20 km off the coast near Hvide Sande**, with a **minimum
installed capacity of 1 GW**. It is the largest of the three sites in
[Denmark's 2.8 GW CfD offshore wind tender](/tenders/denmark-2-8gw-cfd-tender-2026.md),
alongside [Hesselø](/projects/hesselo.md) and the still-open Nordsøen Syd.

# Status

* **20 May 2026** — bidding closed; the Danish Energy Agency received two bids.
* **4 August 2026** — [Vattenfall](/companies/vattenfall.md) named winning
  bidder with the lowest bid, a two-sided Contract for Difference strike price
  of **DKK 504/MWh (~EUR 67.60)**, guaranteed for 20 years from commissioning.
  The contract was awarded to Vattenfall Vindkraft A/S on behalf of a company
  yet to be established. Award is subject to formal verification before
  contract signature.
* Target: **fully operational by 2032**. Consent runs 30 years, extendable by
  10. Grid connection is reserved, with the connection point roughly 50 km
  inland.

No turbine supplier, foundation type, turbine count, layout or construction
schedule has been reported.
