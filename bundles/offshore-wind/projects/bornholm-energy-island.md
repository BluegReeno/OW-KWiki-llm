# Bornholm Energy Island

Bornholm Energy Island is a Danish energy-island project on and around the
Baltic Sea island of Bornholm, developed by the Danish transmission system
operator **Energinet**. Rather than an artificial island, the concept uses
Bornholm itself as the landing and collection point for offshore wind in the
Baltic, with onshore converter and substation facilities on the island as the
hub between the offshore wind connections and the export links.

The project is planned as a Danish-German hybrid connection, which puts
[50Hertz](/companies/50hertz.md) — the German Baltic TSO — on the other side of
the link. That framing is background rather than something reported in the item
below, so no capacity, cable route or commissioning date is asserted here.

## Onshore facilities contract (August 2026)

On 5 August 2026 offshoreWIND.biz reported that Danish construction company
**Per Aarsleff A/S** ([Aarsleff](/companies/aarsleff.md)) had been awarded a
**design-and-build contract worth EUR 495 million** by Energinet for the
project's **onshore facilities**.

This is one of the largest single awards the bundle has recorded on the
[grid-connection](/technology/hvdc-grid-connection.md) side, and it is notable
for landing with a civil construction contractor rather than an electrical
OEM or marine installer: the civil works, buildings and site infrastructure of
an energy island are a contract class of their own, distinct from the converter
technology and the subsea cables.

The item available here is a truncated excerpt. Nothing further is asserted: no
scope breakdown of the "onshore facilities", no construction start or
completion dates, no site or municipality on Bornholm, no procurement route or
competing bidders, no converter or cable supplier, no project capacity, and no
commissioning date for the energy island itself.
