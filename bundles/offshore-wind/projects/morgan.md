# Morgan Offshore Wind Project

Morgan is an offshore wind project of up to **1.5 GW** in the **Irish Sea**,
on a seabed site originally awarded through **The Crown Estate's Offshore
Wind Leasing Round 4 in 2021**. It is unusual in the UK pipeline: a
consented, grid-connected project without a developer.

The site was held by a joint venture between **EnBW** and
[JERA Nex BP](/companies/jera-nex-bp.md). In **January 2026** EnBW decided
to exit both the Morgan and Mona projects in the UK. JERA Nex BP acquired
EnBW's stake in the 1.5 GW **Mona** project, but the joint venture
**discontinued development of Morgan and returned the lease rights to The
Crown Estate**. The Crown Estate announced in **March 2026** that it would
re-tender the site, and launched an accelerated tender process on
**27 July 2026** — see
[Crown Estate Morgan Site Re-Tender (2026)](/tenders/crown-estate-morgan-site-retender-2026.md).

# Development status

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Capacity | Up to 1.5 GW |
| Location | Irish Sea, UK |
| Seabed award | Offshore Wind Leasing Round 4 (2021) |
| Previous developer | EnBW / [JERA Nex BP](/companies/jera-nex-bp.md) joint venture (discontinued January 2026) |
| Generation DCO | Granted August 2025 |
| Transmission DCO | Application submitted jointly with transmission infrastructure for the 480 MW Morecambe offshore wind farm; decision expected September 2026 |
| Grid connection | Agreement in place with the National Energy System Operator (NESO) |
| Current status | Lease returned to The Crown Estate; re-tender under way, development rights to be awarded by end of 2026 |

The Crown Estate has said it aims to **preserve the substantial progress
already made** on the project, so that a new developer can build on the
existing consent and survey work and maintain delivery momentum rather
than restart development.
