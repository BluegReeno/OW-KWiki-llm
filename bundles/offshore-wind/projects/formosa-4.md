# Formosa 4 Offshore Wind Farm

Formosa 4 is a Taiwanese offshore wind farm developed by **Synera Renewable Energy (SRE)**,
to be built approximately **20 kilometres off the coast of Miaoli County**. It is one of
the newer Taiwanese projects alongside [Fengmiao 1](/projects/fengmiao-1.md),
[Hai Long](/projects/hai-long.md) and Ørsted's [Greater Changhua](/projects/changhua.md).

## Configuration

- **Capacity**: 495 MW.
- **Turbines**: 35 [Siemens Gamesa](/companies/siemens-gamesa.md) 14 MW machines
  (selected September 2025).
- **Foundations**: fixed-bottom, with transition pieces fabricated by
  Century Wind Power (CWP) in Taiwan and [SK oceanplant](/companies/sk-oceanplant.md)
  in South Korea.

## Offtake

In late December 2025 SRE said the **entire output** of the 495 MW wind farm had been
sold, under **three corporate power purchase agreements**, while urging the government to
resolve key project financing requirements.

## Milestones

- **January 2026** — construction of the project's offshore substation started (in Vietnam).
- **August 2026** — [Glamox](/companies/glamox.md) secured a contract to supply marine
  lighting for the **35 wind turbine foundations**: more than 900 marine luminaires for the
  transition pieces, under an agreement with **ProCon Taiwan** and
  [SK oceanplant](/companies/sk-oceanplant.md). Coverage includes interior and exterior of
  the transition pieces — boat landing areas, guardrails, ladders and access doors — using a
  Taiwan-specific variant of the MIR G2 system plus FL60 LED floodlights on the platforms.
  Deliveries were scheduled for August 2026.

No financial close, construction schedule or commissioning date is stated in the sources
used here, so none is recorded.
