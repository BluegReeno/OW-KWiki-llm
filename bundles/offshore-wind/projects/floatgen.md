# Overview

Floatgen is a single **2 MW** [floating offshore wind](/technology/floating-wind.md) turbine mounted on a floater from [BW Ideol](/companies/bw-ideol.md). It is one of the earliest floating wind units still operating in Europe, and serves as a long-running demonstrator of floater behaviour, mooring performance and turbine availability in real sea conditions.

# Operating Record

As reported on **28 August 2026**, the turbine has been in operation for **eight years** and has produced **40 GWh** of electricity cumulatively.

The offshoreWIND.biz excerpt available here is truncated: it gives no site location or water depth, no annual output breakdown, no availability or capacity-factor figures, no turbine supplier, no mooring or floater detail, no ownership or offtake arrangement, and no indication of how long the unit is expected to keep running.
