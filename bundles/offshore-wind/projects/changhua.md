# Overview

Ørsted's Greater Changhua offshore wind zone, located 35–60 km off the
coast of Changhua County, Taiwan, comprises four operating or
near-complete phases developed by [Ørsted](/companies/orsted.md) in
partnership with Taiwanese and Canadian investors: Greater Changhua 1
(605 MW) and Greater Changhua 2a (295 MW), commonly referred to together
as the 900 MW "GC1 & 2a" project, and Greater Changhua 2b (337 MW) and
Greater Changhua 4 (583 MW), referred to together as the 920 MW "GC2b &
4" project — for a combined 1,820 MW across the four phases.

GC1 and GC2a reached full commercial operation in April 2024, using
around 111–112 [Siemens Gamesa](/companies/siemens-gamesa.md)
SG 8.0-167 DD 8 MW turbines. GC2b and GC4 use 66 Siemens Gamesa
SG 14-236 14 MW turbines — the first deployment of this turbine model
anywhere in the world — and had all turbines installed as of January
2026, with commercial operation expected in Q3 2026 after a delay caused
by a damaged export cable. Ørsted has progressively sold down equity in
each phase to Taiwanese investors (CDPQ/Cathay PE in GC1; Cathay Life
Insurance/Cathay Power as majority partner in GC2; Cathay Life in GC4)
while retaining a long-term operations and maintenance role from the
Port of Taichung.

# GC2b & 4 completion status (August 2026)

Ørsted's first-half 2026 results, published on 20 August 2026, put the combined
920 MW GC2b & 4 project at approximately **85 per cent complete** and on track
for **full commissioning in Q3 2026**:

- All **42 turbines at Greater Changhua 4** have been commissioned, are
  connected to Taiwan's grid and are generating electricity.
- All turbines across GC2b & 4 were installed by January 2026, and all
  inter-array cables have been installed and energised.
- Installation of the **GC2b export cable** — replacing the cable damaged in
  2025, which pushed commissioning to Q3 2026 — is expected to be completed in
  the coming period.
- The project produced first power in July 2025.

# Turbine fire at Greater Changhua 4 (August 2026)

A fire was reported on a wind turbine at Greater Changhua 4 and the turbine was
taken offline, [Ørsted](/companies/orsted.md) said in a statement reported on
24 August 2026. The affected machine is one of the 66
[Siemens Gamesa](/companies/siemens-gamesa.md) SG 14-236 units shared between
GC2b and GC4 — the model's first deployment worldwide.

The available reporting does not state the cause of the fire, the extent of the
damage, whether anyone was injured, when the turbine might return to service, or
what effect — if any — this has on the Q3 2026 full-commissioning target for the
920 MW GC2b & 4 project.
