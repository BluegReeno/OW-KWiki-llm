# BalWin5

BalWin5 is one of TenneT's next-generation offshore transmission systems in the
German North Sea, built to connect large-scale offshore wind farms to the German
electricity grid. It is planned as a **2.2 GW-class**
[HVDC](/technology/hvdc-grid-connection.md) link and is scheduled for
**commissioning in 2033**.

## System layout

- Offshore converter platform in the North Sea, where the AC generated by the
  connected wind farms is converted to DC.
- Approximately **210 km of subsea cable**, routed under the island of Langeoog,
  coming ashore on the Lower Saxon coast.
- Approximately **115 km of land cable** to an **onshore converter in Bremen**,
  where the power is converted back to AC and fed into the high-voltage grid via
  the adjacent substation.

## Contracts

- **December 2025** — a **GE Vernova-Seatrium consortium** was awarded the contract
  to deliver the offshore converter platform. GE Vernova's Electrification Systems
  business supplies the full HVDC technology and the onshore and offshore converter
  stations; [Seatrium](/companies/seatrium.md) designs, builds, transports and
  installs the offshore converter platform.
- **July 2026** — [SK oceanplant](/companies/sk-oceanplant.md) secured a contract
  from Seatrium worth approximately USD 110.7 million (around EUR 96 million) for
  construction engineering, procurement, fabrication and load-out of one jacket and
  20 piles for the platform. The jacket will weigh approximately 15,000 tonnes.

No wind farms are named as connecting to BalWin5 in the available reporting, and no
construction start, load-out or offshore installation dates have been published, so
none are recorded here.
