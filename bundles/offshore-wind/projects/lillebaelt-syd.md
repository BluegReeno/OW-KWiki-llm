# Overview

Lillebælt Syd is a **165 MW** Danish offshore wind farm planned in the Little
Belt off Sønderborg, in southern Jutland. It is planned to comprise **eleven
15 MW turbines** with an expected annual generation of approximately
**650 GWh**, and is currently scheduled to enter operation in **2029/2030**.

The project began as a local wind farm initiative in 2011. Municipal utility
[SONFOR](/companies/sonfor.md) took it over from Sønderborg Municipality in
2020, with the task of developing it and finding partners. European Energy
joined in 2022 and [TotalEnergies](/companies/totalenergies.md) in 2024, the
latter as majority partner. The project company is **Lillebælt Vind A/S**.

# Consents and grid

As of August 2026 the project has:

* completed its environmental investigations;
* received an establishment (construction) permit — reported November 2024;
* secured a grid connection agreement.

No turbine supplier, foundation type or installation contractor has been
reported.

# Ownership change (August 2026)

TotalEnergies decided to withdraw from Lillebælt Syd, where it was the
majority partner. SONFOR is taking over the majority stake in Lillebælt
Vind A/S and will use the coming months to assess both potential new partners
and the project's business case, a process expected to be completed by the
**end of June 2027**. **European Energy remains a minority shareholder.**

SONFOR said the project remains relevant to electricity supply security and
local development in the Sønderborg area, while acknowledging that market
conditions for offshore wind have changed significantly.

Danish media reports cited by offshoreWIND.biz say TotalEnergies is also
exiting the **Jammerland Bugt** offshore wind project, where European Energy is
likewise the minority shareholder. No page is kept here for Jammerland Bugt:
it is named only in passing, with no capacity, status or timeline.
