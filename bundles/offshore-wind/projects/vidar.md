# Overview

**Vidar** is a 2 GW offshore wind project in Sweden's exclusive economic zone,
planned around 35 kilometres southwest of Strömstad in the northern Skagerrak, and
developed by [Vattenfall](/companies/vattenfall.md) together with Zephyr. The
layout comprises **up to 75 turbines** with an expected annual generation of up to
**7.8 TWh**.

# Permitting

On 24 July 2026 the Swedish government **approved** Vidar, alongside
[Fyrskeppet Offshore](/projects/fyrskeppet-offshore.md), while rejecting eleven
other applications over defence concerns — see
[Swedish Offshore Wind Permitting and Defence Screening](/policy/swedish-offshore-wind-permitting.md).

The permit requires the wind farm to be **completed within ten years, by 2036**, and
separate permits for the **subsea export cables** to the Swedish onshore grid must
still be obtained before construction can begin.

# Status: Approved but Paused

Shortly after the approval, Vattenfall CEO Anna Borg was cited by Reuters as saying
the company **would not proceed with Vidar at this time**, because current market
conditions do not make the project commercially viable. The project therefore holds
a valid permit with a 2036 completion deadline but no active development path as of
July 2026.
