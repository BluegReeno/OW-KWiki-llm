# Choshi Offshore Wind Farm (TEPCO RP)

A planned fixed-bottom offshore wind farm of up to 420 MW off Choshi, Chiba
Prefecture, Japan, for which [TEPCO Renewable Power](/companies/tepco.md)
started the environmental impact assessment process in August 2026.

The site is the Choshi area of [Japan's Round 1 fixed-bottom offshore wind
auction](/tenders/japan-round-1-fixed-bottom-auction.md), originally won in
December 2021 by a Mitsubishi Corporation-led consortium for a 390.6 MW wind
farm. Mitsubishi announced in August 2025 that it would not proceed with that
project — nor with its two Akita projects — citing material changes in the
macroeconomic environment, and the Japanese government has moved to re-tender
the three areas. TEPCO had competed for Choshi in 2021 in a bid with
[Ørsted](/companies/orsted.md) and lost.

## Key Facts
- **Capacity**: up to 420 MW — up to 27 turbines rated between 15 MW and 20 MW
- **Foundations**: fixed-bottom (see [fixed-bottom foundations](/technology/fixed-bottom-foundations.md))
- **Area**: approximately 4,000 hectares off Choshi; the environmental
  assessment area also partly covers Asahi
- **Developer**: TEPCO Renewable Power (TEPCO RP)
- **Construction**: planned to begin 2031 or later
- **Operation**: 2032 or later, subject to subsequent development and permitting stages

## Timeline
- **December 2021**: The Choshi area is awarded in Japan's Round 1 auction to a
  Mitsubishi Corporation-led consortium (390.6 MW); the Ørsted/TEPCO bid loses.
- **August 2025**: Mitsubishi decides to exit the three Round 1 projects,
  including Choshi.
- **20 August 2026**: TEPCO RP submits the planning-stage environmental
  consideration document for a new, up to 420 MW project on the area.
- **21 August – 25 September 2026**: Chiba Prefecture runs the public
  consultation on the document.
