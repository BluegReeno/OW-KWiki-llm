# Projects

* [Floatgen Floating Wind Demonstrator](floatgen.md) - France, a single 2 MW floating wind turbine on a BW Ideol floater; eight years in operation and 40 GWh of cumulative generation reported August 2026, one of the longest-running floating wind units in Europe.

* [Trianel Windpark Borkum I (TWB I)](trianel-windpark-borkum-i.md) - Germany, North Sea ~45 km northwest of Borkum, 200 MW across 40 Adwen AD5-116 turbines, the country's first municipal offshore wind farm, operating since 2015; around 70 MW prequalified for the German balancing energy market and supplying negative secondary control reserve from July 2026.

* [Walney 1 & 2 Offshore Wind Farms](walney.md) - UK, Irish Sea 15 km west of Barrow-in-Furness, 367 MW across 102 Siemens 3.6 MW turbines, in commercial operation since 2012, majority-owned and operated by Ørsted with Offshore Partners Walney holding 24.8%; output sold under a Good Energy PPA (July 2026) and a TotalEnergies PPA on OPW's share running to 2030 (August 2026).

* [Choshi Offshore Wind Farm (TEPCO RP)](choshi-offshore-wind.md) - Japan, off Choshi in Chiba Prefecture, up to 420 MW fixed-bottom (up to 27 turbines of 15-20 MW over ~4,000 hectares) planned by TEPCO Renewable Power on the Round 1 area dropped by Mitsubishi; EIA started 20 August 2026, construction 2031 or later.
* [Formosa 4 Offshore Wind Farm](formosa-4.md) - Taiwan, ~20 km off Miaoli County, 495 MW across 35 Siemens Gamesa 14 MW turbines, developed by Synera Renewable Energy; output fully sold under three corporate PPAs, offshore substation construction started January 2026, Glamox won the transition-piece marine lighting contract in August 2026.

* [Can Gio 2 Offshore Wind Project](can-gio-2.md) - Vietnam, 10-18 km off the Can Gio district of Ho Chi Minh City, proposed at 6 GW by a Xuan Cau Holdings / Trung Nam joint venture; feasibility study reviewed by the city's Standing People's Committee on 20 August 2026, preliminary investment put at VND 587.73 trillion (~EUR 19.2 billion), inclusion sought in the revised Power Development Plan VIII.

* [Sørlige Nordsjø II Offshore Wind Farm](sorlige-nordsjo-ii.md) - Norway, southern North Sea, up to 1.5 GW fixed-bottom, the country's first commercial offshore wind farm; Ventyr (JERA Nex BP/Ingka Investments) won the zone in March 2024 at NOK 1.15/kWh and got a twelve-month extension to 15 October 2027 for its licence application in August 2026.

* [Lillebælt Syd Offshore Wind Farm](lillebaelt-syd.md) - Denmark, Little Belt off Sønderborg, 165 MW across eleven 15 MW turbines (~650 GWh/year), permitted and grid-connected with operation targeted for 2029/2030; TotalEnergies withdrew in August 2026 and SONFOR is taking the majority stake while seeking a new partner by end-June 2027.

* [Bałtyk 2 & 3 Offshore Wind Farms](baltyk-2-3.md) - Poland, Baltic Sea, 1.4 GW, Equinor/Polenergia 50/50 JV with Siemens Gamesa turbines; offshore construction started February 2026 and Sif loaded out the final monopiles in August 2026, first power expected 2027.

* [Dadu Offshore Wind Farms (Dadu I and Dadu II)](dadu.md) - Taiwan, off Changhua County, two Ørsted projects in environmental permitting: Dadu I up to 924 MW (EIS recently completed) and Dadu II up to 2,002 MW over ~234.6 km² with 14-22 MW turbines, entering environmental assessment August 2026.
* [Rudong Offshore Wind Farm (Jiangsu)](rudong.md) - China, Yellow Sea off Rudong county, Jiangsu province, 800 MW operated by China Three Gorges; passed 10 TWh of cumulative generation, reported August 2026.
* [Revolution Wind Offshore Wind Farm](revolution-wind.md) - US, Atlantic off Rhode Island, 704 MW, Ørsted/Skyborn Renewables JV supplying Rhode Island and Connecticut; 65 Siemens Gamesa 11 MW turbines, first power March 2026, 61 turbines installed and full commissioning due H2 2026.
* [Borkum Riffgrund 3 Offshore Wind Farm](borkum-riffgrund-3.md) - Germany, North Sea, 913 MW, Ørsted's largest German wind farm with Nuveen Infrastructure; 83 Siemens Gamesa 11 MW turbines, first power December 2025, more than 99% complete and due for full commissioning in Q3 2026.
* [Sunrise Wind Offshore Wind Farm](sunrise-wind.md) - US, Atlantic off New York, 924 MW wholly owned by Ørsted; 50% complete at 30 June 2026 with 20 of 84 turbines installed, turbine commissioning H2 2026 and full operation not before H2 2027.
* [East Anglia THREE Offshore Wind Farm](east-anglia-three.md) - UK, southern North Sea off Suffolk, 1.4 GW, ScottishPower Renewables (Iberdrola) with Masdar; foundation installation completed August 2026.
* [BC-Wind Offshore Wind Farm](bc-wind.md) - Poland, Baltic Sea ~23 km offshore, up to 390 MW across 26 turbines, Ocean Winds (EDPR/ENGIE); at financial close since end-2025, onshore works underway and first power expected 2028, with Bilfinger appointed technical advisor for the execution phase in August 2026.
* [Hai Long Offshore Wind Farm](hai-long.md) - Taiwan, 45-70 km off Changhua County, more than 1 GW across three phases, Mitsui & Co./Northland Power/Gentari with 73 Siemens Gamesa 14 MW turbines; phases 2A and 2B installed, Hai Long 3 due online end-2026, TWD 55 billion of incremental financing raised with 11 new lenders in August 2026.
* [He Dreiht Offshore Wind Farm](he-dreiht.md) - Germany, North Sea, 960 MW, EnBW with Vestas turbines; all turbines installed as of 12 August 2026.
* [Haiyou Anlan Floating Wind Turbine (Lufeng)](haiyou-anlan-lufeng.md) - China, a single 16 MW floating wind turbine operated by CNOOC to power its Lufeng oilfield; entered operation August 2026, one of the largest floating turbines deployed worldwide.
* [Baltica 2 Offshore Wind Farm](baltica-2.md) - Poland, Baltic Sea, ~1.5 GW, PGE/Ørsted 50/50 JV and the larger phase of the Baltica complex; Van Oord completed all 111 monopile foundations, F.lli Righini delivered the secondary structure installation tools and Northern Helicopter won the medevac contract in August 2026, commissioning scheduled for H2 2027.
* [Bornholm Energy Island](bornholm-energy-island.md) - Denmark, Baltic Sea energy island centred on Bornholm, developed by TSO Energinet to collect Baltic offshore wind and link it onward; Per Aarsleff won a EUR 495 million design-and-build contract for the onshore facilities, August 2026.
* [North Sea I Mid (Nordsøen Midt) Offshore Wind Farm](nordsoen-midt.md) - Denmark, North Sea ~20 km off Hvide Sande, at least 1 GW, Vattenfall; won August 2026 at a DKK 504/MWh CfD strike price, operational by 2032.
* [Hesselø Offshore Wind Farm](hesselo.md) - Denmark, Kattegat between Denmark and Sweden, at least 800 MW, Vattenfall; won August 2026 at a DKK 542/MWh CfD strike price, operational by 2032.
* [Gennaker Offshore Wind Farm](gennaker.md) - Germany, Baltic Sea off Mecklenburg-Vorpommern, 976.5 MW, Skyborn Renewables; Dajin Heavy Industry is fabricating the project's 63 transition pieces and OEG won the subsea corrosion protection contract, August 2026.
* [Llŷr 1 and Llŷr 2 Floating Wind Demonstration Projects](llyr.md) - Wales, Celtic Sea off Pembrokeshire, two 100 MW floating wind test and demonstration projects by Floventis Energy (Cierco Energy / SBM Offshore); Agreement for Lease signed with The Crown Estate August 2026 and Section 36 consent granted by the Welsh government on 7 August 2026.
* [Morgan Offshore Wind Project](morgan.md) - UK, Irish Sea, up to 1.5 GW, Round 4 site consented in 2025 but relinquished by EnBW/JERA Nex BP; re-tendered by The Crown Estate in 2026.
* [Dogger Bank Wind Farm](dogger-bank.md) - UK, North Sea, 3.6 GW across Phases A/B/C, the world's largest offshore wind farm, SSE Renewables/Equinor/Vårgrønn; Phase B turbine installation ongoing, Phase C offshore works from 2027, completion expected mid-2028, marshalled from Maraen Port of Nigg.
* [Hornsea 3 Offshore Wind Farm](hornsea-3.md) - UK, North Sea ~120 km off Norfolk, 2.9 GW, Ørsted with Apollo-managed funds and Mubadala; 197 Siemens Gamesa SG 14-236 DD turbines on XXL monopiles, ~30% complete in August 2026 with commissioning due Q4 2027-Q1 2028; co-located 300 MW/600 MWh Iceni BESS starts operations Q1 2027.
* [Hornsea 2](hornsea-2.md) - UK, North Sea, 1,320 MW, Ørsted, fully operational since 2022.
* [Vineyard Wind 1](vineyard-wind-1.md) - US, Massachusetts, 806 MW, first major commercial-scale US offshore wind farm, Avangrid/CIP.
* [Empire Wind 1](empire-wind-1.md) - US, New York, 810 MW, Equinor, under construction, first power expected late 2026.
* [Saint-Brieuc](saint-brieuc.md) - France, Brittany, 496 MW, Ailes Marines/Iberdrola, fully commissioned May 2024.
* [Saint-Nazaire](saint-nazaire.md) - France, Loire-Atlantique, 480 MW, EDF Renouvelables/EIH, first offshore wind farm commissioned in France.
* [Changhua Offshore Wind Farms (Greater Changhua)](changhua.md) - Taiwan, 1,820 MW across four phases, Ørsted with Taiwanese partners; GC1 & 2a operational since April 2024, GC2b & 4 ~85% complete in August 2026 with all GC4 turbines commissioned and full commissioning due Q3 2026; one GC4 turbine taken offline after a fire in August 2026.
* [Nordlicht Offshore Wind Farms (Nordlicht I & II)](nordlicht.md) - Germany, North Sea, 1.6 GW (980 MW + 630 MW), Vattenfall, offshore construction started July 2026 (first monopile in July, first transition piece installed August 2026), SOV chartered from LD Armateurs, online 2028.
* [Nordergründe Offshore Wind Farm](nordergrunde.md) - Germany, operating offshore wind farm, now wholly owned by Skyborn Renewables after it acquired the remaining 70% stake.
* [Dieppe-Le Tréport Offshore Wind Farm](dieppe-le-treport.md) - France, Normandy, 496 MW, Engie-led consortium/Ocean Winds, all jacket foundations installed June 2026 by DEME Offshore.
* [Baltic Power Offshore Wind Farm](baltic-power.md) - Poland, Baltic Sea, ~1,140 MW, ORLEN/Northland Power JV, 76 Vestas V236-15.0 MW turbines; began delivering Poland's first offshore wind electricity to the grid July 2026; Boskalis's Windpiper mobilised for rock installation there end-July 2026.
* [Buchan Offshore Wind Project](buchan-floating-wind.md) - Scotland, ~1 GW floating wind farm off Aberdeenshire (ScotWind), BayWa r.e./BW Ideol/Elicio JV; Kent appointed Owner's Engineer July 2026.
* [Gwynt Glas Floating Wind Project](gwynt-glas.md) - Wales, Celtic Sea, up to 1.5 GW floating wind farm, EDF Power Solutions/ESB JV with DP Energy; EIA Scoping Report submitted July 2026, Crown Estate Round 5 lease, Sumitomo joined as equity partner August 2026.
* [Inch Cape Offshore Wind Farm](inch-cape.md) - Scotland, North Sea, 1.1 GW, ESB/Red Rock Renewables JV, 72 Vestas V236-15.0 MW turbines on 54 monopiles and 18 jackets; monopiles completed August 2026, Seaway7's Alfa Lift starting jacket pin piles and Cadeler's Wind Mover due to begin turbine installation in November 2026, first power expected late 2026.
* [Northwester 2 Offshore Wind Farm](northwester-2.md) - Belgium, North Sea, 219 MW, operating since 2020, now wholly owned by JERA Nex BP after it bought out Sumitomo Corporation's 30% stake, July 2026.
* [Nobelwind Offshore Wind Farm](nobelwind.md) - Belgium, North Sea, 165 MW, operating since 2017 (Belwind phase 2), JERA Nex BP raised its stake to 80.1% by acquiring Sumitomo Corporation's 39.02% interest, July 2026.
* [Curonian Nord Offshore Wind Farm](curonian-nord.md) - Lithuania, Baltic Sea, 700 MW, Lithuania's first offshore wind project, developed by Ignitis Renewables; received positive EIA decision July 2026, construction permit expected 2027.
* [Northwind Offshore Wind Farm](northwind.md) - Belgium, North Sea, 216 MW, operating since 2014, owned by Aspiravi Offshore and Sumitomo Corporation; Sumitomo divesting its stake to Belgian consortium Publiwind, expected close FY2026.
* [Hollandse Kust West VI Offshore Wind Farm](hollandse-kust-west-vi.md) - Netherlands, North Sea, 760 MW, subsidy-free tender, Ecowende (Shell/Eneco/Chubu Electric Power); started delivering power July 2026, 52 Vestas V236 turbines, Van Oord completed nature-inclusive scour protection July 2026.
* [Jindo Offshore Wind Project Cluster](jindo.md) - South Korea, 3.2 GW cluster off Jindo County (Jeonnam-Gwangju), developer Pacifico Energy Korea, named sole developer of the 2.13 GW Integrated Cluster Phase 2 (Manho, Jindo Baram) in August 2026; local supply chain MOU with EEW KHPC, KMC Line and CS Energy.
* [Éoliennes Flottantes du Golfe du Lion (EFGL)](efgl-golfe-du-lion.md) - France, Mediterranean, 30 MW floating offshore wind farm in the Gulf of Lion, developed by Ocean Winds; reached full power July 2026, Principle Power awarded floating-foundation O&M contract.
* [Bellrock Offshore Wind Farm](bellrock.md) - Floating offshore wind project developed by Nadara; electrical infrastructure studies underway and a geophysical survey of a potential export cable corridor starting July 2026.
* [Thor Offshore Wind Farm](thor.md) - Denmark, North Sea off Jutland, 1.1 GW, Denmark's largest, RWE (51%)/Norges Bank Investment Management (49%) JV with 72 Siemens Gamesa turbines; turbine installation passed halfway (36 of 72) July 2026, full commissioning expected spring 2027.
* [Caledonia Offshore Wind Farm](caledonia.md) - Scotland, Moray Firth, 2 GW (North and South Caledonia), Ocean Winds (EDPR/ENGIE); received offshore consent from the Scottish government July 2026, offshore construction expected 2030 subject to CfD and FID.
* [Shinan Ui Offshore Wind Project](shinan-ui.md) - South Korea, 390 MW off Shinan County (Jeollanam-do), developed by Hanwha Ocean; construction started July 2026.
* [Nordseecluster Offshore Wind Farm](nordseecluster.md) - Germany, North Sea, 1.6 GW in two phases (A: 660 MW, B: 900 MW), RWE with Vestas turbines; Nordseecluster A fed its first electricity into the German grid July 2026.
* [Oga–Katagami–Akita Offshore Wind Project](oga-katagami-akita.md) - Japan, off Akita Prefecture, 315 MW fixed-bottom (21 x 15 MW Vestas turbines), JERA Nex BP/J-POWER/Tohoku Electric/Itochu consortium; Bureau Veritas Japan certificate of conformity July 2026, first JFE Engineering monopile rolled out August 2026, full commissioning 2028.
* [Zeevonk Offshore Wind Farm](zeevonk.md) - Netherlands, North Sea (IJmuiden Ver), Vattenfall/CIP Zeevonk JV; DEME Offshore won the foundation installation contract for the first phase, July 2026.
* [Fyrskeppet Offshore](fyrskeppet-offshore.md) - Sweden, southern Gulf of Bothnia ~54 km east of Söderhamn, Skyborn Renewables; permitted July 2026 for up to 93 turbines (8-11 TWh/year), completion deadline 2036.
* [Vidar Offshore Wind Farm](vidar.md) - Sweden, northern Skagerrak ~35 km southwest of Strömstad, 2 GW / up to 75 turbines, Vattenfall and Zephyr; permitted July 2026 but paused by Vattenfall on commercial grounds.
* [Doordewind Offshore Wind Zone](doordewind.md) - Netherlands, North Sea, ~580 km² zone about 77 km off the north coast planned for 4 GW across two 2 GW sites and the first zone to connect to Eemshaven; RVO tendered a EUR 700,000 benthic survey in July 2026 to build site data for future wind farms.
* [Fengmiao 1 Offshore Wind Farm](fengmiao-1.md) - 495 MW Taiwanese project developed by Copenhagen Infrastructure Partners; offshore substation and export cable works completed in August 2026.
* [Coastal Virginia Offshore Wind (CVOW)](coastal-virginia-offshore-wind.md) - US, Atlantic off Virginia Beach, 2.6 GW, the largest offshore wind farm under construction in the United States, wholly owned by Dominion Energy with Siemens Gamesa turbines; reported 81% complete August 2026, final turbine expected by end of 2027.
* [Culzean Floating Wind Pilot](culzean-floating-wind.md) - UK, North Sea off Scotland, single 3 MW floating turbine powering the Culzean gas platform (INTOG award to TotalEnergies, now led by NEO NEXT+ Energy); turbine-floater integration completed and tow-out imminent, July 2026.
* [TetraSpar Demonstrator](tetraspar-demonstrator.md) - Norway, METCentre off Karmøy, single ~3.6 MW floating turbine on Stiesdal Offshore's modular tetrahedral floater (with RWE, Shell, TEPCO RP), in the water since 2021; decommissioning work started August 2026.
* [Yuetuo Island Offshore Wind Farm (Phase I)](yuetuo-island.md) - China, off Leting in Tangshan (Hebei), 304 MW Guodian project; first site to take Siemens Energy's lightweight HV-TSU integrated offshore substation, installed August 2026 on a ZPMC-built topside and jacket.
* [Dudgeon Offshore Wind Farm](dudgeon.md) - UK, North Sea 32 km off North Norfolk, 402 MW with 67 Siemens Gamesa 6 MW turbines, commissioned late 2017; Equinor/Masdar/China Resources JV operated by Equinor, served by ESVAGT's SOV Esvagt Njord, fitted with NextOcean's WavePredictor in August 2026.
* [BalWin5 Offshore Grid Connection](balwin5.md) - Germany, North Sea, TenneT 2.2 GW-class HVDC converter platform with an onshore converter in Bremen (~210 km subsea + ~115 km land cable); GE Vernova-Seatrium consortium awarded December 2025, SK oceanplant contracted for the jacket July 2026, commissioning 2033.
