# Overview

The Jiangsu Rudong offshore wind project is an 800 MW operating wind farm off
Rudong county in Jiangsu province, eastern China, developed and operated by
[China Three Gorges](/companies/china-three-gorges.md) (CTG).

# Generation Milestone (August 2026)

On 19 August 2026 it was reported that the project had generated more than
10 TWh of electricity since entering service — one of the few cumulative
output milestones recorded in this bundle, most Chinese entries here being
construction or equipment events such as the substation installation at
[Yuetuo Island](/projects/yuetuo-island.md).
