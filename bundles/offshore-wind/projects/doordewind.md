# Overview

Doordewind is a designated Dutch offshore wind farm zone in the North Sea,
located approximately **77 kilometres off the north coast of the Netherlands**.
It covers about **580 square kilometres** and is planned to host a total of
**4 GW split over two sites of 2 GW each**. It will be the first Dutch zone to
connect to shore at **Eemshaven**.

The zone is one of three new areas designated by the Dutch government in 2022,
which together added 10.7 GW of prospective offshore wind capacity. Doordewind
sits within the Dutch offshore wind roadmap, which currently targets **23 GW**
of installed capacity.

# Site development and surveys

Site data for the zone is being prepared ahead of any wind farm tender, in the
Dutch model where the state surveys the site before developers bid.

- **December 2025** — TenneT selected NjordIC for unexploded ordnance (UXO)
  consultancy work at Doordewind, part of the [grid connection](/technology/hvdc-grid-connection.md)
  preparation towards Eemshaven.
- **July 2026** — The Dutch Ministry of Economic Affairs launched a tender,
  commissioned by the **Netherlands Enterprise Agency (RVO)**, for a **benthic
  survey** of the zone. The survey will collect factual benthic data to verify a
  desktop reef detection study (results expected July 2026) and establish a
  baseline of the benthic environment across the area.

# Benthic survey tender (2026)

| Item | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Contracting authority | Dutch Ministry of Economic Affairs, commissioned by RVO |
| Scope | Benthic survey; verify desktop reef detection study, establish benthic baseline |
| Estimated value | EUR 700,000 excluding VAT |
| Bid deadline | 1 October 2026 |
| Performance period | 10 December 2026 – 31 December 2027 |
| Extension option | Until 1 May 2028 if unforeseen external circumstances cause delay |
| Publication | Contract notice in the Official Journal of the EU (OJEU/TED) |

The resulting data will support the preparation of site data for the future
offshore wind farms planned within the zone, alongside other Dutch tender
activity such as the [Gasunie 10-year offshore wind PPA tender](/tenders/gasunie-offshore-wind-ppa-2026.md).
