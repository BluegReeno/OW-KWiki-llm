# Overview

Sunrise Wind is a 924 MW offshore wind farm under construction in US federal
waters off New York, contracted to supply New York State. It is wholly owned by
[Ørsted](/companies/orsted.md), which acquired partner Eversource's share in
2024 when Eversource exited US offshore wind.

# Status

As reported in Ørsted's first-half 2026 results (20 August 2026):

- Overall project completion has reached **50 per cent**.
- **20 of the 84 turbines** had been installed as of **30 June 2026**, with
  foundations installed at **77 positions**.
- Turbine **commissioning is due to start in the second half of 2026**, but the
  project is **not expected to enter full operation until the second half of
  2027**.

Construction was interrupted in December 2025, when the Trump administration
issued stop-work orders on several US projects under construction, including
Sunrise Wind and [Revolution Wind](/projects/revolution-wind.md), citing
national security grounds. Courts ruled the suspensions unlawful and work
resumed — see [US BOEM leasing process](/policy/us-boem-leasing-process.md).
