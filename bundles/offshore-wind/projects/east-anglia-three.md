# East Anglia THREE Offshore Wind Farm

East Anglia THREE is a **1.4 GW** offshore wind farm under construction in the
southern North Sea off the Suffolk coast, part of the East Anglia zone developed
by **ScottishPower Renewables**, the UK arm of
[Iberdrola](/companies/iberdrola.md). **Masdar** holds a minority stake in the
project. It is the larger sibling of East Anglia Two, which appears in this
bundle through a [Smulders/Palfinger davit crane
contract](/digests/2026-07-23-smulders-palfinger-east-anglia-two-davit-cranes.md).

The project has UK offtake support under the
[Contracts for Difference scheme](/policy/uk-contracts-for-difference-scheme.md).
