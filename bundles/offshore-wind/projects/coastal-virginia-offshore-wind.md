# Overview

Coastal Virginia Offshore Wind (CVOW) is a 2.6 GW fixed-bottom offshore wind
farm off Virginia Beach, on the US Atlantic coast. It is developed and wholly
owned by [Dominion Energy](/companies/dominion-energy.md), which is building
it as a regulated utility asset for its Virginia customers, and it is the
largest offshore wind farm under construction in the United States.

Turbines are supplied by [Siemens Gamesa](/companies/siemens-gamesa.md) from
its SG 14-222 DD direct-drive platform.

# Construction Status

- **December 2025** — CVOW Commercial was one of five US projects under
  construction hit by federal stop-work orders citing national security and
  radar-interference concerns (see [US BOEM leasing process](/policy/us-boem-leasing-process.md));
  Dominion Energy was among the parties pursuing legal challenges.
- **August 2026** — Dominion Energy reported the project **81% complete** and
  revised its completion timeline, with the **final turbine now expected by the
  end of 2027**.

The reporting available gives no revised full-commercial-operation date beyond
the final-turbine milestone, and no updated cost figure or reason for the
schedule change.
