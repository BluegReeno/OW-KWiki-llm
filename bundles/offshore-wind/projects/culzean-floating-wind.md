# Overview

The **Culzean Floating Wind pilot** is a single 3 MW [floating offshore wind](/technology/floating-wind.md) turbine to be installed about 2 km west of the Culzean gas platform, roughly 220 km off Scotland's east coast in the North Sea. Once commissioned it is expected to supply around 20% of the platform's electricity demand, displacing part of the power currently generated by the platform's gas turbines and cutting the associated greenhouse gas emissions.

The pilot was one of the 13 projects awarded seabed rights by Crown Estate Scotland in the 2023 **Innovation and Targeted Oil & Gas (INTOG)** leasing round, with [TotalEnergies](https://totalenergies.com/) — operator of the Culzean gas field — securing the rights. TotalEnergies launched the project in August 2024 and fabrication began in November 2024. The project is now led by **NEO NEXT+ Energy**, the independent UK oil and gas producer formed on 30 March 2026 from the merger of NEO Energy, Repsol UK and TotalEnergies' UK upstream business.

Beyond powering the platform, the pilot is intended as a demonstrator: it will generate operational data and experience for future commercial floating projects.

# Technology and Contractors

- **Floater**: a semi-submersible designed and developed by **Ocergy**, its OCG-Wind design — this is the first commercial deployment of that technology. Ocergy states the hull is designed for modular fabrication and optimised for rapid assembly.
- **Turbine**: a [Vestas](/companies/vestas.md) unit (tower, nacelle and blades).
- **EPC contractor**: **Archer**, which reported in July 2026 that turbine integration onto the floater was complete, working with project partners Global Maritime, Navitas Renewables, Wergeland Group, Sarens and Archer Vertikal.

# Status

As of **31 July 2026**, integration of the turbine with the floating foundation is complete and the assembled unit is ready to be towed to its offshore location. No tow date, installation window, mooring campaign schedule or commissioning date has been reported.
