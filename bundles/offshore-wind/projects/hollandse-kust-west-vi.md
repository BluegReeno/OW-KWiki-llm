# Hollandse Kust West VI Offshore Wind Farm

A 760 MW offshore wind farm located roughly 53 km off the Dutch coast, in
Site VI of the Hollandse Kust (west) wind farm zone. It is developed and
operated by [Ecowende](/companies/ecowende.md), a joint venture of Shell,
Eneco, and Chubu Electric Power. The project was awarded to Shell and Eneco
in a subsidy-free tender in December 2022, with Chubu later joining via a
30% stake acquisition.

The wind farm will comprise 52 [Vestas](/companies/vestas.md) V236-15.0 MW
turbines, seven of which will carry a single red-painted blade to test
whether the colour contrast reduces bird-collision risk. Billed as the most
ecological offshore wind farm yet, it also features a dedicated bird
corridor, wider turbine spacing, elevated nacelles, adaptive curtailment,
and AI-assisted radar monitoring for birds and bats.

[Van Oord](/companies/van-oord.md) is contracted for transport and
installation of foundations and turbines and installation of the inter-array
cables. It installed the monopile foundations, completing the industry's
first "silent installation" campaign in April 2026.

As of July 2026, the wind farm has started delivering power, marking first
electricity generation. It is expected to be fully operational by the end of
2026 and to supply roughly 3% of Dutch electricity demand.

## Scour protection

In July 2026, Van Oord completed the nature-inclusive scour protection
campaign, started in September 2025. Using its subsea rock installation
vessels (SRIVs) Bravenes and Nordnes, it placed 300,000 tonnes of rock to
protect turbine foundations and cable infrastructure, including stabilisation
of all cable protection systems and eco-rock berms. The design incorporates
ecological bays, habitat-forming crevices and varied rock sizes — including
rocks up to 450 kg, described as the largest ever installed by an SRIV — to
create habitats for marine life.
