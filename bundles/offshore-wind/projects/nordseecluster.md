# Overview

Nordseecluster is a 1.6 GW offshore wind development in the German North Sea,
built by [RWE](/companies/rwe-offshore-wind.md) in two phases:

- **Nordseecluster A** — 660 MW
- **Nordseecluster B** — 900 MW

[Vestas](/companies/vestas.md) is the turbine supplier for the cluster, with a
firm 660 MW order for Nordseecluster A and a preferred-supplier position across
the wider 1.6 GW programme (at least 104 turbines expected from 2026).
[DEME Offshore](/companies/deme-offshore.md) is engaged on the offshore
installation side, with its new-generation installation vessels entering service
in 2026 on Nordseecluster among other projects.

# Construction Status

- **2026** — first turbine installed at Nordseecluster A.
- **22 July 2026** — the first electricity generated by Nordseecluster A was fed
  into the German grid, marking the project's first power milestone. Turbine
  installation and commissioning at the A phase continue; Nordseecluster B
  follows in a later phase.
