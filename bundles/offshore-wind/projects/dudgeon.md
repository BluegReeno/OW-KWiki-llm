# Dudgeon Offshore Wind Farm

## Key facts

* **Capacity**: 402 MW
* **Location**: North Sea, 32 km off the coast of North Norfolk, East Anglia, UK
* **Turbines**: 67 x [Siemens Gamesa](/companies/siemens-gamesa.md) 6 MW
* **Status**: fully commissioned late 2017
* **Ownership**: Dudgeon Offshore Wind Limited, a joint venture between
  [Equinor](/companies/equinor.md), Masdar and China Resources (Holdings)
* **Operator**: Equinor

## Operations and maintenance

[ESVAGT](/companies/esvagt.md)'s service operation vessel **Esvagt Njord**
has been deployed on Dudgeon for O&M since **autumn 2016**, on a contract
extended in **2021**.

* **August 2026** — [NextOcean](/companies/nextocean.md) won a contract to
  equip Esvagt Njord with its **WavePredictor** vessel-motion prediction
  system (motion radar plus software), scheduled for installation that month,
  to support personnel transfers at Dudgeon in challenging sea states.
