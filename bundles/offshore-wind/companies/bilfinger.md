# Overview

**Bilfinger** is an international industrial services provider whose offshore wind work centres on technical advisory and construction supervision rather than fabrication or installation. It maintains a North Region team in Poland, quoted in the trade press through Operational Unit Manager Jarosław Gałek and Department Manager – North Region PL Bartlomiej Bil.

# Offshore Wind Contracts

- **BC-Wind, Poland (August 2026)** — selected by [Ocean Winds](/companies/ocean-winds.md) to deliver technical advisory services during the execution phase of the [BC-Wind](/projects/bc-wind.md) project in the Polish Baltic Sea. The scope covers oversight of key offshore construction activities — supervision of turbine installation, offshore structures and export cable installation — plus compliance with technical requirements, permits and applicable regulations, and quality, safety and operational efficiency throughout the project lifecycle. Bilfinger had supported the same client on the onshore part of the project in the preceding months.
