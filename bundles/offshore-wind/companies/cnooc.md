# Overview

**China National Offshore Oil Corporation (CNOOC)** is China's state-owned offshore oil and gas producer. Its relevance to offshore wind is as an *end user and operator* rather than a developer selling into a power market: CNOOC deploys wind generation to electrify its own offshore production facilities, displacing fuel burned by platform generators.

# Offshore Wind Activity

- **Haiyou Anlan (16 MW floating turbine, Lufeng oilfield)** — CNOOC started operating the 16 MW *Haiyou Anlan* [floating wind](/technology/floating-wind.md) turbine, which powers its Lufeng oilfield, on **10 August 2026**. See [Haiyou Anlan Floating Wind Turbine](/projects/haiyou-anlan-lufeng.md).

The item reporting this is a short excerpt; no detail on CNOOC's wider offshore wind portfolio, floater supplier, turbine OEM or further electrification plans is asserted here.
