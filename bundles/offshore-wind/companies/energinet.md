# Energinet

Energinet is Denmark's state-owned transmission system operator for electricity
and gas. For offshore wind it is the party that builds and owns the
[grid connection](/technology/hvdc-grid-connection.md) — the substations,
converter facilities and export links that bring Danish offshore wind ashore —
making it the Danish counterpart to [50Hertz](/companies/50hertz.md) in the
German Baltic and TenneT in the German and Dutch North Sea. It is separate from
the Danish Energy Agency, which runs the country's offshore wind capacity
tenders such as the
[2.8 GW CfD round](/tenders/denmark-2-8gw-cfd-tender-2026.md).

## Bornholm Energy Island (August 2026)

Energinet is the owner and contracting authority of
[Bornholm Energy Island](/projects/bornholm-energy-island.md). On 5 August 2026
offshoreWIND.biz reported that it had awarded Danish contractor
[Per Aarsleff A/S](/companies/aarsleff.md) a **design-and-build contract worth
EUR 495 million** for the project's onshore facilities.
