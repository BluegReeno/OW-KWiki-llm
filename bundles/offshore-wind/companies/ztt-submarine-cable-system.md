# ZTT Submarine Cable & System

ZTT Submarine Cable & System is the Chinese submarine cable arm of the ZTT
group, supplying subsea cable systems and building out its own installation
capability — the same combination of manufacturing plus vessel ownership seen
at [LS Cable & System](/companies/ls-cable-system.md),
[Taihan Cable & Solution](/companies/taihan-cable-solution.md),
[NKT](/companies/nkt.md) and [Prysmian](/companies/prysmian.md).

# Vessels

* **August 2026** — launched *Lan Hai Ling Hang*, a new cable-laying vessel for
  offshore wind. The available excerpt does not disclose the building yard,
  vessel dimensions, carousel or turntable capacity, delivery date, or the
  projects the vessel will serve.
