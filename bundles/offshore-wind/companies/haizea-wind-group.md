# Overview

Haizea Wind Group is a Spanish manufacturer of offshore wind foundations and
turbine towers, producing XXL monopiles at its facility at the **Port of
Bilbao**. The company says it is currently the only manufacturer in Spain
capable of producing monopiles of that size, placing it alongside
[EEW Group](/companies/eew-group.md) and
[Steelwind Nordenham](/companies/steelwind-nordenham.md) in the European
[fixed-bottom foundation](/technology/fixed-bottom-foundations.md) supply
chain — and giving that chain an Iberian node next to its northern-European
and Asian ones.

# Key contracts

- **[Hornsea 3](/projects/hornsea-3.md) (UK, 2.9 GW)** — supplying XXL
  monopiles alongside [Dajin Heavy Industry](/companies/dajin-heavy-industry.md).
  On 6 August 2026 Haizea delivered what it describes as the largest monopiles
  ever manufactured in Southern Europe and the largest structures ever built in
  the Iberian Peninsula: over 105 metres long, 11 metres in diameter and more
  than 2,300 tonnes each. Load-out at the Port of Bilbao was performed by
  [Cadeler](/companies/cadeler.md) (load-out and load-in), with Mammoet moving
  the monopiles inside the port on self-propelled modular transporters (SPMTs)
  and logistics support from Grúas Aguado. First Hornsea 3 monopiles from Spain
  arrived in the UK in February 2026.
- **Norfolk Vanguard West (UK)** — started monopile manufacturing, June 2026.
- **Dieppe–Le Tréport (France)** — completed production of the project's
  turbine towers, May 2026.

Contract values, monopile counts per project and delivery schedules were not
reported in the source items.
