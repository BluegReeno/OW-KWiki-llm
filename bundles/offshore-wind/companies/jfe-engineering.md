# JFE Engineering Corporation

Tokyo-based engineering company that has entered offshore wind foundation
manufacturing through its Kasaoka Monopile Plant, built in March 2024 and,
according to the company, currently the only monopile manufacturing base in
Japan.

## Kasaoka Monopile Plant

- Built March 2024
- Described by JFE Engineering as Japan's only monopile manufacturing base

## Oga–Katagami–Akita

In December 2025 JFE Engineering was awarded a contract for foundation
manufacturing and transportation services by Kajima Corporation, which had
itself been contracted by the developer in October 2025 for the manufacturing,
procurement, transportation and installation of the turbine foundations at the
315 MW [Oga–Katagami–Akita](/projects/oga-katagami-akita.md) offshore wind farm.

In August 2026 the company rolled out what it says is Japan's first
domestically manufactured monopile for an offshore wind farm. The
Oga–Katagami–Akita monopiles have a maximum diameter of approximately 11
metres, a length of approximately 80 metres and a weight of approximately
2,000 tonnes.
