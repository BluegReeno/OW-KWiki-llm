# Overview

Encomara is a floating wind and subsea engineering company headquartered in Aberdeen, Scotland. It develops connection technology for [floating offshore wind](/technology/floating-wind.md), an area where mooring, dynamic cable and quick-connect hardware remain among the least standardised parts of the value chain.

# Squid Connection System

Squid is Encomara's floating wind connection system. In August 2026 the company began preparations to trial the first Squid unit in inshore Scottish waters — the concept's first in-water test campaign. Inshore trials are a standard de-risking step ahead of open-water demonstration on a working floating platform.

Details of the trial site, schedule and partners were not disclosed in the reporting available here.

# Commercialisation (August 2026)

On 28 August 2026 Encomara said Squid had reached **Technology Readiness Level 7/8** and commercialisation — three weeks after the first unit went into inshore Scottish waters. TRL 7/8 places the system at system prototype demonstration in an operational environment moving into a qualified, complete system, i.e. ready to be sold rather than only tested.

Nothing further is stated in the material available here: no first commercial order, customer, project, pricing, water depth or load rating, and no named qualification or certification body.
