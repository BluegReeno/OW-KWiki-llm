# Role

Sif Group is a Netherlands-based manufacturer of large-diameter rolled and
welded steel tubulars, and one of Europe's principal suppliers of
[monopile foundations](/technology/fixed-bottom-foundations.md) for offshore
wind. Its production is centred on Roermond, with a deep-water fabrication and
marshalling terminal at Maasvlakte 2 in the Port of Rotterdam from which
finished monopiles are loaded out directly onto installation vessels.

# Recent Activity

- **August 2026**: Loaded out the final monopiles for the 1.4 GW
  [Bałtyk 2 & 3](/projects/baltyk-2-3.md) offshore wind farms in the Polish
  Baltic Sea, developed by [Equinor](/companies/equinor.md) and Polenergia,
  completing its foundation supply campaign for the projects.
