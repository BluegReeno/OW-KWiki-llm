# Windward Offshore

Windward Offshore is a German owner and operator of offshore wind service
vessels. Its offering is built around a series of four hybrid Commissioning
Service Operation Vessels (CSOVs) ordered from Norwegian shipbuilder
[Vard](/companies/vard.md), which the company describes as the backbone of
its integrated service offering to the offshore wind sector.

## CSOV Newbuild Series

| Vessel | Status |
| --- | --- |
| Windward Athens | Delivered October 2025 (first of the series) |
| Windward Paris | Delivered early 2026; reported as due to work for [RWE](/companies/rwe-offshore-wind.md) |
| Windward Munich | Delivered July 2026 from Vard's Vung Tau yard in Vietnam |
| Windward Hamburg | Under construction |

**Windward Munich** is described by the company as an advanced hybrid CSOV
combining high levels of comfort with energy-efficient technology: an
electrically driven, integrated motion-compensated gangway and crane, a
helideck, and accommodation for up to 120 people on board. It had secured a
first charter assignment due to start shortly after delivery, with no further
details disclosed.

Windward Offshore sits alongside
[Bernhard Schulte Offshore](/companies/bernhard-schulte-offshore.md) and
[Marco Polo Marine](/companies/marco-polo-marine.md) in the bundle's CSOV
operator group.
