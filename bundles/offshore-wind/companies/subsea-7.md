# Subsea 7

Subsea 7 is an offshore engineering, construction and services contractor
for the energy industry. Its renewables business, Seaway7, is a leading
offshore wind installation contractor, covering fixed-bottom
[foundations](/technology/fixed-bottom-foundations.md), inter-array and
export cable installation, and heavy transport and installation scopes.

## Proposed merger with Saipem

A proposed merger between [Saipem](/companies/saipem.md) and Subsea 7 is
facing deeper scrutiny in the European Union, with the European Commission
flagging competition concerns (reported 27 July 2026). The deal would
consolidate two of the largest offshore installation contractors serving
oil and gas and offshore wind.

The available excerpt does not state the review's procedural stage,
deadline, the specific markets of concern, or any remedies discussed.

## Seaway7 projects

- **[Inch Cape](/projects/inch-cape.md) (UK, 1.1 GW)** — Seaway7 is set to
  begin installing the **pin piles** for the wind farm's jacket
  [foundations](/technology/fixed-bottom-foundations.md) with the vessel
  **Alfa Lift** (28 August 2026), after the monopile campaign was
  completed earlier in the month.
- **East Anglia Three (UK)** — foundation installation scope.
