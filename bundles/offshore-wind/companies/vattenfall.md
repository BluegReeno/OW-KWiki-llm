# Role

Vattenfall is a Swedish state-owned multinational utility and one of
Europe's established offshore wind developers, with projects across the
UK, the Netherlands, Denmark, and Germany. In Germany, Vattenfall is
developing the 1.6 GW Nordlicht cluster (Nordlicht I and II) in the North
Sea, for which it took final investment decision in January 2026;
construction began offshore in July 2026 with the first monopile
installed by contractor [DEME Offshore](/companies/deme-offshore.md).

# Key Projects

- [Nordlicht Offshore Wind Farms](/projects/nordlicht.md) — 1.6 GW cluster
  (980 MW Nordlicht I + 630 MW Nordlicht II) in the German North Sea,
  FID January 2026, offshore construction started July 2026, expected
  online 2028.
- [Vidar Offshore Wind Farm](/projects/vidar.md) — 2 GW in the northern Skagerrak
  (Sweden), developed with Zephyr; permitted by the Swedish government on 24 July
  2026, but CEO Anna Borg said the company will not proceed at this time as market
  conditions do not make it commercially viable.
- [North Sea I Mid (Nordsøen Midt)](/projects/nordsoen-midt.md) — at least 1 GW
  some 20 km off Hvide Sande, Denmark; won in August 2026 at a DKK 504/MWh CfD
  strike price, operational by 2032.
- [Hesselø](/projects/hesselo.md) — at least 800 MW in the Danish Kattegat; won
  in August 2026 at a DKK 542/MWh CfD strike price, operational by 2032.
- Poseidon (Sweden) — approved by the Swedish government in November 2024, in the
  same decision that rejected 13 other applications on defence grounds.

# Denmark

On 4 August 2026 Vattenfall was named winning bidder for both awarded sites in
[Denmark's 2.8 GW CfD offshore wind tender](/tenders/denmark-2-8gw-cfd-tender-2026.md),
bidding lowest for North Sea I Mid (via Vattenfall Vindkraft A/S, on behalf of a
company yet to be established) and Hesselø (via Vattenfall Vindkraft Offshore
Wind DK P/S). These are the first awards under Denmark's redesigned two-sided
CfD framework, adopted after the previous unsupported 3 GW tender drew zero bids
in late 2024.

# Fleet Operations and Procurement

On 6 August 2026 Vattenfall launched a tender seeking contractors to provide
remotely operated vehicle (ROV) inspections and seabed surveys across its
European offshore wind fleet. Procuring these services fleet-wide rather than
per project points to the scale Vattenfall now operates at in the UK, the
Netherlands, Denmark, Germany and Sweden, and puts subsea inspection and seabed
monitoring — scour, cable exposure, foundation and
[scour-protection](/technology/fixed-bottom-foundations.md) condition — on a
single contractual footing. No scope size, contract duration, number of lots or
bid deadline was reported.

# Sweden

As a Swedish state-owned utility, Vattenfall is exposed to Sweden's
government-level offshore wind permitting regime, where Armed Forces objections
have driven mass rejections and no auction/support mechanism yet exists — see
[Swedish Offshore Wind Permitting and Defence Screening](/policy/swedish-offshore-wind-permitting.md).
