# EEW Group

EEW Group is a German steel pipe manufacturer and one of the world's
leading suppliers of large-diameter [monopile foundations](/technology/fixed-bottom-foundations.md)
for the offshore wind industry, producing foundation components at plants
in Europe and Asia.

## South Korea — EEW KHPC (Gwangyang)

EEW operates a foundation plant in South Korea, **EEW KHPC**, located in
Gwangyang, Jeollanam-do. In July 2026 the group announced it is expanding
this plant, growing its foundation manufacturing footprint in the
South Korean market as the country's [offshore wind pipeline](/tenders/south-korea-offshore-wind-auction-2026.md)
develops.

In July 2026, EEW KHPC also signed a supply chain MOU with developer
[Pacifico Energy Korea](/companies/pacifico-energy-korea.md) to supply
foundation components, including pin piles, for the 3.2 GW
[Jindo Offshore Wind Project Cluster](/projects/jindo.md), alongside
Gwangyang Bay Area partners KMC Line and CS Energy.
