# Glamox

Glamox is a Norwegian company supplying **marine lighting** — luminaires and floodlights —
for offshore structures, including the transition pieces of offshore wind turbine
foundations. Its offshore wind track record in this bundle is concentrated in the
**Taiwanese** market, generally contracted through the foundation fabricator rather than
the developer.

## Product

- **MIR G2** lighting system, with a variant the company says is designed for offshore wind
  applications in Taiwan.
- **FL60 LED floodlights** for platform lighting.
- Scope typically covers interior and exterior of transition pieces: boat landing areas,
  guardrails, ladders and access doors.

## Taiwanese contracts

- **2024** — lighting for [Hai Long](/projects/hai-long.md) 2 and 3, contracted by
  [SK oceanplant](/companies/sk-oceanplant.md), and for Greater Changhua 2b & 4
  ([Changhua](/projects/changhua.md)), contracted by HSG Sungdong Shipbuilding.
- **2025** — transition-piece lighting for the 495 MW
  [Fengmiao 1](/projects/fengmiao-1.md) wind farm, again via SK oceanplant.
- **August 2026** — [Formosa 4](/projects/formosa-4.md): more than **900 marine luminaires**
  for the transition pieces of **35 turbine foundations**, under an agreement with
  **ProCon Taiwan** and SK oceanplant, with deliveries scheduled for August 2026.

Contract values are not disclosed in the sources used here.
