# Role

Siemens Energy is a German energy technology group whose grid technologies
business supplies high-voltage equipment for offshore wind — transformers,
switchgear, converter technology and complete offshore substation topsides —
alongside its wind turbine arm [Siemens Gamesa](/companies/siemens-gamesa.md),
wholly owned since 2023. In July 2026 the group announced it will move to a
new corporate brand, **Omterra**, a change covering Siemens Gamesa as well;
this page is kept under `siemens-energy.md` until the new brand is in use.

# Offshore Substation Products

- **Offshore Transformer Module (OTM)** — prefabricated, factory-tested
  transformer platform concept intended to shorten offshore commissioning;
  deployed at the [Inch Cape](/projects/inch-cape.md) project in Scotland on a
  68 m jacket.
- **High-Voltage TranSwitch Unit (HV-TSU)** — lightweight integrated offshore
  substation that packs key high-voltage components into a more compact design
  to cut topside weight while keeping offshore reliability. Its first
  deployment at an offshore wind project was installed in August 2026 at the
  304 MW [Yuetuo Island](/projects/yuetuo-island.md) wind farm in China, where
  the 300 MW topside (45 x 38 x 23 m, ~1,700 t) and its jacket were built by
  [ZPMC](/companies/zpmc.md). Siemens Energy frames the case for it as lighter,
  more integrated infrastructure optimising project execution and reducing
  complexity in offshore grid connections.
