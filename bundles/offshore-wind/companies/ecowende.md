# Ecowende

Ecowende is a joint venture between Shell, Eneco, and Chubu Electric Power
(Chubu acquired a 30% stake from Eneco), formed to develop and operate the
[Hollandse Kust West VI](/projects/hollandse-kust-west-vi.md) offshore wind
farm, awarded to Shell and Eneco in a subsidy-free Dutch tender in December
2022. The 760 MW project, billed as one of the most nature-inclusive
offshore wind farms to date, started delivering power in July 2026.
