# WIND Group

**WIND** is a Netherlands-based company delivering subsea cable logistics, cable
recovery, and related offshore services. Through the wider WIND Group it offers
cable transportation, storage, cable equipment supply, carousel rental,
logistics and marshalling, and — via its **Draftec** business — equipment supply,
maintenance, and vessel mobilisation services. The group is led by CEO **Tom Nooij**.

## Baltic hub — Port of Oskarshamn, Sweden (July 2026)

In July 2026, WIND Group signed a long-term agreement with port operator
**Smålandshamnar** to establish a dedicated cable storage and offshore service
yard in the **Port of Oskarshamn**, Sweden, moving the firm closer to its clients
in the Baltic region. WIND cited the port's 435-metre quay and minimum water
depth of 10.3 metres as well suited to accommodate the relevant vessels and to
facilitate efficient ship-to-ship cable transfers. From this hub the company
plans to expand its Baltic presence and support offshore wind projects with
cable transportation, storage, and equipment supply.
