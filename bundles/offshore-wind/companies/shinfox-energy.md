# Overview

**Shinfox Energy** is a Taiwanese renewable energy group. In offshore wind
it is the developer behind the **700 MW Youde** project, selected in the
**Phase 3.2** round of [Taiwan's Offshore Wind Block Development
programme](/tenders/taiwan-block-development-phase-3.md) in August 2024.

# Youde and the contract termination

The Youde project company paid **50 per cent of the required performance
bond** when the administrative contract was signed, with the remaining half
due one year later. Taiwan's Energy Administration requested the outstanding
amount on **18 May** and **12 June 2026** and set a final deadline in
**August 2026**; when that passed unpaid, it began the **contract
termination process**, confirmed on **24 August 2026**. Shinfox has
submitted a response within the process.

# Foxwell Energy and financial position

**Foxwell Energy**, a Shinfox affiliate, is building the **300 MW Taipower
Offshore Wind Project Phase II (TPC Changhua Phase II)**, having won the
tender from Taipower in **June 2020**; the first jacket was installed in
March 2025 and the wind farm uses [Siemens Gamesa](/companies/siemens-gamesa.md)
turbines.

In **June 2026**, Shinfox Energy was **delisted from the Taiwan Stock
Exchange** after its net worth turned negative amid substantial financial
losses; according to Taiwanese media those losses include ones incurred by
Foxwell Energy.
