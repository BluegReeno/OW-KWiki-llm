# Overview

China Three Gorges (CTG) is a Chinese state-owned power group active in
offshore wind development and operation. In this bundle it appears as the
owner and operator of the 800 MW Jiangsu Rudong offshore wind farm.

# Key Projects

- [Rudong Offshore Wind Farm (Jiangsu)](/projects/rudong.md) — 800 MW, operating; surpassed 10 TWh of cumulative generation as reported on 19 August 2026.
