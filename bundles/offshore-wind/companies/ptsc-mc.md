# PTSC Mechanical & Construction (PTSC M&C)

PTSC Mechanical & Construction (PTSC M&C) is a Vietnamese offshore fabrication
contractor, part of the PetroVietnam Technical Services Corporation group,
which has moved from oil-and-gas structures into offshore wind fabrication for
the Asia-Pacific market.

## Offshore wind work

In July 2026, PTSC M&C completed the construction and loadout of the offshore
substation for the [Fengmiao 1](/projects/fengmiao-1.md) offshore wind farm in
Taiwan, developed by
[Copenhagen Infrastructure Partners](/companies/copenhagen-infrastructure-partners.md).
This places the yard in the same offshore-substation fabrication segment as
European contractors such as [DEME Offshore](/companies/deme-offshore.md) and
[Aker Solutions](/companies/aker-solutions.md), supplying Taiwanese projects
from Vietnam.
