# NextOcean

NextOcean supplies **WavePredictor**, a vessel-motion prediction system for
offshore operations. Its CEO is **Karel Roozen**.

## WavePredictor

WavePredictor combines **motion radar** with software to give crews
**real-time predictions of vessel movements**, so that conditions can be
assessed ahead of personnel transfers and decisions taken in challenging
sea states. The system shows the officer on watch what the vessel will do
**in the coming minutes**, which the company frames as backing the dozens
of daily go/no-go calls on an SOV with verified predictions rather than
feel, supporting safer technician transfers between vessel and turbine and
potentially widening the weather window in which transfers can be made.

## Esvagt Njord contract (August 2026)

NextOcean secured a contract to equip [ESVAGT](/companies/esvagt.md)'s
service operation vessel **Esvagt Njord** with WavePredictor. The system
supports O&M operations at the [Dudgeon](/projects/dudgeon.md) offshore
wind farm in the UK, where the vessel is deployed. The motion radar and
accompanying software were scheduled for installation on Esvagt Njord in
**August 2026**.

No contract value, duration or installed base was disclosed.
