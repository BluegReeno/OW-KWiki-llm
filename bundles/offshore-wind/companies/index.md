# Companies

* [Trianel](trianel.md) - German municipal-utility network company and offshore wind owner-operator (Trianel Windpark Borkum I); prequalified around 70 MW of the wind farm for the German balancing energy market in July 2026 through its virtual power plant unit.
* [JFE Engineering Corporation](jfe-engineering.md) - Tokyo-based engineering group whose Kasaoka Monopile Plant is, per the company, Japan's only monopile manufacturing base; rolled out what it says is Japan's first domestically manufactured offshore wind monopile in August 2026, for the 315 MW Oga–Katagami–Akita project.
* [TEPCO Renewable Power](tepco.md) - Renewable energy arm of Tokyo Electric Power Company and Japanese offshore wind developer; started the EIA in August 2026 for its own up to 420 MW wind farm off Choshi, the Round 1 area it lost to Mitsubishi in 2021.
* [Glamox](glamox.md) - Norwegian marine and industrial lighting manufacturer supplying luminaires for offshore wind turbine transition pieces, mostly in Taiwan; won the Formosa 4 contract in August 2026 for more than 900 luminaires across 35 foundations, after Hai Long 2 & 3, Greater Changhua 2b & 4 and Fengmiao 1.

* [Xuan Cau Holdings](xuan-cau-holdings.md) - Vietnamese renewable energy developer; joint venture partner with Trung Nam on the proposed 6 GW Can Gio 2 offshore wind project off Ho Chi Minh City, whose feasibility study was reviewed by the city authorities in August 2026.
* [Trung Nam](trung-nam.md) - Vietnamese renewable energy developer; joint venture partner with Xuan Cau Holdings on the proposed 6 GW Can Gio 2 offshore wind project off Ho Chi Minh City.

* [Ventyr](ventyr.md) - Norwegian offshore wind developer, a consortium of JERA Nex BP and Ingka Investments; won Norway's first offshore wind auction in March 2024 and is developing the up to 1.5 GW Sørlige Nordsjø II project, with its licence application now due 15 October 2027.

* [TotalEnergies](totalenergies.md) - French energy major with a European offshore wind development portfolio; took majority ownership of two Danish offshore wind projects in 2024, withdrew from Lillebælt Syd — and, per Danish media, Jammerland Bugt — in August 2026, and signed a long-term PPA for REGO-backed power from the 24.8% Walney stake held by Offshore Partners Walney, running to 2030.
* [SONFOR](sonfor.md) - Danish municipal utility for the Sønderborg area and developer of the 165 MW Lillebælt Syd offshore wind farm; taking over the majority stake in Lillebælt Vind A/S after TotalEnergies' exit and seeking a new partner by end-June 2027.
* [Haventus (Ardersier Port)](haventus.md) - Owner and developer of Ardersier Port on the Moray Firth, Scotland; deep-water quayside land with a high-voltage connection, marketed for offshore wind manufacturing, marshalling and assembly, and MoU partner of Dajin Heavy Industry since August 2026.
* [Port of Darłowo](port-of-darlowo.md) - Polish Baltic port being prepared as an offshore wind service base; completed PLN 174 million (EUR 40.3 million) of KPO-funded works in 2026, including a rebuilt 356.5 m breakwater and a new 229 m transhipment quay for service vessels.
* [Panstar Robotics](panstar-robotics.md) - South Korean subsea robotics company established by KIRO; signed an MoU with Taihan Cable & Solution in August 2026 to co-develop domestic high-performance ROV trenchers for submarine cable burial.

* [Sif Group](sif.md) - Dutch manufacturer of large-diameter steel monopile foundations for offshore wind, with a fabrication and marshalling terminal at Maasvlakte 2, Rotterdam; loaded out the final monopiles for the 1.4 GW Bałtyk 2 & 3 project, August 2026.

* [Osbit](osbit.md) - UK original equipment supplier; designer and builder of NKT's T-3600 subsea trencher and its launch and recovery system, in first testing at the Port of Blyth in August 2026.
* [Helix Robotics Solutions](helix-robotics-solutions.md) - UK robotics division of Helix Energy Solutions Group; four-year frame agreement to install, operate and maintain NKT's T-3600 subsea trencher from a Helix-provided support vessel.
* [China Three Gorges (CTG)](china-three-gorges.md) - Chinese state-owned power group and major offshore wind developer; owner and operator of the 800 MW Jiangsu Rudong offshore wind farm, which passed 10 TWh of cumulative generation in August 2026.
* [Arenko](arenko.md) - UK software company whose Nimbus optimisation, asset management and trading platform was selected by Ørsted in August 2026 to operate the 300 MW/600 MWh Iceni battery energy storage system co-located with Hornsea 3.
* [Tekmar Group](tekmar-group.md) - Provider of subsea protection technology for offshore wind, best known for cable protection systems; won a EUR 1 million concrete protection contract for an unnamed 'major' European offshore wind project, August 2026.
* [Stillstrom by Maersk](stillstrom.md) - Maersk venture developing offshore charging systems for vessels; completed a trial of an automated cable control system for offshore wind vessel charging, August 2026.
* [Semco Maritime](semco-maritime.md) - Danish energy services group active in offshore wind construction, service and maintenance; sold its jack-up vessels Thor and Wind Lift 1 to HEA Energy, August 2026.
* [HEA Energy](hea-energy.md) - Vessel owner in the offshore wind market; acquired the jack-up vessels Thor and Wind Lift 1 from Semco Maritime, August 2026.
* [Bilfinger](bilfinger.md) - International industrial services provider doing offshore wind construction supervision and technical advisory; selected by Ocean Winds in August 2026 to advise on the execution phase of the 390 MW BC-Wind project in Poland.
* [Maraen (Port of Nigg)](maraen.md) - Operator of Maraen Port of Nigg, a deepwater energy port and Green Freeport near Inverness; marshalling harbour for turbine pre-assembly on Dogger Bank B and C, creating up to 180 jobs in the Scottish Highlands (August 2026).
* [ABB](abb.md) - Swiss-Swedish electrification and automation group supplying marine power systems; contracted by Guangzhou Shipyard International in August 2026 to equip Allseas' new offshore converter station transport vessel.
* [Allseas](allseas.md) - Offshore heavy-lift, pipelay and installation contractor; has a new offshore converter station transport vessel on order at Guangzhou Shipyard International, with ABB supplying its power equipment (August 2026).
* [OEG](oeg.md) - Offshore energy services group providing marine, subsea and offshore operational services; secured the subsea corrosion protection contract for Skyborn Renewables' 976.5 MW Gennaker offshore wind farm, August 2026, and part of a tripartite BoP O&M pact for South Korea.
* [China National Offshore Oil Corporation (CNOOC)](cnooc.md) - Chinese state-owned offshore oil and gas producer that uses offshore wind to electrify its own production facilities; put the 16 MW Haiyou Anlan floating wind turbine powering its Lufeng oilfield into operation, August 2026.
* [EOLOS Floating LiDAR Solutions](eolos.md) - Spain-based floating LiDAR specialist providing offshore wind resource and metocean measurement campaigns; partnered with Vietnamese marine services company Enviros in August 2026 to support metocean campaigns in Vietnam.
* [Haizea Wind Group](haizea-wind-group.md) - Spanish manufacturer of offshore wind foundations and towers at the Port of Bilbao; delivered what it says are the largest monopiles ever built in Southern Europe (105+ m, 11 m diameter, 2,300+ t) for Ørsted's Hornsea 3, August 2026.
* [Steelwind Nordenham](steelwind-nordenham.md) - German monopile manufacturer on the Weser, part of the Dillinger/SHS steel group; completed delivery of all monopiles under its contract for the PGE/Ørsted Baltica 2 offshore wind farm, August 2026.
* [Energinet](energinet.md) - Danish state-owned transmission system operator for electricity and gas, responsible for offshore grid connection in Denmark and owner of the Bornholm Energy Island project; awarded Per Aarsleff a EUR 495 million onshore facilities contract in August 2026.
* [Per Aarsleff A/S](aarsleff.md) - Danish construction and civil engineering contractor active in ground engineering, marine and infrastructure works; won a EUR 495 million design-and-build contract from Energinet for the onshore facilities of Bornholm Energy Island, August 2026.
* [50Hertz](50hertz.md) - German transmission system operator responsible for grid connection of offshore wind in the German Baltic Sea; launched a preliminary market consultation in August 2026 to sound out the SOV market for servicing its Baltic offshore grid platforms.
* [Dajin Heavy Industry](dajin-heavy-industry.md) - Chinese heavy steel structure manufacturer for the wind industry and owner of the King-class offshore wind transport vessels; supplied foundations for Hornsea 3, Moray West and Inch Cape, fabricating Gennaker's 63 transition pieces, and MoU partner of Haventus at Ardersier Port since August 2026.
* [Cierco Energy](cierco-energy.md) - UK offshore wind developer focused on floating wind in the Celtic Sea; holds the two 100 MW Llŷr demonstration sites off Wales through Floventis Energy, its joint venture with SBM Offshore; Section 36 consent granted August 2026.

* [Equinor](equinor.md) - Norwegian, majority state-owned energy company (formerly Statoil) and a leading offshore wind developer, pioneering floating wind (Hywind) alongside large fixed-bottom projects such as Dogger Bank (UK) and Empire Wind (US).
* [RWE](rwe-offshore-wind.md) - German multinational utility and the world's second-largest offshore wind operator, developing, building, and operating wind farms across the UK, Germany, Denmark, the Netherlands, and other markets; exited US offshore wind in August 2026 under a USD 1.22 billion lease settlement.
* [Iberdrola / Avangrid](iberdrola.md) - Spanish multinational utility and one of the world's largest offshore wind developers, active across Europe, the US (via wholly-owned subsidiary Avangrid) and Asia-Pacific.
* [Vestas Wind Systems A/S](vestas.md) - Danish wind turbine manufacturer, the world's largest by installed capacity, supplying both onshore and offshore turbines including the V236-15.0 MW offshore platform.
* [Siemens Gamesa Renewable Energy](siemens-gamesa.md) - Wind turbine manufacturer headquartered in Spain, wholly owned by Siemens Energy since 2023 and set to be rebranded Omterra, and one of the leading suppliers of offshore direct-drive turbines worldwide.

* [Dominion Energy](dominion-energy.md) - US regulated utility headquartered in Richmond, Virginia, and the sole owner and developer of the 2.6 GW Coastal Virginia Offshore Wind project, the largest offshore wind farm under construction in the United States.
* [DEME Offshore](deme-offshore.md) - Belgian marine engineering and offshore installation contractor, part of DEME Group, providing EPCI services (foundations, cables, substations) for offshore wind farms worldwide.
* [F.lli Righini](f-lli-righini.md) - Italian offshore equipment engineering and manufacturing company; designed, tested and delivered the Secondary Structure Installation Tools (boat landings, internal and external platforms) for Van Oord's Baltica 2 monopile campaign, announced August 2026.
