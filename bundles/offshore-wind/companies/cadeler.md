# Cadeler

Cadeler is a Danish offshore wind installation contractor providing
transportation, installation, and marine services for offshore wind
turbines and foundations. The company operates a fleet of jack-up
installation vessels and has several newbuilds under construction.

## Fleet expansion

Cadeler took delivery of **Wind Ace**, its eleventh wind installation
vessel, in July 2026. The vessel's first assignment is at the **East
Anglia Two** offshore wind farm in the UK.

Cadeler and **COSCO Shipping Offshore** held a keel-laying ceremony for
**Wind Apex**, a Cadeler installation vessel being built at COSCO's yard,
in July 2026 — marking the start of the vessel taking physical shape.

In August 2026 Cadeler signed **firm contracts with COSCO Shipping
Offshore's shipyard in Qidong, China, for two new wind foundation
installation vessels**, deepening its relationship with the same yard
building Wind Apex. The excerpt available does not give the vessels'
specifications, contract value, or delivery dates.

## Acquisitions

In August 2026 Cadeler **acquired [Menck](/companies/menck.md)**, a
provider of equipment and technology for offshore foundation
installation, for **EUR 501 million** — extending the company from
[foundation](/technology/fixed-bottom-foundations.md) installation
vessels into the installation equipment and technology that goes with
them. The excerpt available does not name the seller, the transaction
structure, financing or closing conditions.

## Projects

- **[Hornsea 3](/projects/hornsea-3.md) (UK, 2.9 GW)** — foundation
  installation with the vessel **Wind Ally**, which started work at the
  offshore site in May 2026. Cadeler also performs the load-out and load-in
  operations for the project's XXL monopiles at
  [Haizea Wind Group](/companies/haizea-wind-group.md)'s Port of Bilbao
  facility (August 2026).
- **[Inch Cape](/projects/inch-cape.md) (UK, 1.1 GW)** — the installation
  vessel **Wind Mover** is due to begin turbine installation in
  **November 2026**, following the jacket pin pile campaign by Seaway7.
- **East Anglia Two (UK)** — first assignment of **Wind Ace**, delivered
  July 2026.
