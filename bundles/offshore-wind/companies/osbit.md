# Osbit

Osbit is a UK original equipment supplier of offshore and subsea equipment
serving the offshore wind and energy sectors.

# NKT T-3600 Subsea Trencher

Osbit is in charge of the design and construction of the **NKT T-3600**
subsea trencher for [NKT](/companies/nkt.md), as well as its launch and
recovery system (LARS). The trencher arrived at the UK's Port of Blyth in
February 2026 for fit-out and commissioning, and in mid-August 2026 NKT
reported that it was approaching final assembly and entering its first
round of testing. NKT described the programme as an "intensive journey"
carried out together with Osbit and Helix Energy Solutions Group, whose
[Helix Robotics Solutions](/companies/helix-robotics-solutions.md)
division will operate the tool.

The reporting does not state Osbit's ownership, size, build location for
the trencher, contract value or delivery date.
