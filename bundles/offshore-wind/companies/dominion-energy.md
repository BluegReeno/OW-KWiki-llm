# Role

Dominion Energy is a US energy company headquartered in Richmond, Virginia,
supplying electricity and gas across several states. Unusually for the sector,
it is building offshore wind as a **regulated utility asset** rather than
through a merchant or joint-venture development model: it owns
[Coastal Virginia Offshore Wind (CVOW)](/projects/coastal-virginia-offshore-wind.md)
outright, and recovers the investment through rates approved by the Virginia
State Corporation Commission.

CVOW, at 2.6 GW off Virginia Beach, is the largest offshore wind farm under
construction in the United States. Turbines come from
[Siemens Gamesa](/companies/siemens-gamesa.md)'s SG 14-222 DD platform.

# Project Status Reporting

Dominion reports CVOW progress alongside its quarterly results. In August 2026
it put the project at 81% complete and revised the schedule so that the final
turbine is expected by the end of 2027.

Dominion was also among the parties responding to the December 2025 federal
stop-work orders that affected CVOW and four other US projects under
construction — see [US BOEM leasing process](/policy/us-boem-leasing-process.md).
