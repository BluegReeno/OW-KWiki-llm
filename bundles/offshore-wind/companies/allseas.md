# Overview

Allseas is an offshore contractor best known for heavy-lift and pipelay
operations with very large purpose-built vessels, and increasingly a player in
offshore wind through the transport and installation of oversized structures.

# Fleet

- **New offshore converter station transport vessel** (announced 11 August
  2026): under construction at Guangzhou Shipyard International (GSI) in
  China, with [ABB](/companies/abb.md) contracted to supply the vessel's power
  equipment. Dedicated tonnage for moving
  [HVDC converter platforms](/technology/hvdc-grid-connection.md) — among the
  heaviest and most expensive single structures in an offshore wind project —
  adds a transport link to a segment the bundle has so far tracked mainly
  through fabricators such as [Seatrium](/companies/seatrium.md),
  [Aker Solutions](/companies/aker-solutions.md) and
  [SK Oceanplant](/companies/sk-oceanplant.md).
