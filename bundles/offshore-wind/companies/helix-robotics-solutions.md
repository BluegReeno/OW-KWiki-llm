# Helix Robotics Solutions

Helix Robotics Solutions is the UK robotics division of Helix Energy
Solutions Group, providing subsea robotics and offshore operations
services.

# NKT T-3600 Frame Agreement

In July 2025, [NKT](/companies/nkt.md) entered into a four-year frame
agreement with Helix Robotics Solutions covering the installation,
offshore operations, project engineering and maintenance of the
**NKT T-3600** subsea trencher, which is to be deployed from a
Helix-provided support vessel. The trencher itself is designed and built
by [Osbit](/companies/osbit.md) and entered its first round of testing in
August 2026.

The reporting does not state the agreement's value, the support vessel to
be used, or the projects the spread will work on.
