# 50Hertz

50Hertz is one of Germany's four transmission system operators (TSOs). Its
control area covers the country's north-east and its Baltic Sea coast, which
makes it the counterpart to [TenneT](/technology/hvdc-grid-connection.md) —
the TSO for the German North Sea — for
[grid connection](/technology/hvdc-grid-connection.md) of German Baltic Sea
offshore wind. Its offshore assets are the
[offshore substation](/technology/offshore-substations.md) and converter
platforms that collect power from Baltic wind farms and export it to shore.

## SOV market consultation (August 2026)

On 5 August 2026 offshoreWIND.biz reported that 50Hertz had launched a
**preliminary market consultation** to assess the market for a service
operation vessel (SOV) to serve its offshore grid platforms in the Baltic Sea.
A preliminary market consultation is a pre-procurement sounding, not yet a call
for bids: it lets the contracting entity gauge available tonnage, technical
capability and pricing before framing a tender, so this page is tagged
`tenders` as the upstream stage of a possible future SOV charter.

The item available here is a truncated excerpt, so no further detail is
asserted: no number of vessels, charter length or start date, no vessel
specification (accommodation, walk-to-work gangway, fuel or propulsion
requirements), no named platforms or wind farms to be served, no response
deadline for the consultation, and no timetable for an actual tender. Whether
any of the CSOV/SOV owners already in this bundle —
[Windward Offshore](/companies/windward-offshore.md),
[Bernhard Schulte Offshore](/companies/bernhard-schulte-offshore.md),
[North Star](/companies/north-star.md) — respond is not reported.

Notable in itself is that the vessel demand comes from a **grid operator**
rather than a wind farm developer: as offshore transmission assets multiply in
the Baltic, the platforms themselves need dedicated offshore service logistics.
