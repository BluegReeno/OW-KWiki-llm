# Overview

Haventus is the owner and developer of **Ardersier Port** in Scotland, on the
Moray Firth. The port is offered to the offshore wind sector on three assets:
extensive quayside land, deep-water access, and a high-voltage power
connection, which Haventus says can support manufacturing, fabrication,
assembly and storage of large-scale structures for offshore wind and other
energy projects.

Its stated ambition is to make Ardersier "the leading facility for offshore
wind in the UK and Northern Europe" (Mark Gillespie, Chief Commercial
Officer). It sits alongside [Maraen (Port of Nigg)](/companies/maraen.md) as a
second deep-water energy port on the Moray Firth / Inverness stretch of the
Scottish coast.

# Partnerships

- **[Dajin Heavy Industry](/companies/dajin-heavy-industry.md) (August 2026)**:
  Memorandum of Understanding to explore combining Dajin's manufacturing and
  transport capability with Ardersier's port infrastructure, in support of both
  [fixed-bottom](/technology/fixed-bottom-foundations.md) and
  [floating wind](/technology/floating-wind.md) projects in the UK and across
  Europe. The two parties will also identify new commercial opportunities and
  assess how Ardersier could provide Dajin with a site for storage,
  marshalling, assembly and transportation of offshore wind components.
  The MoU is exploratory: no investment figure, tenancy, lease term, volume
  commitment or named wind farm was reported.

# History

Ardersier Port was the intended site of a GBP 1.5 billion offshore wind
turbine factory planned by the Chinese turbine manufacturer **Mingyang Smart
Energy**. The UK government rejected those plans, stating that Mingyang's
turbines would not be suitable for UK projects and citing national security
concerns. Mingyang was subsequently reported to be eyeing a factory in Spain
instead.
