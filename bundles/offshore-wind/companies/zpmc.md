# Overview

Shanghai Zhenhua Heavy Industries (ZPMC) is a Chinese heavy-equipment
manufacturer and shipbuilder, best known worldwide for port cranes, whose
yards also build specialised offshore wind tonnage — cable-laying vessels and
service operation vessels — for both Chinese and European owners.

# Offshore wind newbuilds

- **23 July 2026**: started construction of a large-capacity multipurpose
  cable-laying vessel for Chinese cable manufacturer Far East Cable
  ([digest](/digests/2026-07-23-zpmc-clv-far-east-cable.md)).
- **10 August 2026**: laid the keel for the second service operation vessel
  (SOV) in a series for French shipowner
  [Louis Dreyfus Armateurs](/companies/louis-dreyfus-armateurs.md); that
  vessel is chartered for [Vattenfall](/companies/vattenfall.md)'s
  [Nordlicht](/projects/nordlicht.md) offshore wind farms in the German
  North Sea.

# Offshore substation fabrication

- **18 August 2026**: built the topside module, jacket foundation and
  foundation pin piles for the offshore substation of the 304 MW
  [Yuetuo Island](/projects/yuetuo-island.md) wind farm in North China — the
  first offshore wind deployment of
  [Siemens Energy](/companies/siemens-energy.md)'s lightweight HV-TSU design.
  The 300 MW topside measures 45 x 38 x 23 m and weighs about 1,700 tonnes
  ([digest](/digests/2026-08-18-siemens-energy-hv-tsu-yuetuo-island.md)).
