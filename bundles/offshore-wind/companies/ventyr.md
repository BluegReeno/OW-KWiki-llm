# Ventyr

Ventyr is the developer consortium behind
[Sørlige Nordsjø II](/projects/sorlige-nordsjo-ii.md), Norway's first
commercial offshore wind farm. It is made up of
[JERA Nex BP](/companies/jera-nex-bp.md) and Ingka Investments.

- **March 2024**: Won Norway's first offshore wind auction for the up to
  1.5 GW Sørlige Nordsjø II zone with a bid of NOK 1.15/kWh (EUR 0.099/kWh).
- **June 2026**: Asked the Norwegian Ministry of Energy to extend its licence
  application deadline, citing unexpected development and planning challenges
  and the difficult macroeconomic environment in offshore wind, and the need to
  study an alternative landfall and grid connection route ("G 2.0") near the
  Lista substation at Alcoa's aluminium plant.
- **August 2026**: Granted a twelve-month extension, with the licence
  application now due by 15 October 2027.
