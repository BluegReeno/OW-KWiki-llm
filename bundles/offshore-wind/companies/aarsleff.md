# Per Aarsleff A/S

Per Aarsleff A/S is a Danish construction and civil engineering group. Its work
spans ground engineering, marine and infrastructure construction — the civil
side of energy projects rather than turbines, foundations or electrical
equipment. In offshore wind it appears on the **onshore** end of the chain:
converter station sites, substations, cable landfalls and the buildings and
groundworks around them.

## Bornholm Energy Island onshore facilities (August 2026)

On 5 August 2026 offshoreWIND.biz reported that Aarsleff had been awarded a
**design-and-build contract worth EUR 495 million** by
[Energinet](/companies/energinet.md) for the onshore facilities of
[Bornholm Energy Island](/projects/bornholm-energy-island.md), the Danish
Baltic Sea energy-island project.

The page is tagged `tenders`: this is a contract award by a transmission system
operator acting as contracting authority. The item is a truncated excerpt, so
the scope of the "onshore facilities", the construction schedule, the
procurement route and competing bidders, and any consortium partners or
subcontractors are not reported and are not asserted here.
