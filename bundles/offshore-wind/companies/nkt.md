# NKT

NKT is a Danish high-voltage power cable manufacturer that both produces
and installs subsea cable systems for offshore wind export links and grid
interconnectors, including [HVDC grid connections](/technology/hvdc-grid-connection.md).
Alongside its cable factories, NKT operates its own cable-installation
fleet, putting it in the same integrated manufacture-and-install position
as peers such as [LS Cable & System](/companies/ls-cable-system.md) and
[Taihan Cable & Solution](/companies/taihan-cable-solution.md).

# NKT Eleonora (cable-laying vessel under construction)

*NKT Eleonora* is NKT's new cable-laying vessel (CLV), being built by
shipbuilder [Vard](/companies/vard.md). In July 2026 the vessel departed Romania for Norway,
with construction continuing there. Per the available excerpt, the vessel
will be able to run on dual fuel; the excerpt is truncated and does not
state the fuels, cable-carousel capacity, delivery date or the projects
the CLV will serve.

# NKT T-3600 subsea trencher

NKT announced its investment in the **T-3600** subsea trencher in June
2025 as part of a wider build-out of its
[cable installation](/technology/subsea-cable-installation.md)
capabilities. Billed as the world's most powerful subsea trencher, the
T-3600 will deliver 3,600 horsepower and bury cables up to 5.5 metres
below the seabed, with both jetting and cutting functions so it can work
across a wide range of soil conditions.

The tool and its launch and recovery system (LARS) are designed and built
by UK equipment supplier [Osbit](/companies/osbit.md). In July 2025 NKT
signed a four-year frame agreement with
[Helix Robotics Solutions](/companies/helix-robotics-solutions.md), the UK
robotics division of Helix Energy Solutions Group, covering installation,
offshore operations, project engineering and maintenance of the trencher,
which will be deployed from a Helix-provided support vessel. The trencher
arrived at the UK's Port of Blyth in February 2026 for fit-out and
commissioning, and in mid-August 2026 NKT reported it was approaching
final assembly and entering its first round of testing. No delivery date
or first project has been stated.
