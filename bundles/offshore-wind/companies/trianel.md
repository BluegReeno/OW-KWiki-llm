# Trianel

German company owned by municipal utilities (Stadtwerke), based in Aachen, active in
power trading, virtual power plant operation and generation assets. In offshore wind it
is the sponsor and operator of
[Trianel Windpark Borkum I](/projects/trianel-windpark-borkum-i.md) (TWB I), described as
Germany's first municipal offshore wind farm.

# Virtual power plant and balancing market

Trianel operates a virtual power plant business, headed by **Lars Becker** (Head of
Virtual Power Plant, Trianel GmbH). In July 2026 it brought
[Trianel Windpark Borkum I](/projects/trianel-windpark-borkum-i.md) into Germany's
balancing energy market:

- Around **70 MW** of the wind farm's capacity **prequalified** for the balancing market.
- Supply of **negative secondary control reserve** (negative aFRR) began in July 2026,
  with marketing of the balancing capacity starting **22 July 2026**.
- The transmission system operator can automatically curtail the wind farm's output on
  request; per Trianel, the adjustment must be carried out **within five minutes**.
- A dedicated **remote-control infrastructure** built for the wind farm relays TSO
  requests in real time and implements the output change automatically, with a
  **redundant architecture** meeting balancing-market technical requirements.

Trianel presents the prequalification as an additional revenue stream alongside the wind
farm's existing power sales arrangements, including power purchase agreements.
