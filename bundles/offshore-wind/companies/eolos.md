# EOLOS Floating LiDAR Solutions

EOLOS is a Spain-based specialist in floating LiDAR systems — buoy-mounted
laser wind measurement units used to characterise wind resource and metocean
conditions at offshore wind sites before construction, as an alternative to
fixed met masts.

## Vietnam partnership

In August 2026, EOLOS announced a partnership with Enviros, a Vietnamese
marine services company, to support offshore wind metocean measurement
campaigns in Vietnam. The tie-up pairs the Spanish measurement technology
provider with a local marine services operator, the standard route into an
emerging Asia-Pacific offshore wind market — the same country whose
fabrication capability the bundle tracks through
[PTSC M&C](/companies/ptsc-mc.md).

Site measurement campaigns sit at the front end of project development, ahead
of the survey and consenting work tracked elsewhere in the bundle, and are
relevant to both [fixed-bottom](/technology/fixed-bottom-foundations.md) and
[floating wind](/technology/floating-wind.md) sites, where buoy-based systems
avoid the cost of a met mast in deep water.

Terms of the partnership, its duration, target projects and clients were not
reported.
