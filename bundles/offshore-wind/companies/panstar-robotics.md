# Panstar Robotics

Panstar Robotics is a South Korean robotics company established by the
**Korea Institute of Robotics & Technology Convergence (KIRO)**, working on
remotely operated vehicles (ROVs) for
[subsea cable installation and burial](/technology/subsea-cable-installation.md).

# MoU with Taihan Cable & Solution

On **19 August 2026** Panstar Robotics signed a memorandum of understanding
with [Taihan Cable & Solution](/companies/taihan-cable-solution.md) to jointly
develop high-performance ROV trenchers, their operating technologies and
installation solutions, with the explicit aim of replacing equipment and
overseas specialists that Korea's submarine cable industry currently depends
on. Under the MoU the partners will:

- develop domestic ROV trenchers and operating systems for offshore wind
  cable systems and long-distance grid interconnection and
  [HVDC](/technology/hvdc-grid-connection.md) submarine cable projects,
  including the **West Coast Energy Highway**
- run demonstration testing of submarine cable burial and protection
  technologies and pursue commercialisation
- participate in national R&D programmes and jointly explore overseas markets
