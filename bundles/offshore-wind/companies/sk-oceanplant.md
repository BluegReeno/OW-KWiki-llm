# SK oceanplant

SK oceanplant is a South Korean manufacturer of offshore wind foundations and
offshore structures. It sits in the same fabrication segment as
[EEW Group](/companies/eew-group.md), [CS Wind Offshore](/companies/cs-wind-offshore.md)
and [Seatrium](/companies/seatrium.md), and is part of the wider Korean industrial
push into European offshore wind supply chains alongside
[Hanwha Ocean](/companies/hanwha-ocean.md), [LS Cable & System](/companies/ls-cable-system.md)
and [Taihan Cable & Solution](/companies/taihan-cable-solution.md).

## European HVDC work

The company entered the European offshore wind market through the **DolWin4** and
**BorWin4** German grid connection projects, signing contracts in April 2025 and
delivering key components in May 2026.

## BalWin5 jacket contract (July 2026)

On 31 July 2026 SK oceanplant announced a contract from
[Seatrium](/companies/seatrium.md) to manufacture and supply the jacket foundation
and piles for TenneT's [BalWin5](/projects/balwin5.md) HVDC offshore converter
platform in the German North Sea:

- **Value**: approximately USD 110.7 million (around EUR 96 million).
- **Scope**: construction engineering, procurement, fabrication and load-out of
  one jacket and 20 piles for the offshore substation.
- **Structure**: the jacket substructure will weigh approximately 15,000 tonnes.

According to the company, the award is the first time a Korean supplier has secured
a *full* jacket supply agreement for a European
[HVDC](/technology/hvdc-grid-connection.md) offshore substation — Korean firms had
previously supplied individual structural components rather than a complete jacket
for a large-scale HVDC offshore substation connected to the European grid.

The article does not state the fabrication yard, the delivery or load-out date, or
the installation contractor, so none is recorded here.

## Taiwanese transition pieces

SK oceanplant is a recurring transition-piece fabricator for Taiwanese offshore wind
farms, and the contracting party for the foundation lighting supplied by
[Glamox](/companies/glamox.md) on [Hai Long](/projects/hai-long.md) 2 and 3 (2024),
[Fengmiao 1](/projects/fengmiao-1.md) (2025) and — jointly with ProCon Taiwan, reported
25 August 2026 — the 35 foundations of [Formosa 4](/projects/formosa-4.md), where the
transition pieces are fabricated by Century Wind Power in Taiwan and SK oceanplant in
South Korea.
