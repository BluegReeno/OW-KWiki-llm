# Role

RWE is a German multinational energy company and one of the world's leading players in offshore wind, describing itself as the number 2 global operator in the sector with over 20 years of experience. It is active across the full value chain — development, construction, and operation — and operates 18-19 offshore wind farms worldwide. RWE has set a target of expanding its global offshore wind capacity to 10 GW by 2030, with more than 4 GW currently under construction in Europe. Core markets are the UK, Germany, Denmark, and the Netherlands, alongside early-stage development in Taiwan; RWE left the US offshore wind market in August 2026, relinquishing its entire ~6 GW US lease portfolio in a settlement with the US government.

In January 2026, RWE was the single biggest winner of the UK's [UK CfD Allocation Round 7](/tenders/uk-cfd-allocation-round-7.md), securing Contracts for Difference for 6.9 GW of offshore wind capacity — Norfolk Vanguard East, Norfolk Vanguard West, Dogger Bank South East, Dogger Bank South West, and Awel y Môr — at a strike price of £91.20/MWh, alongside a long-term partnership with investor KKR on the Norfolk Vanguard projects.

# Key Projects

- **Sofia** (1.4 GW, UK) — RWE's largest single project, located on Dogger Bank in the North Sea, using Siemens Gamesa 14 MW turbines; currently in installation phase with commissioning expected 2026. Note: Sofia is an independently RWE-owned project, distinct from the separate "Dogger Bank Wind Farm" (A/B/C) developed by SSE Renewables and Equinor on a neighbouring site.
- **[Thor](/projects/thor.md)** (1.1 GW, Denmark) — Denmark's largest offshore wind farm, a joint project of RWE (51%) and Norges Bank Investment Management (49%), using 72 [Siemens Gamesa](/companies/siemens-gamesa.md) turbines with recyclable rotor blades; turbine installation passed the halfway mark (36 of 72) in July 2026, full commissioning expected spring 2027.
- **[Nordseecluster](/projects/nordseecluster.md)** (1.6 GW, Germany) — developed in two phases (Nordseecluster A: 660 MW, Nordseecluster B: 900 MW), with [Vestas](/companies/vestas.md) selected as turbine supplier; first turbine installed 2026 and Nordseecluster A fed its first electricity into the German grid in July 2026.
- **OranjeWind** (795 MW, Netherlands) — developed with TotalEnergies, which acquired a 50% stake and dedicates its share to green hydrogen production for its European refineries.
- **Windbostel Ost / Windbostel West** (4 GW combined, Germany) — two North Sea sites awarded to RWE in Germany's 2024 offshore auction, being jointly developed with TotalEnergies (50% equity stake); investment decisions expected 2027-2028.
- **Norfolk Vanguard East / West and Dogger Bank South East / West** (UK) — awarded CfDs in AR7 (January 2026); Norfolk Vanguard developed with KKR as strategic partner, FID targeted for summer 2026.
- **Awel y Môr** (UK, Irish Sea) — also awarded a CfD in AR7.
- **Community Offshore Wind** (2.8 GW, New York/New Jersey, US) — lease area OCS-A 0539, developed with National Grid Ventures; paused from April 2025 and cancelled in August 2026 when RWE relinquished the lease under a settlement with the US Department of the Interior.
- **Canopy** (1.6 GW potential, California, US) — floating wind lease off Humboldt County; relinquished under the same August 2026 settlement, as was a 1.24 GW lease off Louisiana.
- **Chu Feng** (up to 448 MW, Taiwan) — early-stage project with Asia Cement Corporation off Hsinchu, part of RWE's push into Asia-Pacific floating and fixed-bottom development.

# Technology Focus

RWE is active in [floating offshore wind](/technology/floating-wind.md) development (Taiwan, US West Coast) alongside its established fixed-bottom portfolio, and pursues offshore [power-to-x](/technology/power-to-x.md) applications, including the RWE-BASF "Offshore-to-X" green hydrogen project and green-hydrogen testbeds at Nordsee One and Kaskasi.

# Recent Developments

- **UK**: Won 6.9 GW in CfD Allocation Round 7 (January 2026), the largest single award in "Europe's biggest ever offshore wind auction."
- **Germany**: Formalised a 4 GW joint development partnership with TotalEnergies for the Windbostel sites (finalised October 2024).
- **Earnings (July 2026)**: RWE raised the full-year 2026 earnings outlook for its Offshore Wind division, and lifted its 2027 outlook, after first-half 2026 results the company said exceeded market expectations.
- **United States (August 2026)**: RWE exited the US offshore wind market. Under a settlement agreement with the Department of the Interior announced on 6 August 2026, RWE relinquishes its leases in the New York Bight and off California and Louisiana — a portfolio of nearly 6 GW (Community Offshore Wind 2.8 GW, Canopy 1.6 GW, Louisiana 1.24 GW) — in exchange for USD 1.22 billion (about EUR 1.06 billion), resolving RWE U.S. Offshore's claims against the government. RWE said it had invested more than USD 1 billion in the leases and their development but saw "no path forward to permit these projects in the U.S. for the foreseeable future". It is the largest US offshore wind lease termination settlement announced to date, following those with TotalEnergies, Ocean Winds-led Bluepoint Wind and Golden State Wind, Invenergy and Duke Energy — see [US BOEM Offshore Wind Leasing Process](/policy/us-boem-leasing-process.md). RWE had frozen US offshore activity in April 2025 citing political uncertainty, and reported exit talks in May 2026 drew objections from more than 50 US organisations.
- **US capital redeployment**: RWE said it will redirect capital to projects it can advance with greater certainty, including USD 900 million for an indirect 16% stake in the Louisiana LNG project and a USD 300 million turbine reservation agreement for gas peaking projects, within roughly EUR 17 billion of planned US investment over six years to grow US generation capacity from about 13 GW to 22 GW by 2031. The company said it remains focused on growing offshore wind globally, citing 18 wind farms in operation, four under construction, and 6.9 GW secured in the UK's latest auction.
- **Offshore Wind earnings (H1 2026)**: adjusted EBITDA of EUR 810 million, up from EUR 643 million a year earlier, on higher production in more favourable wind conditions; full-year 2026 division guidance raised by EUR 100 million to EUR 1.65-2.15 billion.
