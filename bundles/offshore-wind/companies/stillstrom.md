# Stillstrom by Maersk

Stillstrom by Maersk is a Maersk venture developing offshore charging
systems that let vessels take power at sea rather than burning fuel while
on station or in transit.

## Automated cable control trial (August 2026)

In August 2026 Stillstrom **completed a trial of an automated cable
control system for charging vessels at offshore wind farms**. The excerpt
available gives no further detail: it does not state where or when the
