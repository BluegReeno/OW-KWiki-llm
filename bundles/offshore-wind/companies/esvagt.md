# ESVAGT

ESVAGT owns and operates **service operation vessels (SOVs)** used for the
operation and maintenance of offshore wind farms, accommodating technicians
offshore and transferring them to turbines.

## Esvagt Njord (Dudgeon)

**Esvagt Njord** was first deployed on the [Dudgeon](/projects/dudgeon.md)
offshore wind farm in **autumn 2016**, shortly after ESVAGT took delivery of
the vessel. The initial contract included options to extend by up to five
additional years, and was **extended in 2021**.

In **August 2026** ESVAGT contracted [NextOcean](/companies/nextocean.md) to
fit the vessel with the **WavePredictor** motion-radar prediction system, to
support go/no-go decisions on personnel transfers at Dudgeon.

ESVAGT also appears alongside [Stillstrom by Maersk](/companies/stillstrom.md)
and Semco Maritime in the August 2026 offshore vessel-charging trial, though
no role for it was stated there.
