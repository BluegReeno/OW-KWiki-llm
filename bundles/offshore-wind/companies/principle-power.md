# Overview

**Principle Power** is a floating offshore wind technology company, best known for its WindFloat semi-submersible floating foundation platform. In July 2026 it was awarded the **Operations and Maintenance (O&M) support contract** for the floating foundations of the [Éoliennes Flottantes du Golfe du Lion (EFGL)](/projects/efgl-golfe-du-lion.md) floating wind farm in the French Mediterranean, developed by [Ocean Winds](/companies/ocean-winds.md).
