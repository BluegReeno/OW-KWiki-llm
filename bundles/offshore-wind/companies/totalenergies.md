# TotalEnergies

TotalEnergies is a French energy major that has built an offshore wind
development portfolio in Europe, typically by taking stakes in projects led by
local developers rather than originating them itself.

## Denmark: entry 2024, exit 2026

In **December 2024** TotalEnergies took **majority ownership of two Danish
offshore wind projects**, joining
[SONFOR](/companies/sonfor.md) and European Energy at
[Lillebælt Syd](/projects/lillebaelt-syd.md) (165 MW, Little Belt).

On **21 August 2026** offshoreWIND.biz reported that TotalEnergies had decided
to **withdraw from Lillebælt Syd**, where it was the majority partner, handing
the majority stake in project company Lillebælt Vind A/S back to SONFOR.
Citing Danish media, the same report says TotalEnergies is also **exiting the
Jammerland Bugt** offshore wind project, in which European Energy is likewise
the minority shareholder.

No reason, valuation or transaction terms were given for the withdrawal, and
no statement from TotalEnergies is quoted; SONFOR attributed the wider context
to significantly changed offshore wind market conditions.

## UK: offtaker at Walney

On **27 August 2026** offshoreWIND.biz reported that **TotalEnergies Gas &
Power Limited** had entered into a **long-term power purchase agreement** for
electricity from the [Walney 1 & 2](/projects/walney.md) offshore wind farms
(367 MW, Irish Sea), majority-owned and operated by
[Ørsted](/companies/orsted.md). The PPA was signed with **Offshore Partners
Walney (OPW)**, the 24.8% co-owner, and covers **REGO-backed electricity**
bought annually from OPW's share of generation **through to 2030**. No volume,
price or start date was disclosed.

The deal is an offtake position, not an equity one — TotalEnergies buys the
power, it does not own the asset.
