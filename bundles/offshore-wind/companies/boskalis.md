# Boskalis

Boskalis is a Dutch marine engineering, dredging, and offshore services
group active in offshore wind construction, including subsea rock
installation and cable installation works.

## Windpiper - Subsea Rock Installation Vessel

Boskalis christened its new subsea rock installation (SRI) vessel
**Windpiper** on 3 July 2026, following a year and a half of conversion
work on a purchased vessel (acquisition announced January 2025). Boskalis
describes it as the largest SRI vessel in the industry:

* Rock-carrying capacity of 45,500 tonnes.
* 227 metres long, 40 metres wide.
* Total installed power exceeding 31,000 kW.
* Over a hundred single-occupancy cabins.

On 30 July 2026, Windpiper departed the Port of Rotterdam to start work on
[Baltic Power](/projects/baltic-power.md), Poland's first offshore wind
farm — the vessel's maiden rock installation project.
