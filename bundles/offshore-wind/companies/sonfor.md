# SONFOR

SONFOR is a Danish utility serving the Sønderborg area in southern Jutland. In
offshore wind it is the developer behind
[Lillebælt Syd](/projects/lillebaelt-syd.md), a 165 MW project in the Little
Belt that it took over from Sønderborg Municipality in **2020** with the task
of developing it and finding partners. The project originated as a local wind
farm initiative in 2011.

## Lillebælt Syd ownership (August 2026)

SONFOR brought in European Energy as a partner in 2022 and
[TotalEnergies](/companies/totalenergies.md) in 2024, the latter taking the
majority. After TotalEnergies decided to withdraw, SONFOR is **taking over the
majority stake in Lillebælt Vind A/S**, the project company, and will spend the
coming months assessing potential new partners as well as the project's
business case — a process expected to conclude by the **end of June 2027**.
European Energy stays on as a minority shareholder.

SONFOR framed the project as still relevant to electricity supply security and
local development around Sønderborg, while noting that offshore wind market
conditions have changed significantly.
