# Overview

Arenko is a UK company supplying **Nimbus**, an optimisation, asset management
and trading platform for grid-scale batteries. Its chief executive is
**Rupert Newland**.

# Iceni BESS (Hornsea 3)

In August 2026 [Ørsted](/companies/orsted.md) selected Arenko to deploy Nimbus
at **Iceni**, the 300 MW/600 MWh battery energy storage system that shares an
onshore grid connection with the 2.9 GW
[Hornsea 3](/projects/hornsea-3.md) offshore wind farm. Under the agreement,
Nimbus will:

- forecast market and asset conditions;
- determine how battery capacity is allocated in real time;
- automate trading across power markets;
- support the technical, data and controls integration needed to operate the
  battery alongside the wind farm through their shared connection point.

Arenko describes Iceni as the world's first utility-scale battery directly
integrated into offshore transmission infrastructure, and says it has worked
with Ørsted over time as the developer's thinking on flexibility evolved.
