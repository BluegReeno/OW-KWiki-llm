# Overview

**Ocean Winds** is an offshore wind joint venture between EDP Renewables and ENGIE, developing and operating both fixed-bottom and floating offshore wind projects internationally. In France it is the developer of the 30 MW [Éoliennes Flottantes du Golfe du Lion (EFGL)](/projects/efgl-golfe-du-lion.md) floating wind farm, which reached full power in July 2026, and is associated with the [Dieppe-Le Tréport](/projects/dieppe-le-treport.md) fixed-bottom project. In Scotland it is developing the 2 GW [Caledonia](/projects/caledonia.md) offshore wind farm in the Moray Firth, which received offshore consent from the Scottish government in July 2026.

In Poland, Ocean Winds is building [BC-Wind](/projects/bc-wind.md), its first project in the country: 26 turbines for up to 390 MW some 23 km off the Polish Baltic coast, at financial close since the end of 2025 and due to deliver first power in 2028. In August 2026 it selected [Bilfinger](/companies/bilfinger.md) to provide technical advisory services during the execution phase, after Bilfinger had supported the onshore part of the works.
