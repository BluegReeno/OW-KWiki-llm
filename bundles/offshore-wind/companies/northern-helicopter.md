# Northern Helicopter

Northern Helicopter is a helicopter operator serving offshore installations, including offshore
wind farms, with search-and-rescue, medical evacuation (medevac) and crew transport flights.

# Offshore wind work

- **August 2026** — Named as the contractor for helicopter **medical evacuation services** at the
  [Baltica 2](/projects/baltica-2.md) offshore wind farm in the Polish Baltic Sea, awarded by
  [Ørsted](/companies/orsted.md) on behalf of its 50/50 joint venture with PGE Polska Grupa
  Energetyczna. Contract duration, value and the base of operations were not reported.
