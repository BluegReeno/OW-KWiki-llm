# Vard

Vard is a Norwegian shipbuilder of specialised offshore vessels, with
shipyards in Norway, Romania and Vietnam. It has become one of the main
suppliers of purpose-built offshore wind support tonnage.

## Offshore Wind Newbuilds

* **Windward Offshore CSOV series** - four hybrid Commissioning Service
  Operation Vessels for [Windward Offshore](/companies/windward-offshore.md).
  Windward Athens was delivered in October 2025, Windward Paris in early 2026,
  and Windward Munich in July 2026 from the Vung Tau yard in Vietnam;
  Windward Hamburg remains under construction.
* **NKT Eleonora** - cable-laying vessel under construction for
  [NKT](/companies/nkt.md), which left Romania for Norway in July 2026 as
  construction progressed.
