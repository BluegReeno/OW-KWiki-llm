# Saipem

Saipem is an Italian energy services group and one of the largest offshore
engineering and construction contractors worldwide. Alongside its oil and
gas business, it serves offshore wind through heavy marine installation,
foundation and subsea/cable work, competing with contractors such as
[DEME Offshore](/companies/deme-offshore.md),
[Van Oord](/companies/van-oord.md) and
[Boskalis](/companies/boskalis.md).

## Proposed merger with Subsea 7

A proposed merger between Saipem and [Subsea 7](/companies/subsea-7.md) is
facing deeper scrutiny in the European Union, with the European Commission
flagging competition concerns (reported 27 July 2026). The combination
would bring together two of the largest offshore engineering and
installation contractors serving both oil and gas and offshore wind.

The available excerpt does not state the review's procedural stage,
deadline, the specific markets of concern, or any remedies discussed.
