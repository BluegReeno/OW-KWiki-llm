# Role

F.lli Righini S.p.A. is an Italian engineering and manufacturing company specialised in
offshore equipment, active in both offshore wind and offshore oil & gas. It designs,
manufactures, assembles and tests its equipment in-house, treating each installation tool
as a bespoke prototype engineered to a specific project's tolerances and offshore
operational constraints, with mechanical machining and the main production phases kept
internal and some activities shared with qualified local partners. Its scope extends past
delivery to technical assistance during commissioning and offshore installation phases.

# Secondary Structure Installation Tools (SSIT)

SSITs handle the secondary steel fitted to
[monopile foundations](/technology/fixed-bottom-foundations.md) — boat landings and
internal platforms mounted on the foundation piles — with a separate dedicated tool
developed for external platform operations. The complete systems, including offshore
seafastening structures, are designed for safe, unmanned and remotely operated
installation in demanding marine conditions, the stated engineering challenge being to
handle different secondary steel components while holding strict installation tolerances
and positioning accuracy offshore.

# Projects

- [Baltica 2](/projects/baltica-2.md) — designed, tested and delivered SSITs for
  [Van Oord](/companies/van-oord.md)'s monopile campaign at the ~1.5 GW Polish project,
  announced 27 August 2026.
- **Germany, 2023** — delivered two Secondary Steel Installation Tools for monopiles of
  7 m and 7.5 m diameter, supplied to German offshore wind farm projects (not named).
- **January 2026** — awarded a contract by Heerema Marine Contractors for a high-capacity
  lifting system.
