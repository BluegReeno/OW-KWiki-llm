# Overview

CS Wind Offshore is the offshore structures arm of South Korean wind tower
manufacturer CS Wind, with fabrication operations in Aalborg, Denmark. It
produces steel offshore wind components, notably transition pieces (TPs)
that sit between a monopile foundation and the turbine tower — a segment of
the [fixed-bottom foundation](/technology/fixed-bottom-foundations.md)
supply chain also served by monopile makers such as
[EEW Group](/companies/eew-group.md).

# Key projects

- **[Nordlicht I](/projects/nordlicht.md) (Germany)**: supplier of the
  transition pieces for [Vattenfall](/companies/vattenfall.md)'s 980 MW
  wind farm, which needs 68 TPs, one per monopile. The first batch was
  shipped from Aalborg on 30 July 2026, roughly four weeks after
  [DEME Offshore](/companies/deme-offshore.md) installed the site's first
  monopile. DEME holds the transport and installation contract for both the
  monopiles and the TPs.
