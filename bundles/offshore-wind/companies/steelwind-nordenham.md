# Overview

Steelwind Nordenham is a German manufacturer of large-diameter monopile foundations, with a
fabrication plant and quayside in Nordenham on the Weser estuary. It belongs to the Dillinger/SHS
steel group, which supplies the heavy plate the monopiles are rolled from. It is one of the
European monopile fabricators serving the
[fixed-bottom foundation](/technology/fixed-bottom-foundations.md) supply chain, alongside
[EEW Group](/companies/eew-group.md), and works upstream of transition-piece makers such as
[CS Wind Offshore](/companies/cs-wind-offshore.md).

# Key projects

- **[Baltica 2](/projects/baltica-2.md) (Poland)**: monopile supplier for the PGE /
  [Ørsted](/companies/orsted.md) joint venture in the Polish Baltic Sea. On 5 August 2026,
  Steelwind reported completing the delivery of all monopiles under its contract for the project.
