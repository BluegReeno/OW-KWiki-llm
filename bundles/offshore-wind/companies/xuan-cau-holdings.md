# Xuan Cau Holdings

Xuan Cau Holdings is a Vietnamese renewable energy developer.

## Offshore wind

* **[Can Gio 2](/projects/can-gio-2.md)** (Vietnam) — joint venture partner
  with [Trung Nam](/companies/trung-nam.md) on the proposed 6 GW development
  10-18 km off the Can Gio district of Ho Chi Minh City. The city's Standing
  People's Committee reviewed the project's feasibility study on 20 August
  2026, and the project is being proposed for inclusion in Vietnam's revised
  [Power Development Plan VIII](/policy/vietnam-power-development-plan-8.md).
