# Hanwha Ocean

Hanwha Ocean is a South Korean shipbuilding and offshore engineering
company, part of the Hanwha Group. In the offshore wind sector it is the
developer of the 390 MW [Shinan Ui offshore wind project](/projects/shinan-ui.md)
off Shinan County (Jeollanam-do), where it started construction in July
2026.
