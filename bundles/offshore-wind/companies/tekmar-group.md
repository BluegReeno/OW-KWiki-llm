# Tekmar Group

Tekmar Group supplies subsea protection technology to the offshore wind sector.
The source item files the company under cable protection systems, the product
family it is best known for, and reports it extending that work into concrete
protection.

# Contracts

- **August 2026 — unnamed 'major' European offshore wind project**: awarded a
  contract valued at around EUR 1 million to supply concrete protection. The
  client, the project, its country and capacity, the scope volume, the product
  type and the delivery schedule were not disclosed in the source item.

# Context in this bundle

Concrete protection sits in the same layer of the supply chain as the seabed and
structure protection scopes already recorded here: scour protection at
[Dogger Bank](/projects/dogger-bank.md) and
[Hollandse Kust West VI](/projects/hollandse-kust-west-vi.md), and the subsea
corrosion protection contract [OEG](/companies/oeg.md) took at
