# GBM Works

GBM Works is active in offshore foundation installation technology, the
segment covered in this bundle under
[fixed-bottom foundations](/technology/fixed-bottom-foundations.md).

## Vibrojet Partnership (August 2026)

In August 2026 GBM Works and [CAPE Holland](/companies/cape-holland.md)
signed a **Memorandum of Understanding** to jointly commercialise the
**Vibrojet** for offshore wind.

nothing further: it does not describe the Vibrojet's working principle
