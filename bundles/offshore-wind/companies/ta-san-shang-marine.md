# Ta San Shang Marine (TSSM)

Ta San Shang Marine (TSSM) is an offshore wind vessel owner established
as a joint venture in which [Mitsui O.S.K. Lines](/companies/mitsui-osk-lines.md)
(MOL) is a partner. The reporting excerpt available to this bundle names
MOL as one side of the joint venture but is cut off before the other
partner or partners are identified, so no further ownership claim is made
here.

## New CSOV launched in Vietnam, August 2026

On 10 August 2026, offshoreWIND.biz reported that TSSM had launched a new
commissioning service operation vessel (CSOV) in Vietnam. The article is
filed under **Damen** and **Ha Long Shipyard**, which points to a
Damen-associated design built at the Vietnamese yard; the excerpt itself
does not state the design or the builder, and no vessel name, dimensions,
accommodation capacity, propulsion arrangement, delivery date, charter or
target market is asserted here.

CSOVs are the accommodation-and-workshop vessels used for turbine
commissioning and offshore [O&M](/technology/fixed-bottom-foundations.md)
campaigns; the bundle tracks the same segment through
[Windward Offshore](/companies/windward-offshore.md),
[Bernhard Schulte Offshore](/companies/bernhard-schulte-offshore.md),
[Pelagic Partners](/companies/pelagic-partners.md) and
[Marco Polo Marine](/companies/marco-polo-marine.md).
