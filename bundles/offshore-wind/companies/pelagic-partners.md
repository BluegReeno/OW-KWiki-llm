# Pelagic Partners

Pelagic Partners owns and operates vessels serving the offshore wind sector. It enters this
bundle as one of two vessel providers awarded Commissioning Service Operation Vessel (CSOV)
contracts by Elektrownia Wiatrowa Baltica – 2, the project company developing the 1.5 GW
[Baltica 2](/projects/baltica-2.md) offshore wind farm in the Polish Baltic Sea.

# Contracts

- **August 2026** — awarded a CSOV contract for [Baltica 2](/projects/baltica-2.md), together
  with [Bernhard Schulte Offshore](/companies/bernhard-schulte-offshore.md). The vessel names,
  charter durations and contract values were not reported.
