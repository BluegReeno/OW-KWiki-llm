# Overview

BW Ideol is a [floating offshore wind](/technology/floating-wind.md) foundation company, majority-owned by BW Offshore. It designs the ring-shaped "Damping Pool" floating foundation, a damping-pool concept intended for serial fabrication in concrete or steel to carry large offshore wind turbines in deep water beyond the reach of [fixed-bottom foundations](/technology/fixed-bottom-foundations.md).

# NGE Manufacturing Partnership

In July 2026, BW Ideol signed an **exclusive partnership agreement** with French construction group **NGE** covering a floating wind foundation manufacturing facility in **Southern France**. The tie-up pairs BW Ideol's floater design with NGE's civil-works and construction capability, aimed at building out local serial-fabrication capacity for floating foundations — a key supply-chain bottleneck for commercial-scale floating wind in France and the wider Mediterranean.

The offshoreWIND.biz excerpt is truncated and gives no site location, capacity, investment figure, timeline or target projects.

# Operating Track Record — Floatgen

BW Ideol's **2 MW [Floatgen](/projects/floatgen.md)** floating wind turbine had been in operation for **eight years** and had produced **40 GWh** of electricity as of **28 August 2026** — one of the longest continuous operating records for a floating wind unit in Europe, and the practical reference behind the company's serial-fabrication ambitions.
