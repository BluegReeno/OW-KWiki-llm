# Role

Marco Polo Marine is a Singapore-based marine logistics and shipyard group active in
the Asia-Pacific offshore wind market through its offshore wind subsidiary
**PKR Offshore (PKRO)**, which supplies and operates vessels — including commissioning
service operation vessels (CSOVs) — to turbine suppliers and developers. It is one of
the regional operators building out an Asia-Pacific CSOV fleet for turbine installation
support, commissioning and O&M campaigns, a market segment otherwise dominated by
European owners such as [Bernhard Schulte Offshore](/companies/bernhard-schulte-offshore.md).

# Contracts

- **Siemens Gamesa CSOV framework agreement (July 2026)** — PKR Offshore signed a
  framework agreement under which [Siemens Gamesa](/companies/siemens-gamesa.md) books
  two CSOVs for offshore wind work in Taiwan. As reported, the agreement covers two
  vessels for Taiwanese offshore wind campaigns; the available reporting gives no
  contract value, duration, vessel names or named wind farms.
