# Overview

ABB is a Swiss-Swedish electrification and automation group whose marine
division supplies power, propulsion and automation systems to shipyards
building offshore and specialised vessels — the shipboard electrical side of
the offshore wind supply chain, alongside the hull builders and vessel owners
the bundle otherwise tracks.

# Offshore wind vessel contracts

- **11 August 2026**: secured a contract with Chinese yard Guangzhou Shipyard
  International (GSI) to supply the power equipment for a new offshore
  converter station transport vessel being built for
  [Allseas](/companies/allseas.md). The vessel is intended to move offshore
  converter stations, the heaviest single structures in
  [HVDC grid connections](/technology/hvdc-grid-connection.md). The excerpt
  available is truncated mid-sentence, so the scope beyond "the power [...]"
  is not detailed here.
