# Semco Maritime

Semco Maritime is a Danish energy services group whose offshore wind
business covers construction support, service and maintenance work.

## Fleet divestment

In August 2026 Semco Maritime **sold its offshore wind jack-up vessels
Thor and Wind Lift 1 to [HEA Energy](/companies/hea-energy.md)**. The
excerpt available does not give the transaction value, the closing date,
the vessels' specifications, or any ongoing charter or contract attached
to them, and does not state Semco Maritime's reasons for the sale or what
it retains of its marine asset base.

## Corporate

In July 2026 the German offshore wind service company **Wind Multiplikator
GmbH** was renamed **Semco Maritime Offshore Wind Service**, bringing that
business under the Semco Maritime brand — see the
[rebrand digest](/digests/2026-07-23-wind-multiplikator-semco-maritime-rebrand.md).
