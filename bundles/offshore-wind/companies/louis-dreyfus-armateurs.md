# Overview

Louis Dreyfus Armateurs (LD Armateurs) is a French shipowning and marine
services group whose offshore wind activity centres on owning and operating
service operation vessels (SOVs) chartered to wind farm operators for
offshore construction support and O&M.

# Fleet and newbuilds

- LD Armateurs has a series of SOVs under construction at
  [ZPMC](/companies/zpmc.md) (Shanghai Zhenhua Heavy Industries) in China.
- **10 August 2026**: ZPMC laid the keel for the second vessel of the
  series. This SOV is chartered for the
  [Nordlicht offshore wind farms](/projects/nordlicht.md), the 1.6 GW German
  North Sea cluster developed by [Vattenfall](/companies/vattenfall.md).

The reporting available here does not give the vessel's dimensions, design,
accommodation capacity, propulsion, delivery date, charter length or charter
counterparty, nor the status of the first vessel in the series.
