# Overview

Neretek is a UK-based offshore wind foundation company launched on 8 July 2026 by global offshore wind engineering firm Wood Thilsted and COP Frontier, a sister company to offshore wind developer Copenhagen Offshore Partners (COP). The company was established to unlock cost-effective deepwater offshore wind. Industry veteran Jonathan Cole — former Chair of the Global Wind Energy Council (GWEC) — was appointed Executive Chair.

# Neretek BC90 technology

Neretek's proprietary Neretek BC90 is a next-generation foundation targeting the 60-90 m "transitional" water-depth band, aiming to bridge the gap between conventional [fixed-bottom foundations](/technology/fixed-bottom-foundations.md) (uneconomic beyond roughly 60-80 m) and [floating wind](/technology/floating-wind.md) (not yet commercialised at scale). It is said to deliver the simplicity of monopiles at these depths with significantly lower CAPEX than deepwater monopiles or jackets.

Key claims:
- Deployable using a combination of proven technologies, without reliance on large specialist installation vessels.
- Manufacturable from existing local supply chains and using existing port infrastructure.
- Product of roughly a decade of research by offshore structural designers.

# Market context

The launch came two days after the UK Government announced [AR8 (Allocation Round 8)](/tenders/uk-cfd-allocation-round-7.md) on 6 July 2026, which set out plans to exploit UK wind resources in 60-90 m depths under a new "Other Deepwater Offshore Wind" (ODOW) category. Neretek positions the BC90 as the answer to that challenge, and is also targeting deeper-water markets in Europe and Southeast Asia. UK Energy Minister Michael Shanks welcomed the launch.
