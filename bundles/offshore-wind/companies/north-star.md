# North Star

North Star is a UK offshore vessel owner and operator that has been building a
fleet of Service Operation Vessels (SOVs) for the offshore wind market
alongside its established offshore support activity.

## Financing

In August 2026 the company secured a **GBP 275 million (approximately
EUR 321 million) debt financing** package to support the expansion of its
offshore wind fleet. The reported item does not name the lenders, the facility
structure or tenor, nor the specific vessels the money will fund, so none is
recorded here.

North Star sits in the bundle's SOV/CSOV operator group alongside
[Windward Offshore](/companies/windward-offshore.md),
[Bernhard Schulte Offshore](/companies/bernhard-schulte-offshore.md) and
[Marco Polo Marine](/companies/marco-polo-marine.md). Its financing round is
part of the wider 2026 vessel-fleet build-out thread that also runs through
[Cadeler](/companies/cadeler.md), [Vard](/companies/vard.md) and
[Mitsui O.S.K. Lines](/companies/mitsui-osk-lines.md).
