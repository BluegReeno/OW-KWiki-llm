# HEA Energy

HEA Energy is a company active in the offshore wind vessel market.

## Fleet

In August 2026 HEA Energy **acquired the jack-up vessels Thor and Wind
Lift 1 from [Semco Maritime](/companies/semco-maritime.md)**. Both are
used in offshore wind work; jack-ups of this class typically serve
turbine and [foundation](/technology/fixed-bottom-foundations.md)
installation and maintenance campaigns.

The excerpt available does not give the purchase price, the closing date,
the vessels' specifications or build years, HEA Energy's nationality,
ownership or wider fleet, or the assignments the vessels will take on
under the new owner.
