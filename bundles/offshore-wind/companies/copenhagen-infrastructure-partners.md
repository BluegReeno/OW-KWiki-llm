# Copenhagen Infrastructure Partners (CIP)

Copenhagen Infrastructure Partners (CIP) is a Danish fund manager and offshore
wind developer/investor, backing projects across Europe, the US, and
Asia-Pacific. It is a partner in [Vineyard Wind 1](/projects/vineyard-wind-1.md)
in the US (with Avangrid).

## South Korea

In July 2026, South Korea's government selected five offshore wind projects
totalling roughly 1,786 MW in its offshore wind auction. CIP secured 1 GW of
this capacity across two of its projects, including one reported as Haesong.

## Taiwan

CIP develops the [Fengmiao 1](/projects/fengmiao-1.md) offshore wind farm in
Taiwan, whose offshore substation was built and loaded out by
[PTSC M&C](/companies/ptsc-mc.md) in July 2026.

## Philippines

CIP co-owns, with ACEN Corporation, the **901 MW San Miguel Bay** offshore
wind project, described as Southeast Asia's first offshore wind project. It
is one of four projects advancing through the Philippines'
[GEA-5 auction](/tenders/philippines-gea-5-offshore-wind-auction.md). At the
end of June 2026 CIP met the Philippine Department of Finance to discuss
project milestones, financing arrangements and auction preparations; the
auction was suspended on 4 July 2026 and is now indicatively targeted for
1 December 2026.
