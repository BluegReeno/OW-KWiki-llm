# OEG

OEG is an offshore energy services group supplying marine, subsea and offshore
operational services to offshore wind projects. It appears in this bundle both
as a service partner in emerging Asian markets and, from August 2026, as a
subsea contractor on a European project.

# Contracts and partnerships

- **August 2026 — [Gennaker](/projects/gennaker.md) (Germany, Baltic Sea,
  976.5 MW)**: secured a contract to provide a subsea corrosion protection
  system for the wind farm, developed by
  [Skyborn Renewables](/companies/skyborn.md). Contract value, scope size,
  system type and delivery schedule were not reported in the source item.
- **July 2026 — South Korea**: signed a tripartite memorandum of understanding
  with consultancy OWC and Korea Leading Energy Management (KLEM) to deliver
  balance-of-plant O&M services for Korean offshore wind farms, with OEG
  providing the marine, subsea and offshore operational scope. See the
  [digest](/digests/2026-07-16-owc-klem-oeg-korea-bop-om-pact.md).
