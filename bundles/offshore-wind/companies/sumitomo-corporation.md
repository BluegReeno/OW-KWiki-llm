# Sumitomo Corporation

Sumitomo Corporation is a Japanese trading and investment conglomerate that held minority equity stakes in three Belgian offshore wind farms. In 2026 it decided to divest all three as part of a portfolio rotation strategy, redirecting proceeds toward other growth areas under its Medium-Term Management Plan 2026 (announced 2024).

## Belgian Offshore Wind Divestments (2026)
- **[Nobelwind](/projects/nobelwind.md)** (165 MW): 39.02% stake sold to [JERA Nex BP](/companies/jera-nex-bp.md); completed July 2026.
- **[Northwester 2](/projects/northwester-2.md)** (219 MW): 30% stake sold to [JERA Nex BP](/companies/jera-nex-bp.md); completed July 2026.
- **[Northwind](/projects/northwind.md)** (216 MW): stake being sold to Publiwind, a consortium of Belgian investment firms and public institutions (Ethias, SOCOFE, SFPIM); expected to close during fiscal year 2026, subject to regulatory approval.

## Acquisitions (2026)
- **[Gwynt Glas](/projects/gwynt-glas.md)** (up to 1.5 GW, Celtic Sea, Wales): Sumitomo
  joined the floating wind project in August 2026, taking a stake alongside developers
  EDF Power Solutions and [ESB](/companies/esb.md), with DP Energy as exclusive
  development partner. The stake size and terms were not reported. It is the company's
  first [floating wind](/technology/floating-wind.md) position recorded in this bundle,
  and comes while it exits its operating Belgian fixed-bottom assets.
