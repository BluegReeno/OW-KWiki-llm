# JERA Nex BP

JERA Nex BP is an offshore wind joint venture between Japan's JERA and bp. In Belgium it develops, builds, and operates offshore wind farms from an operations hub in Ostend, including [Northwester 2](/projects/northwester-2.md) (now wholly owned) and [Nobelwind](/projects/nobelwind.md) (80.1% owned). It is also active in UK, Australian, and Taiwanese offshore wind development (e.g. Mona, Morven, Blue Mackerel).

## Projects
- **Japan**: Partner in the Oga Katagami Akita Offshore Green Energy consortium (with J-POWER, Tohoku Electric Power and Itochu) developing the 315 MW [Oga–Katagami–Akita](/projects/oga-katagami-akita.md) offshore wind farm off Akita Prefecture, which received a Bureau Veritas Japan certificate of conformity in July 2026.
- **Belgium**: [Northwester 2](/projects/northwester-2.md), [Nobelwind](/projects/nobelwind.md).
- **Norway**: Member, with Ingka Investments, of the [Ventyr](/companies/ventyr.md) consortium developing the up to 1.5 GW [Sørlige Nordsjø II](/projects/sorlige-nordsjo-ii.md) project, Norway's first commercial offshore wind farm, won in the country's first offshore wind auction in March 2024; the Ministry of Energy extended the licence application deadline by twelve months to 15 October 2027 in August 2026.

- **UK**: Mona (1.5 GW, full ownership since January 2026) and Morven (with EnBW). The joint venture with EnBW discontinued the up to 1.5 GW [Morgan](/projects/morgan.md) project in January 2026 and returned the lease to The Crown Estate, which is now [re-tendering the site](/tenders/crown-estate-morgan-site-retender-2026.md).

## Key Transactions
- **January 2026**: After EnBW decided to exit the Morgan and Mona projects in the UK, JERA Nex BP acquired EnBW's stake in 1.5 GW Mona; the JV discontinued [Morgan](/projects/morgan.md) and relinquished its Round 4 lease rights.
- **July 2026**: Completed acquisition of [Sumitomo Corporation](/companies/sumitomo-corporation.md)'s remaining stakes in Northwester 2 (30%) and Nobelwind (39.02%), taking full ownership of Northwester 2 and raising its Nobelwind stake to 80.1%. Sumitomo's stake in nearby Belgian wind farm [Northwind](/projects/northwind.md), where JERA Nex BP has no interest, was sold separately to a Belgian investor consortium.
