# Overview

**Cierco Energy** is a UK offshore wind developer working on [floating wind](/technology/floating-wind.md)
in the Celtic Sea. Its managing director is **Scott Harper**.

The company holds the two 100 MW [Llŷr](/projects/llyr.md) test and demonstration sites off
the Pembrokeshire coast of Wales, secured in The Crown Estate's 2021 leasing opportunity for
early commercial-scale Celtic Sea floating wind. The sites are developed through
**Floventis Energy**, Cierco's joint venture with **SBM Offshore**.

# Activity

- **2021** — selected for development of the two Llŷr sites in The Crown Estate's Celtic Sea floating wind leasing opportunity.
- **Early 2024** — Floventis Energy opened public consultation on Llŷr 1.
- **July 2025** — signed an MoU with **Marine Power Systems (MPS)** on potentially deploying MPS's PelaFlex technology at one of the Llŷr sites.
- **4 August 2026** — signed an **Agreement for Lease** with The Crown Estate covering both Llŷr projects.
- **7 August 2026** — the Welsh government granted **Section 36 consent** to the Llŷr Wind Farm, taking the demonstration programme through development consent in the same week it secured seabed rights.
