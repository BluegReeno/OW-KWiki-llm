# Overview

Maraen operates Maraen Port of Nigg, a deepwater integrated port for the
energy industry on the Cromarty Firth near Inverness, in the Scottish
Highlands, and a designated Green Freeport. Its CEO and Managing Director
is Yoshihiro Hayakawa. The port's deep-water quays, laydown capacity and
heavy ground loading capabilities are aimed at large-scale offshore wind
deployment and complex project logistics; since 2018 it has supported four
consecutive offshore wind farm construction campaigns, including SSE's
Beatrice and Seagreen wind farms.

# Dogger Bank marshalling role (2026)

Maraen Port of Nigg was announced in May 2026 as the marshalling harbour
for wind turbine components for the B and C phases of the 3.6 GW
[Dogger Bank Wind Farm](/projects/dogger-bank.md). Turbine components are
prepared and pre-assembled on site before being loaded onto vessels for
transport and installation offshore.

On 13 August 2026, following a visit by Scottish Government Energy Minister
Stephen Gethins, it was reported that up to 180 new jobs are being created
at the port to support the work — project managers, port operators, and
mechanical and electrical technicians involved in turbine pre-assembly of
[GE Vernova](/companies/ge-vernova-offshore-wind.md) Haliade-X 13 MW and
14 MW components. Recruitment has been underway for several months, and the
roles are expected to run through to the expected completion of Dogger Bank
in mid-2028. Maraen says it is committed to continued investment in the port.
