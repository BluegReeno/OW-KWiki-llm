# Taihan Cable & Solution

Taihan Cable & Solution is a South Korean power and cable manufacturer
building out subsea cable installation capability alongside its cable
manufacturing business, positioning itself in the offshore wind supply
chain much like compatriot [LS Cable & System](/companies/ls-cable-system.md).

# Skandi Connector

Taihan bought the **Skandi Connector** from Norway's DOF Group, announcing
the acquisition in May 2026 and taking delivery in early July 2026. It is
Taihan's second cable-laying vessel (CLV) and, per the reporting, the
second offshore wind-dedicated CLV introduced in Korea.

- Built 2016, designed by Dutch shipbuilder **Damen**
- 10,000-tonne class, 138.4 m long

A safe operation ceremony was held on **12 August 2026** at Orient Shipyard
in Gamcheon Port, Busan, ahead of full-scale deployment. The vessel is
scheduled to mobilise in August 2026 to the **Yeonggwang Nakwol** offshore
wind project in South Korea to carry out burial operations for submarine
cables — its first offshore wind assignment. Taihan frames the vessel as
strengthening its turnkey, end-to-end submarine cable offering.

Skandi Connector joins **PALOS**, described as Korea's first CLV dedicated
to offshore wind.

# Domestic ROV Trenchers (Panstar Robotics MoU)

On **19 August 2026** Taihan signed an MoU with
[Panstar Robotics](/companies/panstar-robotics.md), a company established by
the Korea Institute of Robotics & Technology Convergence (KIRO), to jointly
develop high-performance ROV trenchers, operating technologies and
installation solutions — building domestic alternatives in areas that
currently depend on foreign equipment and overseas specialists. The tools are
intended for offshore wind cable systems and for long-distance grid
interconnection and [HVDC](/technology/hvdc-grid-connection.md) submarine
cable projects, including the **West Coast Energy Highway**. The partners
will also run demonstration testing of cable burial and protection
technologies, pursue commercialisation, join national R&D programmes and
explore overseas markets together. Chun-won Lee, Executive Vice President,
framed it as strengthening the competitiveness of Korea's submarine cable
supply chain and national energy security.

# Manufacturing and Test Infrastructure

- **Submarine Cable Plant 2** under construction, capable of producing
  640 kV HVDC submarine cables, following completion of the first plant
- A dedicated **cable test centre** completed earlier in 2026, allowing
  simultaneous testing of two 640 kV-class HVDC cable circuits
