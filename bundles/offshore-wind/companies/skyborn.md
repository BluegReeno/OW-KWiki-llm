# Skyborn Renewables

Offshore wind developer, a portfolio company of Global Infrastructure Partners (GIP).

## Key Projects

- [Gennaker](/projects/gennaker.md) (Germany, Baltic Sea, 976.5 MW) — fabrication of
  the project's 63 transition pieces started at
  [Dajin Heavy Industry](/companies/dajin-heavy-industry.md) in August 2026.
- [Nordergründe Offshore Wind Farm](/projects/nordergrunde.md) (Germany) — became sole
  owner in June 2026 after acquiring the remaining 70% stake.
- [Fyrskeppet Offshore](/projects/fyrskeppet-offshore.md) (Sweden, southern Gulf of
  Bothnia) — permitted by the Swedish government on 24 July 2026 for up to 93
  turbines and an expected 8-11 TWh/year, to be completed by 2036; earlier plans
  described 187 turbines totalling ~2.8 GW. See
  [Swedish offshore wind permitting](/policy/swedish-offshore-wind-permitting.md).
- [Revolution Wind](/projects/revolution-wind.md) (US, 704 MW, more than 95% complete in
  August 2026) and South Fork Wind (US, 132 MW, operating) — joint
  ventures with [Ørsted](/companies/orsted.md), following Eversource's 2024 exit from
  US offshore wind.
