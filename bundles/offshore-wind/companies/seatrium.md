# Seatrium

Seatrium is a Singapore-headquartered offshore engineering, fabrication and
shipyard group, formed from the merger of Sembcorp Marine and Keppel Offshore &
Marine. Its renewables arm, **Seatrium Offshore Renewable Services (ORS)**,
serves the offshore wind sector alongside the group's oil-and-gas and
newbuilding work, placing it in the same heavy-structures segment as
[Aker Solutions](/companies/aker-solutions.md),
[Saipem](/companies/saipem.md) and
[Subsea 7](/companies/subsea-7.md).

## UK hub (2026)

In July 2026 Seatrium ORS started construction of a new offshore wind facility
in the UK. Per offshoreWIND.biz, the new hub is intended to support delivery of
the [TenneT](/technology/hvdc-grid-connection.md) 2 GW programme — the Dutch-German
transmission operator's standardised 2 GW / ±525 kV
[HVDC](/technology/hvdc-grid-connection.md) offshore grid connections — and of
the Sofia offshore wind farm, [RWE](/companies/rwe-offshore-wind.md)'s 1.4 GW
project on Dogger Bank in the UK North Sea.

The available excerpt is truncated ("has started construction of a new offshore
wind operations […]"), so the site's location, size, investment, workforce,
completion date and precise function (fabrication, assembly, marshalling or
operations base) are not recorded here.

## BalWin5 converter platform (Germany)

In December 2025 a **GE Vernova-Seatrium consortium** was awarded the contract for
TenneT's [BalWin5](/projects/balwin5.md) 2.2 GW-class HVDC converter platform in the
German North Sea, with Seatrium designing, building, transporting and installing the
offshore platform and GE Vernova's Electrification Systems supplying the HVDC
technology and both converter stations. On 31 July 2026 Seatrium's subcontract for the
platform's substructure was announced: [SK oceanplant](/companies/sk-oceanplant.md)
will deliver one ~15,000-tonne jacket and 20 piles under a contract worth about
USD 110.7 million (around EUR 96 million). BalWin5 is planned for commissioning in
2033.
