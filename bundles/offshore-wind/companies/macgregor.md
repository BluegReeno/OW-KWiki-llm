# MacGregor

MacGregor supplies marine cargo and load handling equipment. Its offshore
wind-facing products include the **Horizon** range of motion-compensated
walk-to-work gangways, used for personnel transfer from service vessels to
offshore structures, and subsea cranes for offshore wind construction
vessels.

## Floating-to-Floating Personnel Transfer (August 2026)

MacGregor received **DNV certification** for floating-to-floating (F2F)
personnel transfers using the Horizon gangway, following a sea acceptance
test. Reported details:

- For F2F operations the gangway uses a **purpose-designed flap** at its tip
  instead of the docking head used for conventional landings on fixed
  structures.
- **LiDAR-based relative-motion tracking** feeds the gangway's
  motion-compensation system, which adjusts to the movements of *both*
  vessels to hold the connection.
- The company positions the capability for [floating wind](/technology/floating-wind.md)
  service logistics — where, in the words of Prateek Sadhana, MacGregor
  Product Owner for Walk-to-Work Gangways, "there is no fixed structure to
  land on whatsoever" — as well as FPSOs, floating rigs and offshore service
  fleets, enabling crew changes and vessel-to-vessel access offshore without
  fixed landing points or port calls.
- The F2F function is offered as an **extension of the existing Horizon
  range**, not a separate product, and MacGregor says it can be retrofitted
  or added to Horizon gangways already in operation.

## Other Offshore Wind Scope

- Supplying a **subsea crane** for a South Korean floating wind construction
  vessel (February 2026).

Motion-compensated gangways of this class are standard equipment on the CSOV
and SOV fleets tracked here, including those of
[Windward Offshore](/companies/windward-offshore.md),
[Bernhard Schulte Offshore](/companies/bernhard-schulte-offshore.md) and
