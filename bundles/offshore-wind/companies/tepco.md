# TEPCO Renewable Power

Tokyo Electric Power Company Renewable Power (TEPCO RP) is the renewable energy
company of the TEPCO group, and an offshore wind developer in Japan.

## Offshore wind activity
- **Choshi, Chiba Prefecture**: bid for the area in
  [Japan's Round 1 fixed-bottom auction](/tenders/japan-round-1-fixed-bottom-auction.md)
  jointly with [Ørsted](/companies/orsted.md) in 2021 (a 370 MW proposal) and
  lost to the Mitsubishi Corporation-led consortium. After Mitsubishi's 2025
  exit, TEPCO RP filed the planning-stage environmental consideration document
  on **20 August 2026** for its own
  [Choshi offshore wind farm](/projects/choshi-offshore-wind.md) of up to
  **420 MW** (up to 27 turbines of 15-20 MW across roughly 4,000 hectares),
  with construction from 2031 or later.
