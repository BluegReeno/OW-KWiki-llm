# Prysmian

Prysmian is an Italian cabling company that manufactures and installs
high-voltage power cable systems, including the subsea export cables and
interconnectors that underpin [HVDC grid connections](/technology/hvdc-grid-connection.md)
for offshore wind. It sits alongside [NKT](/companies/nkt.md),
[LS Cable & System](/companies/ls-cable-system.md) and
[Taihan Cable & Solution](/companies/taihan-cable-solution.md) in the
integrated manufacture-and-install segment of the cable supply chain.

# Training and skills

In August 2026 Prysmian acquired a training facility for high-voltage
cable jointers in Scotland. Cable jointing — the skilled termination and
splicing of high-voltage cores — is a recurring bottleneck in offshore
wind and grid build-out, and buying a training site takes the company
into supplying the workforce as well as the cable. Per the available
excerpt, which is truncated after the opening clause, the facility's
name and location, the seller, the transaction value, the site's
capacity or throughput, and any linked projects are not stated.
