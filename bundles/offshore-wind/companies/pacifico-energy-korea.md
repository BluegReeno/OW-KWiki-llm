# Pacifico Energy Korea

Pacifico Energy Korea is a U.S.-based offshore wind developer active in
South Korea, where it leads the 3.2 GW
[Jindo Offshore Wind Project Cluster](/projects/jindo.md) off Jindo County
in the Jeonnam-Gwangju (Jeollanam-do) region, taking responsibility for
overall investment and project management.

On 20 August 2026 the company said it had been selected as the **sole
developer of the 2.13 GW Jindo Offshore Wind Integrated Cluster Phase 2**,
comprising its Manho (990 MW) and Jindo Baram (1.8 GW) projects — the second
and third phases of the Jindo cluster. The selection followed an evaluation
of developer suitability by a committee set up by Jeonnam-Gwangju Special
Metropolitan City in June 2026, and the signing of an Implementation
Management Agreement with the regional government and Jindo County. Its
first-phase 420 MW Myeong Ryang project secured an Electricity Business
Licence in September 2025; licence applications for Manho and Jindo Baram
remain under review.

Its stated strategy prioritises the local offshore wind supply chain. In
July 2026 it signed a memorandum of understanding with three Gwangyang Bay
Area suppliers — [EEW KHPC](/companies/eew-group.md) (foundations),
KMC Line (marine logistics and vessels) and CS Energy (foundation
components) — to build a tailored local supply chain for the Jindo cluster
and to help establish Jeonnam-Gwangju as an Asia-Pacific offshore wind
supply chain hub.
