# Technology

* [Floating Offshore Wind](floating-wind.md) - Turbines on buoyant moored platforms, unlocking deep-water sites beyond fixed-bottom reach.
* [Fixed-Bottom Foundations](fixed-bottom-foundations.md) - Monopile, jacket, and gravity-based structures supporting most installed offshore wind capacity, and the methods and equipment used to drive them into the seabed.
* [HVDC Grid Connection](hvdc-grid-connection.md) - High-voltage DC transmission via offshore converter platforms for large or distant wind farms, including TenneT's 2 GW-class German and Dutch platforms.
* [Power-to-X (Offshore Green Hydrogen)](power-to-x.md) - Converting offshore wind electricity directly into hydrogen or other energy carriers at sea; includes the PosHYdon platform pilot.
* [Offshore Energy Storage](offshore-energy-storage.md) - Storing offshore wind electricity at sea: subsea and floating pumped-hydro-style systems such as Sizable Energy's Calabria demonstrator, compressed air, and marine batteries; plus onshore batteries co-located under a wind farm's own transmission connection, as at Hornsea 3's Iceni BESS.
* [Offshore Substations](offshore-substations.md) - Platforms collecting and stepping up turbine power before export to shore, from modular transformer topsides to lightweight integrated units such as Siemens Energy's HV-TSU.
* [Subsea Cable Installation and Burial](subsea-cable-installation.md) - Laying and burying array and export cables — cable-laying vessels, ROV trenchers, jetting versus cutting, and the localisation of trenching spreads in markets such as South Korea.* [Turbine Scaling](turbine-scaling.md) - Growth in turbine capacity, rotor diameter, and hub height, and its economic impact.
