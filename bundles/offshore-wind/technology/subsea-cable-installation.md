# Subsea Cable Installation and Burial

Offshore wind farms depend on two cable layers: inter-array cables linking
turbines to an [offshore substation](/technology/offshore-substations.md),
and export cables carrying power to shore, increasingly as
[HVDC links](/technology/hvdc-grid-connection.md). Both are laid from
cable-laying vessels and then buried below the seabed to protect them from
anchors, fishing gear and seabed movement — the main cause of costly
offshore outages when it fails.

# Trenching

Burial is performed by subsea trenchers, tracked or skidded tools lowered
from a support vessel through a launch and recovery system (LARS). Two
working principles dominate, and powerful tools carry both so a single
spread can cross varied soils:

- **Jetting** — high-pressure water fluidises soft sediment so the cable
  settles into the trench; suited to sands and soft clays.
- **Cutting** — mechanical chain or wheel cutters excavate stiff clay and
  rock where jetting has no effect.

Available power sets what a trencher can cut and how deep it can lower the
cable. [NKT](/companies/nkt.md)'s **T-3600**, described as the world's most
powerful subsea trencher, delivers 3,600 horsepower and is designed to bury
cable up to 5.5 metres below the seabed with both jetting and cutting
functions. It is designed and built by [Osbit](/companies/osbit.md) and
will be operated by
[Helix Robotics Solutions](/companies/helix-robotics-solutions.md).
[Van Oord](/companies/van-oord.md) operates its own trencher, **Dig-It**,
alongside its cable-laying vessel Nexus.

Trenchers are commonly built as remotely operated vehicles (ROVs), and both
the equipment and the specialists who operate it are concentrated in a small
number of mostly European suppliers — which makes trenching spreads a
localisation target for markets building their own cable supply chains. In
South Korea, [Taihan Cable & Solution](/companies/taihan-cable-solution.md)
and [Panstar Robotics](/companies/panstar-robotics.md) agreed in August 2026
to co-develop domestic high-performance ROV trenchers and their operating
systems, for offshore wind array and export cables as well as long-distance
[HVDC](/technology/hvdc-grid-connection.md) interconnections, together with
demonstration testing of burial and protection technologies.
